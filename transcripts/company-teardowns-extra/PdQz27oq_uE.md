The first psychological phenomena, Von Restorff effect or isolation effect, states that when multiple similar objects are present, the one that 

[06:17] differs from the rest is most likely to be remembered. Think back to the childhood games you used to play. Remember those spot the difference puzzles? The puzzles were made up of images. But cow number three doesn't have any spots. The Von Restorff effect tells us that the cow without spots, the unique cow, will be the one we remember. 

[06:49] In UX design, this is why the call to action buttons look different from the rest of the buttons on a site or app because we want them to stand out. The second psychological phenomena, serial position effect, says that when people are given a list of items, they are more likely to remember the first
