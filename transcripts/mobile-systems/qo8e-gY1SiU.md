A tab bar is a row of three to five buttons, pinned to the bottom of the screen. Each one has an icon and a label. And it's there on every page, so shoppers can jump anywhere with one tap. 

[00:30] Why the bottom? Researcher Steven Hoober watched people using their phones, more than thirteen hundred times. About half held the phone in one hand. With one hand, the bottom of the screen is easy for your thumb. The top corner, where most menus live, is a stretch. The second reason: people use what they can see. Nielsen Norman Group tested hidden menus against visible navigation. When the menu was hidden behind an icon, people used it less, and took longer to finish their tasks.
