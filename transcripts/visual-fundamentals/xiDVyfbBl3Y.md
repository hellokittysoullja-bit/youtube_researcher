Web typography is the set of rules for formatting site text to convey information to the audience in the easiest form to read and perceive. Text formatting affects the amount of time a user spends on your site. The completion of a target action and the overall impression of the site. Our task is to ensure that the text on the 

[00:30] page is actually read. As the internet has developed and the volume of information has grown, users have gradually learned to filter information . You need to make it stand out. Text is the main element on a page. It is what pushes the user to take action. And to create clear, readable text that is easy to navigate, you need to turn to the rules of typography. And remember, no matter how useful the information in a text block is, if the text is formatted incorrectly, it will 

[01:00] go unnoticed. To begin with, let's look at the basic concepts so it is easier to absorb the subsequent rules. Fonts and typefaces. A font is a set of small and large characters, punctuation marks , numbers, and special symbols of a single size and weight for a specific typeface. A typeface is a collection of fonts that share common stylistic features and principles of character design. For example, Roboto has Regular , Italic, Bold, and Bold Italic styles —that is a font. At the same time, 

[01:30] Roboto is a typeface. There are different types of typefaces: sans-serifs, serifs, slab serifs, scripts, display, and symbol fonts. Sans-serifs are easily read on devices due to the lack of contrast between strokes, the absence of serifs, and the increased height of lowercase letters. They are used in all text blocks. Serifs are a classic type. The contrast between main and connecting strokes is increased. Wide, rounded, or rectangular serifs appear. They are 

[02:01] used in headings and text blocks styled like printed publications or a specific historical period. Slab serifs have more massive rectangular serifs. The contrast between main and connecting strokes is reduced. They are used in headings. Sometimes they can be used in printed publications. Typefaces that imitate human handwriting, built on the principles of proper calligraphy, are used to increase the expressiveness of a heading. Display. This group combines typefaces that did not fit into other categories. They give great 

[02:33] expressiveness to the text and attract people's attention. They are used to increase the expressiveness of a heading. Symbol fonts consist of signs that do not contain letters of the alphabet. They can take the form of pictograms or abstract elements. The best option for web pages is to use sans-serif typefaces. They are simpler for visual perception, clear, and understandable. Most often, sans-serifs and serifs are combined. In situations where a different character width is required, you should not stretch or compress the font manually. There are 

[03:04] specially designed font variants. Normal or proportional means the character width in the font set varies depending on the proportions. Narrower, more compressed, and compact, with a smaller character width and narrower spacing between characters. Wide fonts, expanded with a greater character width proportional to their height, which makes the characters almost square. This effect is immediately noticeable in headings. Monospaced. All characters have the same width. Designed for 

[03:34] typewriters, but now used in systems with a fixed set of characters. Type size. Type size determines the vertical plane where characters are placed, from the bottom of a 'p' to the top of a 'b' . And every time we say we need to increase or decrease the font size, to put it mildly, it’s incorrect. We are changing the type size. In this image, we see the designation of the baseline, the descender, and the ascender. Also the presence of a serif, and, actually, 

[04:05] the type size itself. By the way, the latest Figma update allows us to align text to baselines. For those who missed the video about the latest Figma update , you can watch it on my channel. You will find the link to the video in the description. In print publications, font sizes are measured in points, and on screen, in pixels. The size ratio is calculated by the formula: 12 points equals pixels. I don’t think you’ll be doing such calculations every time, but it’s good to know for general 

[04:37] information. The optimal size for websites is 16-20 pixels for computers and 14-18 for phones. For printed materials, 10-12 points. Weight or thickness? Weight or thickness is determined by changing the thickness of the main and connecting strokes. Within a single typeface, weight can vary from the thinnest to the thickest and is described by words like thin or black, or numerical values from 100 to 900. For paragraphs, weights from 100 to 400 

[05:09] are most often used. For headings, weights from 500 to 900 are used. Contrast. Contrast is the ratio of the thickness of the main and connecting strokes. Contrast can range from non-contrasting fonts with a one-to-one ratio to high-contrast fonts with a ratio of more than three. In the image, we see where the main and connecting strokes are located. Low-contrast fonts are easy to read on monitors, perceived 

[05:40] more easily by the eye, and do not cause discomfort during long reading. Texts with low contrast, a two-to-one ratio, are harder to read on monitors, but are still used on the web. They can often create a beautiful accent when combined with non-contrasting fonts. High contrast, with a ratio of more than three, causes discomfort during long reading, so it is used only for headings and as accent elements on a page. Tracking and kerning. Tracking is 

[06:11] the uniform change of spacing between characters in an entire text block. In words set in a large size, the distance between letters should be decreased. This makes the words in a line become cohesive. Tracking in text with small sizes should be increased to facilitate visual perception of the text. Text with positive tracking does not have clear spaces between two words. The text falls apart into pieces and is difficult for the eye to perceive. Text with normal tracking has a clear visual separation into individual words. Such 

[06:43] text forms a solid block and is easily perceived. Text with negative tracking has insufficient letter spacing, so while the information is legible, it causes visual discomfort for the reader . Kerning is the adjustment of spacing between a specific pair of characters. Modern fonts already have built-in kerning, so there is no need to edit the kerning of individual letters. So, tracking is the space between all letters, while kerning is the space between adjacent letters. X-height. The 

[07:16] height from the baseline, which is determined by the letter X. Web text uses a large x-height to improve the legibility of small font sizes. In this image, we see a large x-height, which is better to use for websites. Here we see a smaller x-height, which is more suitable for print. Leading and line spacing. Leading is the distance between the baselines of adjacent lines of text. Line spacing is the distance 

[07:47] from the baseline to the top of the lowercase letters on the next line. For website text, paragraph leading should be set between 120%and 170%. Usually, leading is adjusted by the designer by eye, but the recommended leading is up to 150%. In this case, the lines do not fall apart, but are well separated, and the text remains readable. Negative leading is often used in headlines. The distance between lines is less than the height of the letters. In this case, 

[08:18] the headline becomes a cohesive block and does not fall apart. Headlines are kept short so as not to force the reader's eyes to scan across the screen .
