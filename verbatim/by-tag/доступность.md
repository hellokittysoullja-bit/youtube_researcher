### Интерлиньяж зависит от длины строки: длинная строка требует большего  ·  [10:52](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=652)
**Мысль:** Если читатель теряет начало следующей строки, дизайнер совершил серьёзную ошибку. Длинные строки получают больший интервал, короткие можно сжимать.

> There is a small rule for this. Line height depends on line length. Let's repeat it . Line height depends on line length. The shorter your text, the smaller the line height. The longer your line, the larger the line height. I think you've encountered a moment when you were reading a book and reached the end of a line. And after that, you were very careful, afraid of losing the thread of the narrative, carefully returning to the beginning of the line and jumping to the next one, or tracing it with your finger or using a ruler. So, if your user has to perform such manipulations, then as a designer, you have made a very big mistake that must not be made. Therefore, if the line length is very long, we increase the line spacing so that it is very easy for us to jump between lines. If the line is very short, we can compress our text to increase reading speed.

**Для Напарника:** Ограничить ширину читаемого текста (max-w) и подбирать интерлиньяж под неё, особенно на лендинге.
