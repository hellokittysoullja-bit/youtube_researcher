> Many live service games have daily login rewards. In behavioural psychology, when an action is followed by a reward, your brain learns to repeat it. Every day that you log in and receive this reward, you get a hit of dopamine, building a habit over time. Players who are conditioned to logging in every day do it not because they’re excited to play, but because it feels like brushing their teeth: just something they have to do. Some games have login streaks. This is where the rewards escalate for each sequential day you log in. When you break the streak, your rewards reset back down to the minimum level. Now, humans have a strong sense of loss aversion, where losses feel roughly twice as bad as gains feel good, and hence players will go significantly out of their way to avoid breaking these streaks. As I mentioned before, I have a friend who was on an off-the-grid camping trip and had to walk six kilometres with his laptop and a 3G modem so that he could keep a login streak going in some random game he was playing. There are even games out there that let you pay real money to restore a broken streak.

**Для Напарника:** Не применимо: стрики со штрафом сброса противоречат тону без стыда; вместо этого накопительный остров, который не откатывается.
