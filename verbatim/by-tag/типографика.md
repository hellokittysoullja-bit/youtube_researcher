### Вес шрифта: bold при слабом контрасте, thin для лёгкости; тело Regular, заголовки Bold  ·  [05:03](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=303)
**Мысль:** Тонкий текст выглядит серым и почти невидимым, жирный яркий и заметный, хотя цвет тот же. Вес управляет вниманием.

> Why do symbols even need different weights at all? It is needed for cases where, for example, you have insufficient contrast with your background. If you have insufficient contrast, you use Bold to make your characters slightly wider. If you have good contrast and want to give your font a different mood, you take and use Thin, because Thin, as you can see, is very slender, very light, low-contrast, and radiates a kind of airiness and calm. A certain lightness. Bold, on the contrary, is monumental, heavy, and complex. So, as you can see, the weight allows you to work with the visual hierarchy of your text, as well as convey additional moods, like the ones I just used. Which weight should you use for body text? The answer is simple . It is Regular. And then, depending on your situation, you can deviate, for example, by using Light or Medium. Of course, I do not recommend using Bold for body text. Bold, Extra Bold, Black, and Medium are mainly used for headings . Regular and Light are used for body text. There are also other weights, such as Regular Italic, Thin Italic, and Medium Italic. The suffix "Italic" means that this weight is available in a slanted style. As you can see, it is all very simple, but pay attention, be sure to pay attention, that the difference between Thin and Bold is huge. Even though I used the same color everywhere and did not use any transparency settings. I wrote everything the same way, I just changed the weight. But look, it seems like the Thin text is already gray and almost invisible. Bold, it is very bright, white, and very noticeable. With the help of this principle, the principle of visual hierarchy, you can control the attention of your users.

**Для Напарника:** Для основного текста реплик использовать Regular/Light, а заголовки и ключевые числа Medium или Bold; не использовать Thin на тёмном фоне.

But keep in mind that 16 pixels is for mobile devices, a minimum of 18 pixels for desktop devices,.

**Для Напарника:** Основной текст 16px и выше на Доме и Фокусе; рассмотреть опцию увеличенного шрифта.


### Не используй auto для интерлиньяжа: 150% минимум, лучше 170–180%  ·  [08:50](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=530)
**Мысль:** Большой интерлиньяж облегчает сканирование и запоминание, а auto слишком мал.

> By default, Figma and Photoshop set it to auto, and then our text turns into this little mess that isn't particularly pleasant to read. To make it more pleasant to read, we increase the line spacing. As you can see, our text is starting to become much clearer. It is easy for us to read and easy for us to perceive. But honestly, I don't recommend using 150%; or rather, I recommend using that only as a minimum value, because it is better, of course, to use 170-180%, because at that line height value our text becomes more spaced out, making it easier for us to read, to scan specific sentences or read specific words, pick them out and remember them, which means it is easier to interact with the text. Therefore, I recommend that you use 170-180%, with 150%as a minimum. And never use auto, because it is too small a value.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики напарника и длинные тексты лендинга задавать с line-height около 1.6–1.7.


### Интерлиньяж зависит от длины строки: длинная строка требует большего  ·  [10:52](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=652)
**Мысль:** Если читатель теряет начало следующей строки, дизайнер совершил серьёзную ошибку. Длинные строки получают больший интервал, короткие можно сжимать.

> There is a small rule for this. Line height depends on line length. Let's repeat it . Line height depends on line length. The shorter your text, the smaller the line height. The longer your line, the larger the line height. I think you've encountered a moment when you were reading a book and reached the end of a line. And after that, you were very careful, afraid of losing the thread of the narrative, carefully returning to the beginning of the line and jumping to the next one, or tracing it with your finger or using a ruler. So, if your user has to perform such manipulations, then as a designer, you have made a very big mistake that must not be made. Therefore, if the line length is very long, we increase the line spacing so that it is very easy for us to jump between lines. If the line is very short, we can compress our text to increase reading speed.

**Для Напарника:** Ограничить ширину читаемого текста (max-w) и подбирать интерлиньяж под неё, особенно на лендинге.


### Трекинг: широкий только для заголовков, в основном тексте ±5% (максимум ±8%)  ·  [12:19](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=739)
**Мысль:** Раньше бумага стоила дорого, поэтому широкие надписи воспринимаются как более важные и дорогие. В тексте это мешает чтению.

> By increasing it, we give it more importance because our text takes up more space. And as I told you earlier, or rather, as I just told you, people used to have to save space on paper to convey as much information as possible. And that means if a word or phrase takes up more space than it should, it means they are very important, because it was simply expensive to do that before. Therefore, such wide inscriptions seem more expensive and more important to us. Of course, I recommend using this technique only with headlines, perhaps with subheadings, for body text that the user will be reading. It is better not to do this trick, because if you space the letters out that much, reading will become very uncomfortable. There is a practical recommendation. This recommendation is to use plus or minus 5%for body text, or a maximum of plus or minus 8%. I think that is the interval you can afford. No more. Some people extend this interval up to 10%, but it all depends on your font. I, of course, recommend up to plus or minus five. And in the most difficult cases, plus or minus 8%. And set larger distances between letters only in headlines to give them more significance or importance.

**Для Напарника:** Разрядку применять только к маленьким заглавным лейблам и заголовкам, а основной текст оставить с нулевым трекингом.
