## 1. СДВГ не изобретает новых проблем, а усиливает универсальные
`СДВГ · стыд · исследования` · [01:19](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=79)

**Мысль:** Отвлекаться, забывать, откладывать свойственно всем; разница — частота, влияние и сила эмоций, а не вид.

**Слова автора:**

> So, I have a saying that ADHD doesn't invent new problems, it just exacerbates the universal ones. We all get distracted sometimes. We all forget things, we all procrastinate, right? This is not a unique experience only to people with ADHD. The big difference is folks with ADHD have these struggles more often. It might have a bigger impact on their life, it might be more visible to others. They might have more emotional, stronger feelings about it. That's really the big difference. It's not a difference in kind, it's a difference in degree.

**Для Напарника:** Тон Напарника нормализующий: «с каждым так бывает»; без патологизации.

## 2. Слепота ко времени: постоянная, а не от тревоги или недосыпа
`СДВГ · время · исследования` · [02:14](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=134)

**Мысль:** Время ощущается скользким, поэтому сложнее планировать и координировать задачи.

**Слова автора:**

> Folks with ADHD have a harder time sort of seeing time, right? It's harder to sort of see how long things are going to take, and just generally to sort of manage tasks in relation to time. Obviously, lots of other things can affect your ability to sort of see and be aware of time, so anxiety or depression, sleep deprivation, having a cold. But in ADHD, it's a much more sort of consistent, pervasive part of your life experience. Time just feels much more slippery to you perhaps, and therefore, planning is harder, coordinating tasks across time is harder. It impacts your ability to get stuff done in lots of different ways.

**Для Напарника:** Показывать длительность шага визуально; предлагать оценку времени, а не требовать её от человека.

## 3. СДВГ-налог: штрафы, забытые зарядки и другие скрытые траты
`СДВГ · стыд · исследования` · [03:04](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=184)

**Мысль:** Жизнь с СДВГ дороже из-за пропущенных платежей и потерянных вещей; важно понимать эту цену.

**Слова автора:**

> Okay, so obviously there's not an actual tax on ADHD, but effectively there is. It's all those ways that having ADHD makes your life more expensive. So things like the late fees you pay because you forget to pay a bill on time, or having to buy a charger for your phone because you can't find the one that you have, or you don't have it with you when you need it. So when you add all of this up, it's just yet another way that ADHD impacts your life.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник может мягко помогать с мелкими бытовыми «налогами» (напоминания-шаги), не стыдя.

## 4. Маскинг: польза на работе, но высокая цена энергией
`СДВГ · стыд · этика` · [03:59](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=239)

**Мысль:** Попытка выглядеть «нормальным» даёт социальную выгоду, но требует огромных усилий, например доработки по вечерам.

**Слова автора:**

> Masking is to put on the appearance of being neurotypical, to look like you don't have ADHD, or as some people might even put it "to look normal," as if that's like one thing. There is a benefit to it, right? It benefits you at work, if your coworkers see you as someone who is kind of predictable and reliable and consistent. It might benefit you with friends, in romantic relationships. The problem is it can come at a really high cost. It takes a lot of energy. So for example, if you work two or three extra hours at home at the end of a workday to finish the project you didn't finish during the workday, it's helpful in terms of your relationship with your boss, but it comes at a really high cost to yourself. This is just yet another way that ADHD can carry a cost to those who have it.

**Для Напарника:** В Напарнике человеку не нужно маскироваться: тон разрешает быть как есть, без отчётности.

## 5. «Высокофункциональность» — это совпадение требований, способностей и поддержки
`СДВГ · доступность · исследования` · [06:36](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=396)

**Мысль:** Результат зависит не только от человека, но и от опор вокруг и давления среды; сильная среда повышает работоспособность.

**Слова автора:**

> How you perform is really a function of what are all of your abilities and strengths that you have from within you? What are the supports in the world around you? And what are the demands being placed upon you? The better the match is between the demands and your abilities and supports, the higher your functioning is going to be. So one of the implications of this is if you come from an area that has a good educational system and you have a healthcare system that is well tuned into ADHD, if you have a family who's also tuned in and is willing to spend resources, then you're going to function at a much higher level. If you have, I don't know, a position as a CEO, and you have a number of assistants, again, you're going to function very well. It's really about the fit between demands and abilities that makes the difference in terms of what your level of functioning is.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник — внешняя опора: он снижает требования шага и добавляет поддержку, а не повышает планку.

## 6. Body doubling: человек рядом не помогает, а просто напоминает держаться курса
`body-doubling · СДВГ · внимание` · [07:40](https://youtu.be/YL5N1Yx5994?t=460)

**Мысль:** Присутствие другого, занятого своим, служит ненавязчивым напоминанием; в конце короткий чек-ин «успел?».

**Слова автора:**

> But actually body doubling is an old ADHD coping strategy. As you work on your task, there's somebody in the same room who's over there working on whatever they're doing. They're not helping you, they're not specifically keeping you on track, you're not collaborating on a project together, but just there's something about having another person there that just serves as a reminder, I think, for all of us, to sort of stay on track. We are just a bit more focused and productive when there's somebody else there. Some people have even started doing this kind of virtually, where, you know, you are on your computer working on your stuff, somebody else is on the other side of their webcam working on their stuff, and then at the end of a certain amount of time, you sort of check in and say like, "Hey, so did you get done what it is that you need to do?" So body doubling is a great way to stay accountable and maybe just get a little bit more done.

**Для Напарника:** Кот сидит рядом без вмешательства; в финале сессии тёплый чек-ин вместо отчёта.
