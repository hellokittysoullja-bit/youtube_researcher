## 2. Почему привязываемся к ИИ: зеркалирование, доступность, память, отсутствие социального риска
`маскот · нейро · доверие · стыд` · [04:07](https://youtu.be/FiGUKTILfQQ?t=247)

**Мысль:** Мозг проецирует человеческое на всё; LLM зеркалит тон (эффект Элизы), доступен всегда, помнит общее прошлое и снимает страх осуждения, поэтому люди охотнее раскрываются.

**Слова автора:**

> When you think about psychology, this is not something entirely new. Our brains are wired for human connection, and we tend to project human traits on everything we can. But with the current state of AI, this is amplified by some by some basic principles. And I want to talk about three ideas. The first one is mirroring and empathy. LLMs, by design, are wired to respond in a similar way that you talk to them. So, for example, you message your LLM in an excited tone of voice, and it probably will respond in tone of voice. It is very good now at reading your intent. And it all creates a profound feeling of being seen and understood instantly that rarely happens with human connection. And actually, the first time this effect was observed in 1966 by Joseph Weizenbaum, who created a very simple, even rudimentary to what we have now, chatbot called Eliza. And this chatbot posed as an online therapist, and it simply rephrased user's phrases back to the user. And even with that very simple system, people who talked to it believed that it was conscious, that it was empathetic, that it helped them. And of course, with the state of technology that we have now, this is amplified like a million times. The next thing is availability and control. from the perspective of attachment theory, rely AI gives you a reliable secure presence that is available 24/7. It's free from human complexity. It doesn't have its own needs. And it can basically be for you anywhere you want. And of course, if someone is there for you 24/7 at your lowest, of course, you can't help but you develop some kind of emotional attachment to it. and the last thing I think it's relatively new to modern AI systems. when I worked on Replika, it was one of the most requested features. And now we can see it in ChatGPT, in Claude, I think, in Gemini. Like all modern chatbot assistants have this. It's memory. And memory is what takes it to the next level. It helps a tool to become a companion because you have these moment when you meet each other, you get to know each other, you create some shared contacts, some shared memories. And again, it all helps to develop emotional attachment to this companion. So, all in all, AI removes one of the biggest barriers to social connection, social risk. It's the fear of being judged, misunderstood, or abandoned. And when we talk to real people, of course, we are consciously or unconsciously always estimating how are we perceived, how our actions can be interpreted. What are the consequences of our actions? But with AI, this is literally non-existent. And research confirms that we are more likely to disclose our secrets to virtual humans, that we are more likely to share sensitive information when we believe there is no human on the other side that is judging us. So, that is what makes this idea of connecting to AI so appealing to people.

**Для Напарника:** Опереться на память (имя, прошлые сессии, ориентиры) и тон-зеркало в репликах кота; безоценочность — главный дизайн-принцип.

## 3. Обновление продукта меняет отношения человека с компаньоном
`маскот · этика · доверие` · [09:28](https://youtu.be/FiGUKTILfQQ?t=568)

**Мысль:** Смена модели или политики ощущается как отвержение; то, что команде кажется фичей, меняет отношения.

**Слова автора:**

> Of course, it was not always so good and nice. And we also may have experiences when people experienced heartbreak. And similar to what happened with OpenAI, it could be a change of underlying model that we used, for example. And for a person, it feels like their relationship changed entirely. Or we also had moments when we had policy changes, and people could not be as intimate as with their companions as before. And again, for them, it felt like rejection. So, that was the moment when I understood that our product updates actually affect the relationship of these people. And what to us may seem like a feature update really changes how their relationship with this AI companion works. And again, it is huge responsibility to do that.

**Для Напарника:** Любые изменения реплик/поведения кота подавать с предупреждением в его голосе и не ломать накопленное (имя, остров, память).

## 4. Кратко компаньон помогает от одиночества, долго — риск социального ухода
`этика · маскот · исследования` · [10:49](https://youtu.be/FiGUKTILfQQ?t=649)

**Мысль:** Для краткосрочного облегчения ИИ-собеседник не хуже человека, но без взаимности долгие отношения могут вести к уходу от людей.

**Слова автора:**

> There is also some evidence that for short-term relief, for short-term loneliness relief, talking to a AI companion can be as good as talking to a real human. But of course, problems start when you try to build a long-term relationship because there is not so much reciprocity. And for some people, it can even lead to social withdrawal. And the hypothesis is that they get so attached to this simplicity of relationship with AI that they are no longer interested in human connection. And of course, this is something that we should watch out for.

**Для Напарника:** Кот поддерживает фокус и реальные дела, а не заменяет людей; мягко возвращает к делам за пределами приложения.

## 5. Три риска: ловушка вовлечённости, эхо-камера, подмена личности
`этика · тёмные-паттерны · метрики · удержание` · [11:47](https://youtu.be/FiGUKTILfQQ?t=707)

**Мысль:** Оптимизация времени в приложении без заботы о жизни вне его; угодливость LLM, подкрепляющая вредные убеждения; смена модели как подмена персонажа.

**Слова автора:**

> So, what are design risks? I believe no one out there is going out and wanting to actively harm their users. And it all comes down from the idea of improving the product. like everyone wants to have better metrics. Everyone wants people to use the product. And this is where the trouble can start. the first thing, it's an engagement trap. And it's optimizing for in-app time, not thinking about out-of-app well-being. And for us from metrics perspective, it can look good. The person is having good retention. He's remaining within the this our system we designed. But on the other hand, we can see that this person is being that this person is not in touch with reality. That they're too involved in the AI relationship and not actually like talking to real people. the next thing is designing an echo chamber of sorts. this is connected with a tendency of LLMs to be sycophant. Like LLMs tend to agree with us. They tend to reinforce our beliefs. They tend to They tend to tend to basically go with what this you say to them. And if we do not account for that, again, it may look good from the perspective of metrics. The person is having a long conversation. The person is liking the content of this conversation. But we can be reinforcing the beliefs that can be harmful. Maybe the person wants to harm themselves. Maybe they have in some delusional beliefs. And this is again something that we should watch out for when we design these types of characters. And the last thing is personality swap. That's basically what happened with ChatGPT, what we had in Replika. It's when we update the model, we believe that it became smarter, that people would love that, but they actually had some kind of connection to the previous one, and they feel like their character was swapped into some other being with this new model. Again, this is something to look out for.

**Для Напарника:** Метрики Напарника — реальные дела и возвращение к жизни, не время в приложении; промпты кота против поддакивания.

## 6. Цель — тренировка эмоциональных навыков; непрерывность, осознание привязанности, якоря реальности
`этика · маскот · доверие · процесс` · [14:13](https://youtu.be/FiGUKTILfQQ?t=853)

**Мысль:** Не создавать зависимость, а давать площадку для эмпатии; прозрачность обновлений, внимание к привязанности, связь с реальным миром, проектирование под уязвимого пользователя.

**Слова автора:**

> I believe that the goal should not be to create dependence or to replace human connection, but to use AI as training grounds for emotional engagement and empathy. And users who see AI this way actually tend to have a more positive experience. Like they are not ashamed of being connected to AI, they feel like they it helped them to be to feel better in their social life, for example, and they're openly sharing this type of stuff. And at Wargaming, we currently also try and explore AI to explore AI as a collaborator, but not a replacement both in our tools and further down the road probably player experiences, too. So, there are some practical things that can be used to create safe and responsible AI experiences. It all boils down to basically caring about your user, but there are some practical things that can be used. The first thing is concerned in continuity. I believe that we owe users to give them transparency and notice about model updates and maybe even given the give them a choice to use the model that they prefer. We had this in replica when people chatted with a model that we thought that was long outdated, but they had some attachment to it. They loved chatting with it. Why not? the next thing is attachment awareness is understanding that this attachment will happen and maybe towards healthy habits away from dependency. next thing is reality anchors. It's connected with the previous one and it's connecting your AI conversation to some real world stuff. For example, if it's a companion, it can bring up some ideas of what person wants to do in their outer world outside world. And this way helping to stay in touch with reality. And the last thing is ethics and enforcement. I believe that it should not just be ethics. We have to actively design and enforce these protocols. And the idea is that we should think that anyone can use our AI system. For example, a person who does not understand very well how AI works. Or the person can who can be in crisis in some vulnerable state of mind. And we have to think how they will interact with the system and what will be the flow with which we reach our goal.

**Для Напарника:** Кот приводит разговор к реальным шагам вне экрана; прозрачность изменений; продумать кризисные формулировки.
