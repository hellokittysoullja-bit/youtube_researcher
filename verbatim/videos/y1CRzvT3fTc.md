## 2. Качества бота: вежливость без осуждения, игривость, внимательное любопытство, эмпатия
`чат · стыд · маскот` · [03:08](https://youtu.be/y1CRzvT3fTc?t=188)

**Мысль:** Модель PEACE выделяет вежливость (уважительное, неосуждающее поведение), развлечение, внимательное любопытство и эмпатию как ключевые ожидаемые качества.

**Слова автора:**

> Politeness chatbots ability to establish respectful and non-judgmental behavior entertainment a promise of playful interactions diversity in chatbot's responses sense of humor and small talk attentive curiosity chatbot's ability to adapt to the users anticipate their needs and play a role of an active listener and empathy ability of an agent to recognize and respond to user emotions appropriately.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-лист тона для промптов api/companion: неосуждающий, игривый, любопытный, эмпатичный.

## 3. Вежливость как условие, юмор ведёт к принятию, эмпатия решает
`чат · исследования · доверие · маскот` · [06:17](https://youtu.be/y1CRzvT3fTc?t=377)

**Мысль:** Эмпирика (536 участников): вежливость — предпосылка вовлечения; юмор и развлечение сильнее всего влияют на намерение использовать; люди раскрываются при вежливости, поэтому нужны активное слушание и вопросы; эмпатия решающа.

**Слова автора:**

> It also allows us to extract several important implications first it demonstrates that individual differences in people's attitudes play a considerable role in forming their expectations about future chatbot technology therefore chatpot designers and developers should use this initial knowledge about their users to tailor user experience second politeness proved to be an essential prerequisite for people to consider engagement with social conversational agents therefore it is critical for developers to meticulously control the quality of the training data for their conversational models and regulate unethical chatbots replies by other means third in line with previous studies the peace model supports that entertainment and humor demonstrates superior leading effect on user adoption intention indicating that these qualities should be provisioned as a part of the childbirth social intelligence next our findings suggest that users would be willing to share their thoughts and information with the agent once the politeness requirement is satisfied thus developers should incorporate active listening and question asking capabilities into their chatbots finally our results prove chatbot's empathy to be one of the decisive factors influencing users desire to accept it so it's vital for future agents to combine both the ability to detect the user emotion accurately and generate an emotionally appropriate response.
