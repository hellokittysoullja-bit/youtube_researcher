## 1. Idle-игры менее веселы в минуту, но гораздо более затягивающие
`игровой-дизайн · дофамин · награды` · [02:21](https://youtu.be/SEBTKjD0x7M?t=141)

**Мысль:** Кликеры ускоряют прогресс эффективнее, чем игры с сюжетом, и требуют минимум навыка и внимания: чистая петля, морковка на палке.

**Слова автора:**

> Twists and turns of the experience itself and in general Idol games are less fun per minute than regular titles but are much more compelling per minute but how clickers FastTrack player progression at an efficiency that a game with a story or characters can't quite match at the same time they require minimal investment in player skill or attention so in many cases they're a distillation of the basic gameplay Loop that dangling carrot on the industry's game design stick.

**Для Напарника:** Минимальный навык и вход для начала шага: петля должна запускаться без усилия, особенно при СДВГ.

## 2. Ускорение производства возвращает в петлю, хотя стоимость растёт быстрее дохода
`награды · дофамин · игровой-дизайн · геймификация` · [05:39](https://youtu.be/SEBTKjD0x7M?t=339)

**Мысль:** Радость от резкого скачка скорости производства притягивает, а в целом стоимость новых ресурсов постепенно перегоняет накопление: действия становятся менее эффективными.

**Слова автора:**

> Of these buildings can be upgraded eight times and each upgrade doubles your production so the higher the base production rate the more effective the upgrade and it's extremely satisfying to watch your production rate Skyrocket after clicking your fingers off and it pulls you right back into the groove of things even if you're not necessarily within flow range and unlike a typical game where the mechanics get more and more challenging as you progress clickers make your actions less and less efficient instead with each new upgrade taking longer than the last so let's try to flesh out our previously one-dimensional summary of clickers and instead say this the cost of new resources gradually but inevitably outpaces your wealth accumulation that's clickers in a nutshell.

**Для Напарника:** Для острова: рост не должен усложняться в стоимость быстрее, чем радует; редкие заметные скачки (новая постройка) возвращают интерес.

## 3. Качественный успех: нет победы и поражения, всё решает восприятие игрока
`стыд · игровой-дизайн · доверие · мотивация` · [07:40](https://youtu.be/SEBTKjD0x7M?t=460)

**Мысль:** Без состояний провала барьер входа нулевой, а каждый апгрейд ощущается успехом без болезненного контраста поражения. Игрок по умолчанию идёт в своём темпе.

**Слова автора:**

> Important advantage to idle games that many modern AAA titles lack a subjective or qualitative success to give you an idea of what I mean by qualitative success let's look at the counterpart quantitative success if you die to an enemy ambush in Dark Souls you invoke a fail state which involves the game explicitly telling you that you died and you get sent back to the last bonfire at the end of your journey you can count up all the times you died and have a quantitative measure of all the times the game called your failures along the same lines when you defeat an opponent in Hearthstone this is an unqualified success a quantitative measure of your Victory the other guy of course gets a quantitative failure State when he loses but idle games by contrast have no Victory or failure conditions you can't reach a point where your reflexes or logic aren't sufficient enough to continue you likewise there's no amount of reflexes or logic that will get you to the end credits because they don't exist either so the barrier for entry is effectively zero but with all the Allure and satisfaction of sweet arbitrary progression and since games do lack Victory or failure conditions then whether you're succeeding or failing is entirely up to your own mind regardless of your actual efficiency your perception of progress in an idol game is unique to you and completely uninfluenced by those pesky quantitative measures that come from the game itself more often than not an idle game becomes more and more rewarding the more attention you're actively putting into it's just as easy to play clicker Heroes for an hour in a full-screen window as it is to leave it running in a browser Tab and just check on it every few minutes and even after tens or hundreds of hours idle games can still be compelling every upgrade feels like a success but you never feel that contrasting sting of failure you're encouraged by default to take things at your own pace whether it's 5 minutes a day or 5 hours there are no complex controls that require your dexterity no complex puzzle that require your full attention and in general idle games will just keep on trucking until you've got some time to come back and manage them.

**Для Напарника:** Базовый принцип без стыда: любой шаг считается успехом, нет красных состояний провала; игрок сам определяет темп.

## 4. Долгий хвост: новое обещание в каждой зоне и крупные открытия спустя недели
`удержание · игровой-дизайн · награды` · [09:28](https://youtu.be/SEBTKjD0x7M?t=568)

**Мысль:** Контент математически закрыт за днями и неделями игры, а каждая зона обещает что-то новое (экипаж, новая механика), что оживляет интерес при угасании.

**Слова автора:**

> Unlocks and new content can be mathematically locked behind days weeks or even months of play so there's always a long-term goal for the player to shoot for and the fact that a game can still surprise you after months plays heavily into what gives Idol games such a long tale I always look to a game called trimps here that exemplifies just how skillfully this can be done trims revolves around sending animals called trimps into battle you start in zone one and every Zone has 100 Battles but every battle is just a little bit more difficult than the last it's not uncommon to reach Zone 25 after a day or two of play but it can be weeks or even months before you reach Zone 230 which by the way unlocks a brand new game mechanic called magma this drastically changes the core gameplay altering the numerical Power Balance that you've become so familiar with up to this point and giving you something new to contend with and even the most efficient autoc clicking program won't be able to reach Zone to 230 in any meaningful amount of time to test this out I've had a game going for about a week now using an autor progression program called Auto trimps which makes the most efficient choices possible to progress I'm just now pushing into the lower 30 zones and it's only becoming more and more difficult from here but luckily every single zone carries the promise of something new like stronger equipment new jobs for your trimps or a brand new mechanic that revitalizes your interest.

**Для Напарника:** Показывать на острове горизонт будущего (силуэт ещё не открытого ориентира), а крупные открытия на значимых вехах.

## 5. Короткая петля без риска провала и без ощущения, что делаешь неправильно
`игровой-дизайн · стыд · награды · удержание` · [10:56](https://youtu.be/SEBTKjD0x7M?t=656)

**Мысль:** Кликеры замыкают привычную петлю на мгновенную награду без фейла, оставляют крошки о следующих наградах и работают по твоему расписанию.

**Слова автора:**

> So in a nutshell clickers are surprisingly s satisfying because they Short Circuit the conventional gameplay Loop into something more immediately rewarding without the risk of a fail State then once you're hooked they provide numerical progression at a ridiculous rate while leaving breadcrumbs about what kind of rewards you'll get next and while they can certainly be played with 100% of your attention they operate mostly on your schedule so you can play them as frequently or as rarely as you want without feeling like you're doing it wrong.

**Для Напарника:** Резюме принципов: мгновенная награда, намёк на следующую, любой ритм допустим.
