And that can be weird. Like sometimes people think of this dystopian kind of future that is already here, but it's not. like we as humans and designers and builders have a strong active choice in how we shape that future. And so we kept landing on this idea of optimism and how do we how do we show that optimism through craft because it's one thing to say that but visually speaking that craft we could have easily stopped this brand work after 4 weeks but we pushed it for 11 12 weeks because we really wanted to bring this colorful sunrise palette to contrast sterile white and black kind of feel these somber tones.
