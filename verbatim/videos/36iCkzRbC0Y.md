## 1. Много неформальных петель обратной связи, а не один еженедельный крит
`процесс · крафт` · [00:22](https://youtu.be/36iCkzRbC0Y?t=22)

**Мысль:** Формальный крит раздувается подготовкой; в Ramp завели небольшие группы доверия («truth seeking pods») по 3–4 человека, где просят честный отзыв: добрый, но не вежливый.

**Слова автора:**

> The best designs come from design teams not designers and it's all about feedback cloops you create within the team the problem is too many teams have a weekly crit and that's it so what happens is the formality of crit increases over time and this is something that Diego Zach the VP of design at ramp saw happen as the company scaled people were spending a lot of time preparing for it and making this like big presentations and hey I wanted to look good and I'm making all these Transitions and figma and all this stuff and actually like and the work became 20% of it now I've never been one to make particularly pretty decks but I've been in this situation multiple times and having a single weekly crit puts a ton of pressure on designers to make the most out of that time as a result you're probably only talking about the highlevel really big strategy decisions so here's how Diego handled that at ramp great if you want to be formal here's a place where you're really formal and then let's create all these other avenues for you to get feedback the least formal one the safest environment that you have is we've created these truth seeking pods which is three or four designers that are across the entire platform so they're not working the same things they get together two or three times a week and every time that they get together a different person presents their work through the lens of a decision that they've made so the goal is to have a very small group of high trust people that are going to help you deconstruct a decision a framework that got you somewhere and you set the expectation that you want real feedback you want people to be kind not nice.

## 2. Работа с открытой дверью: поздняя демонстрация превращает отзыв в нападение
`процесс · стыд · мотивация` · [02:20](https://youtu.be/36iCkzRbC0Y?t=140)

**Мысль:** Чем глубже в работе, тем сильнее эмоциональная привязанность и тем больнее обратная связь, плюс времени менять уже нет; делиться нужно на этапе наброска на салфетке.

**Слова автора:**

> That's a huge part of my philosophy as a design leader is the designers need to always have again the grass door up like you need to let people see what you're working on comment on it and really give you that sort of ongoing feedback that was so crucial problema in very many different design teams struggle with where designers wait a little too long before they share their work and so one when you too deep into the work you sort of have an emotional attachment to the solutions and so the feedback you get feels like an attack on you not on the work and people get like start of defend their solution their ideas rather than listening to feedback and it's also often a little too late really to kind of shift like you wen't the designer is likely running out of time and so they're showing the final thing like well you know there's only so much you're going to be able to change before you need to go and build this and so making sure designers sh share the work at the beginning of the idea like hey like here's a real concept I have I made a sketch on a napkin like let's talk about my sketch on a napkin.

**Для Напарника:** Созвучно тону продукта без стыда: показывать и просить помощи рано (первый шаг, черновик), пока нет привязанности и страха оценки.

## 3. Polish reviews: постоянная панель сеньоров перед передачей в разработку
`процесс · крафт · дизайн-система` · [06:16](https://youtu.be/36iCkzRbC0Y?t=376)
