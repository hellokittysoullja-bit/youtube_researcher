## 2. Предфильтр страшного сообщения: ИИ сначала проверяет, не плохое ли оно
`ИИ · СДВГ · стыд · запуск-задачи` · [02:58](https://youtu.be/NUU1n5O6d4o?t=178)

**Мысль:** Тревога перед открытием письма/сообщения создаёт барьер; если посредник заранее снимает угрозу, человек перестаёт откладывать.

**Слова автора:**

> And another way to pre-filter information is if you're actually scared of the information. If you don't want to know it for some reason, let's say there is a text from your friend just waiting on your phone, and you're not sure if it's good or bad, or you see an email from your boss and you're thinking, "Damn, what if it's bad?" Pre-filtering the information here can be, "Here is this thing that I received. Like, don't look at it. Just copy-paste it. Don't read the actual text. Give it any important context, like, this is from my boss, and if this is bad, it might mess up my confidence and I'll be thinking about it all evening. Please don't tell me what it's about, but in case it's not bad, then tell me what it's about, so then I can reply." I have done that so many times, and most of the time it wasn't anything scary, so it just helped me to overcome that initial blockage of looking at whatever it is, and it saved me the dread of keeping that task in the back of my head for the next month.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник может предлагать «посмотрю сам, скажу мягко, что там» для пугающей задачи — снижение эмоционального барьера старта.

## 3. Застревание часто ложная дилемма: ищем середину, а не силу воли
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · ИИ · мотивация` · [03:52](https://youtu.be/NUU1n5O6d4o?t=232)

**Мысль:** Выбор «A или B» обычно ложен; промежуточный вариант, к которому тянет, снимает паралич. Совет «заставь себя» не работает.

**Слова автора:**

> Getting unstuck or overcoming decision fatigue. Like we will often be like, "I want to do this, but I actually have to do this. So therefore, I'll be on my phone or I'll be doing nothing." You will be surprised how often that happens. But as a coach, I've noticed that it's often a logical fallacy. It's not do A or do B, and you can't do that. There are often middle grounds, you know, like the previous one where we talked about removing the emotional barrier first. And what I'll do is I'll just tell the whole scenario to ChatGPT. I'll be like, "Hey, I want to write a script, but I can't because I actually feel like to complete the script, I need to have a better website, which will be linked underneath the video." Literally happened to me this week. And ChatGPT said, "Well, you can write the script draft. And once you have done that, you can go back and do some decisions for the website. They will help you move forward with the next steps, and eventually when that's done, you can add that to finalize your script." And there you go, you're no longer stuck because there is a middle ground. It's not all or nothing. Tell whatever your story is to ChatGPT and ask for some perspective or some options that you can take that are ADHD friendly. Things you are inclined to doing because if it tells you, "Oh, just force yourself to do the thing you don't want to do," that's not going to work.

**Для Напарника:** В разбиении задачи (api/breakdown) предлагать «середину»: начерно один кусок вместо всего-или-ничего; без принуждения.

## 5. Ролевая репетиция сценария снимает тревогу перед реальным делом
`ИИ · мотивация · СДВГ` · [10:47](https://youtu.be/NUU1n5O6d4o?t=647)

**Мысль:** Проигранный в игре сценарий превращается в пережитый опыт, и страх первого раза уменьшается.

**Слова автора:**

> And practice for learning and confidence. Because if you were, let's say, a Renaissance person and you were still alive today, you would definitely ace a history test on the Renaissance because your brain would remember the sounds, the sights, the people, the daily struggles. It wouldn't just feel like some facts on a piece of paper. It would be your real life experience. And obviously, you can't time travel unless you know something that I don't, but you can do something very similar in ChatGPT, which is to play pretend and help yourself learn faster, better, and actually remember things in a way that sticks. Like, I'm learning about this thing. Can we pretend that I'm actually doing it? Give me a character, give me a task. Let's run through the scenario where I have to use my knowledge like I actually would need to in real life. That also removes some of the anxiety about trying something in a real situation because now you've practiced it. Like it gives you the confidence that you would know how to act if it did happen.

**Для Напарника:** Идея для напарника: «давай прорепетируем» трудный разговор/звонок перед запуском задачи.

## 6. Поддержка без пустых комплиментов: реальные причины, что решение верное
`ИИ · мотивация · стыд · тексты` · [12:00](https://youtu.be/NUU1n5O6d4o?t=720)

**Мысль:** Пустые похвалы не работают; конкретные аргументы, снимающие вину, дают решимость действовать.

**Слова автора:**

> Encouragement. Let's say you want to raise your prices, but telling your customers that you'll be charging more makes you feel guilty. And you can't make yourself bring it up. You can really use some new perspective from ChatGPT. So, go ahead and say, "Hey, I want to do this, but it's hard. I need encouragement. Don't lie to me. Don't give me empty compliments because that doesn't work. Just tell me real reasons why I can do this and why it's the right decision and why I shouldn't feel guilty about it." Like raising your prices isn't selfish, it's sustainable. If you undercharge, you will burn out and then you can't help anyone. Reading a few of these will make you so much more assertive and help you go and charge what you're worth, assert your boundaries, do things that push you out of your comfort zone, stick to your decisions without constantly overthinking them or spiraling into self-doubt. Use that encouragement to validate you and affirm you so you can go and do the thing.

**Для Напарника:** Тексты напарника: поддержка через конкретные причины («ты уже сделал X»), а не «ты молодец».
