## 1. Решения принимает лимбическая система; эмоция — тай-брейкер между логичными вариантами
`дизайн-система · нейро · доверие` · [00:41](https://youtu.be/ZCcMIchXHP0?t=41)

**Мысль:** Когда есть несколько рациональных вариантов, выбор делает ощущение «это правильно»; эмоциональный опыт улучшает не только конверсию, но и желание рассказать другим.

**Слова автора:**

> But the reality is we're not dr spock most of the way that we make decisions on a day-to-day basis is with what's called our lizard brain which is our limbic system and that's like the base of your brain where emotions are formed and actually long-term memory is recorded there as well so emotion is such a critical part of interface design because when we're designing interfaces we're really designing pathways for people and we're telling stories we're communicators and a lot of the way that people make decisions is through emotion so why did i choose to wear what i'm wearing today or you know the way that we kind of style our identity and our physical person and online and so forth has a lot to do with emotional decision making i mean there's some logic that happens there i won't deny that but when it boils down to it there are a lot of times where we find multiple logical options and how do we arrive at a final decision it's by saying oh this feels right that emotion is the tie breaker and just the decision-making point and if you think about like the types of conversations we have on in interface design it's like about you know getting people to convert to sign up or getting them to complete a task that sort of thing it's all about decision making points keeping them on that path and helping them complete that task if we could use emotion if we could think about that psychology lizard brain limbic system stuff we could perhaps make a better experience for people and it's not just about like okay i got this conversion but it's also the softer side of usability that i felt good about that experience and then that encourages me to tell others.

**Для Напарника:** Эмоциональный дизайн Напарника (кот, остров, тёплый тон) — не украшение, а механизм принятия решения «открыть приложение и начать».

## 2. Иерархия потребностей интерфейса: функциональность, надёжность, удобство, затем приятность
`дизайн-система · крафт · вкус` · [02:51](https://youtu.be/ZCcMIchXHP0?t=171)

**Мысль:** Десять лет индустрия решала удобство, а верх пирамиды (радость, запоминаемость, доверие) остался с низкой планкой; именно он создаёт глубину вовлечения.

**Слова автора:**

> So in the talk that i'm doing today and a book that i'm working on now too i talk about this hierarchy of needs which is based on the idea of you know the 1950s maslow's hierarchy of needs which is just a series of strata getting up to the top where you know you've got these base needs and then it enhances to like the self-actualization of forming an identity and relationship in a community and we can apply that to interface design and at the base of that an interface needs to be functional once it's functional it needs to be reliable we can count on it so no fail wheels then it needs to be usable that we can perform the tasks we want to perform and then there's an emotionally engaging a pleasurable aspect that's at the top and the premise of what i've been researching in my work at mailchimp and you know for my book and for my talks and stuff is that top piece that i feel like we've set that bar low that for the past 10 years we've been working really hard just to get things usable which is fantastic that's a noble cause and a very important thing that i'm sure is that our users appreciate that we're heading towards but you know couldn't we make something that's more pleasurable that's memorable that's fun or you know that inspires trust so it's just a deeper engagement level.

**Для Напарника:** После надёжного таймера и чата добавляем приятность: живой фон, дыхание кота, звук костра; это верхний уровень и основной отличитель Напарника.

## 3. Brand persona: спроси не только «кто они», но и «кто мы»; персонаж — каркас для решений
`маскот · персонаж · крафт` · [04:52](https://youtu.be/ZCcMIchXHP0?t=292)

**Мысль:** Описав черты личности бренда (дружелюбный, доверчивый, ворчливый), можно проверять каждую идею, пасхалку или игровую механику на уместность.

**Слова автора:**

> One place that people can start is by creating a brand persona and so we spend a lot of time as user experience designers we do research in the field we research our users try to understand what they need and then we design for that which is great so we're always asking who are they but we never ask who are we and what we're really trying to do as interface designers is we're trying to shift from human computer interaction to human to human interaction where our interface is that layer between the humans that are connecting here so creating a brand persona is flipping that on its head and saying okay so this is the persona of the brand that i'd like to create it has a personality that a product could be a personality or like a person and if that were a person who is it what are its traits it's personality traits is it an aggressive and you know somewhat friendly personality or is it sort of curmudgeon and kind of spiteful personality which may be appropriate in some situations or maybe it's docile and trustworthy so we identify those we create that persona and once we have that it's sort of like a framework on which we can hang our ideas and think about like is this the right thing for this interaction that we're going to design here like if we were going to create an easter egg or we were going to create like some fun discovered experience or maybe a game theory inspired experience is that appropriate for this personality.

**Для Напарника:** Задать для кота письменную персону (тёплый, ненадзирающий, присутствие) и проверять каждую реплику и анимацию на соответствие; согласуется с запретом на стыд.

## 4. Редизайн как длинный шахматный матч: маленькие шаги за месяцы, а не переворот за ночь
`процесс · удержание · дизайн-система` · [07:35](https://youtu.be/ZCcMIchXHP0?t=455)

**Мысль:** Изменения вводят постепенно (смена шрифта заголовков, организации), чтобы не пугать людей и не терять привычный поток действий.

**Слова автора:**

> Redesign process has been it's a long chess match so we have hundreds of thousands of people using the app and you can't just like flip it on its head overnight i know that some people felt like we flipped it on its head but all we really did just a few weeks ago was we changed the colors that was that was basically it and i know that seems dramatic but we started last year about seven months ago making small shifts knowing that where we were going was where we are today we're we're moving in that direction and so let's make a change over here to the interface that people might notice they might not we switched from trebuchet for headings to helvetica knowing that the product site was going to be very helvetica based and then we made you know a few organizational changes and stuff so it's like make this move over here so you can make this move to make that move and what you can do is you know that euphemism that you can boil a frog in a pot of water if you put them in while the water's cold and just so slow that's exactly what you're doing it's interesting it's like designing a forest yeah it i mean it really is that's that's the idea because we want we know that change can be a freaky thing for people and that doesn't mean that we're not going to change because although people might not like the idea of change they probably would not like us if we did not change they would they would become disenchanted yes so we have to change but we'll do that in a slow graduate way that helps people keep on doing what they're doing right.

**Для Напарника:** Менять AppBackdrop, навигацию и ключевые экраны мелкими итерациями; людям с СДВГ резкое изменение привычного интерфейса вредно.
