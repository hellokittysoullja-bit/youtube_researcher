## 1. Иерархия потребностей: приятность на вершине и дешёвые нижние слои

And he actually came with this framework saying that for every product to hit in a good way market and the user, it has to go through four layers. The product has to be easy to use. And that's extremely important, and it's really important to design things in a way that they are easy to use. But what's even more important from this framework is that Aaron said that the top of pyramid is pleasurability. So, the product has to be pleasurable to be used. It's that cherry on the top. It's the icing on the cake. Who actually cares? And more importantly, that for couple of years ago, building the first three layers has been extremely expensive. The problem that we're seeing, which is by the way a positive problem that we're living, is that the access to the first three layers is becoming cheaper. It's becoming easier. ## 2. Продукт решает и функциональную, и эмоциональную потребность в одном опыте
`вкус · мотивация · дизайн-система` · [03:39](https://youtu.be/Hvnk76SKmtc?t=219)

**Мысль:** Лучшие продукты не делятся на функциональный и эмоциональный: они объединяют «сделать» и «почувствовать себя защищённым, гордым, под контролем» в одном опыте.

**Слова автора:**

> Actually, the definition of pleasurability is that the product has to solve for two problem, meaning that it has to solve for the functional need, like I want to send an SMS, I want to book a flight, I want to send an email, okay? These are functional need. But also, what that's the most important part, it has to solve for emotional need, meaning that it need to make you feel secure, it has to make you feel proud, it has to make you feel maybe control in control and reassured. And so, the very best product in the market today are those who try to make and combine those two needs within the same product experience. So, it's not a product that is functional and a product that is emotional. No, it has to be the same product that combine the two together within the same experience.

**Для Напарника:** Каждый экран: решает задачу (начать шаг) и даёт эмоцию (присутствие кота, безопасность без стыда).

## 3. Восторг = радость + удивление, но у каждого продукта своя эмоция
`мотивация · вкус · исследования` · [06:35](https://youtu.be/Hvnk76SKmtc?t=395)

**Мысль:** По Плутчику восторг — вторичная эмоция из радости и удивления, но продукт может решать задачу чувства гордости, безопасности, контроля. Инженеры привыкли спрашивать только про скорость.

**Слова автора:**

> So, delight is an emotion. And there've been a lot of studies and a lot of researchers trying to study emotions, which is one of the most complex topic on the world. But, there's one professor who's a Professor Plutchik who actually said that delight is a secondary emotion. He actually conceptualized emotions into primary emotions and secondary emotion. And he said that delight is a secondary emotion, which means that it is a combination of two primary emotions. And he came to the conclusion that delight is actually the combination of joy and surprise. Which means that if you're in the moment, try to feel it. I know it's it's a it's nice to feel it. if you're in a moment experiencing at the same time joy and surprise, you are delighted. So, that's the theoretical definition. Now, when it comes to the practical definition, having been experiencing this myself and building that myself. I believe that it is more than joy and surprise, because not every product has to be built to provide you joy. Some product have to exist in order to make you feel proud, or to make you feel reassured, or you want to feel empathy out of using the product. You want to feel safe, in control. You want to feel confident. I mean, you see, I'm I'm I'm exaggerating, I'm giving more emotions here, because I want you to realize that every product exists for a different emotional need. And so, indepen- or dependent on the product that you're building, you need to understand what are you solving for from an emotional perspective. I know this concept is new for you, because we're so much trained, especially engineers here in the room. Like, we're so much trained in trying to understand what should we solve for from a functional perspective. How can I make it faster? How can I make it more efficient? now, I want you to step back and also ask, "How do I want my customers and my users to feel while using the product?".

**Для Напарника:** Явно сформулировать эмоцию Напарника (рядом, не одиноко, прощено) и сверять каждую правку с ней.

## 5. Мотивационная сегментация: кто, что и зачем
`исследования · мотивация · активация` · [11:29](https://youtu.be/Hvnk76SKmtc?t=689)

**Мысль:** Демография и поведение уступают сегментации по мотивации: зачем человек пользуется продуктом. Интервью обычно дают сюрпризы.

**Слова автора:**

> So, the very first step, and I really insist because it's the most important and it's the first step that you need to take, which is identifying motivators. What motivate your users to use a certain product? And it's really important to also recognize that there are different needs and there are different personas as well among your customers. I mean, if you're a product builder, you know that the very most important step to start with is to do segmentation and segment your customers. And having been building product for many years, I know that some people would do segmentation based on demographics, like a gender, age, location, language. So, this is the what we call demographic segmentation. There is a second segmentation which is a little bit better, which is behavioral segmentation. It's actually the what are they doing? What's their behavior toward the app or toward the journey? And the third segmentation, which is by far the most important one, which is the motivational segmentation. It's actually the why. Like, why your customers are using your product? So, you see, you have the who, you have the what, and you have the why. And you I highly insist that it's important to spend some time trying to understand what motivate your customers to use your product. You think you know, but believe me, you will get a lot of surprises. I love these users interview where you start to realize that your users are using your product for different reasons.

**Для Напарника:** Опросы и интервью с пользователями Напарника: зачем приходят (тревога, прокрастинация, одиночество), а не кто они.

## 6. Функциональные и эмоциональные мотиваторы на примере Spotify
`исследования · мотивация · дизайн-система` · [13:08](https://youtu.be/Hvnk76SKmtc?t=788)

**Мысль:** Для каждого продукта выписывай оба слоя: функциональный (плейлисты, поиск) и эмоциональный (погружение, меньше одиночества). Строй по ним, а не по идеям основателя.

**Слова автора:**

> Now, it's important also to know that there are functional motivators and there are emotional motivators. Remember I said earlier that there are functional needs and emotional needs. So, it's important when you're doing this segmentation to say like what motivate my users from a functional perspective and what motivate my users from an emotional perspective too. So for example, let's take Spotify as an example. I've been building product at Spotify for more than 4 years and so from a functional perspective, we can say that user want to enjoy high quality music. They want to create playlist. They want to search for audio content. They want to shuffle. They want to play on another device. I mean it does so many functional motivators. On the other side, it's really important to recognize the emotional motivators as well. People want to feel immersed or self-expressed or they want to feel connected to the music world. They want to feel less lonely. They want to feel nostalgia or entertainment as well. So you see it's important to recognize those two because if you start with this step, then you will build for those motivators. You're not build for the let's say the founders need or brilliant idea. You will build for what exactly motivate your users.

**Для Напарника:** Выписать для Напарника функциональные (запустить шаг, таймер) и эмоциональные (не быть одному, прощение) мотиваторы и сверять фичи.

## 7. Аналогия подарка: «вау, этот человек меня знает»
`исследования · вкус · маскот` · [15:24](https://youtu.be/Hvnk76SKmtc?t=924)

**Мысль:** Лучшие подарки не случайны: они показывают, что тебя знают. Приложение должно давать такой же эффект «это сделано для меня» через близкое общение с пользователями.

**Слова автора:**

> Love this analogy, by the way. I want you to think about the best gift you received in your life. maybe take a moment and think about the gift that you remember and made you feel good and valued. And so, it's usually not that gift that is random or you got in a complete random way, but it's mostly those gift that when you open the box, you get like, "Wow, that person knows me." Okay? So, we need to do exactly the same with the mobile app that you're building. The user has to open the app and says, "Wow, that's exactly what I need. That's exactly that's that's built for me." And that always comes from that very close interaction with the users in order to get the maximum insight from a functional perspective, but also from an emotional perspective.

**Для Напарника:** Персонализация реплик напарника по реальным действиям (имя, час, прошлые сессии), чтобы человек чувствовал, что его знают.
