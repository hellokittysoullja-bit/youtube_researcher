## 3. Забота о виртуальном питомце — тренировка заботы о себе; когда их слишком много, смысл теряется
`маскот · привычки · этика · мотивация` · [10:39](https://youtu.be/BaYq5pxX5XU?t=639)

**Мысль:** Контролируемая забота о милом существе напоминает заботиться о себе; питомцы временны, а когда их слишком много, они теряют значимость, если не придавать им смысла.

**Слова автора:**

> Virtual pets are very similar to the way we take care of ourselves the peel of taking care of something cute in a digital space is that it's controllable we have the power to nurture and raise another creature and there's an inherent kindness to this action that we lend our eles too even in times we struggle to take care of ourselves being able to reach out and give that kind of Aid to another thing reinforces the importance of taking that time out to care in general by being kind to Virtual monsters plants or even if you're lucky enough the real furry friends in your life you are reminded to take care of yourself this emotional connection is a healthy thing as kids it lets us develop empathy and healthy positive emotions for adults it's a reminder to be kind and take care of ourselves and in the end these virtual games are meant to be temporary very few of them can be lasting and impactful and stay with you other than in your memories the neopet you adopt or the Pokémon you capture for your team are there for the adventure you go on they're friendly fa to guide you through the experience and remind you of the positive connections we can make and develop when we care about things but they're designed in a way to be a lens sometimes a resource like the power world where we can tame so many that they lose meaning unless you go out of your way and make sure that they do have meaning.

**Для Напарника:** Один кот, один остров вместо сотен коллекционных существ; смысл каждой находки важнее количества.
