# How to pitch your designs like a pro

**Канал:** Dive Club 🤿 · 6 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/w3CtCzW9G8s

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.

## 1. Строить рассказ вокруг одного пикового момента, а не 20 идей
`процесс · тексты · внимание` · [01:06](https://youtu.be/w3CtCzW9G8s?t=66)

**Мысль:** Руководитель запомнит одну-две идеи из 20-минутной презентации, поэтому нужно осознанно выбрать пик и проектировать вокруг него, иначе слушатель выберет сам, и не обязательно главное.

**Слова автора:**

> The biggest mistake that you can make is starting to craft your presentation by pulling from all of the various visual artifacts that you've made over the last few weeks of exploration they'll sort of use these visuals as like their Q card they'll see it on screen they'll respond to it they'll react to it and then they'll repeat and they'll go to the next slide they'll look at what's on the slide they'll decide what they're going to say so you get a lot of content but it's not cohesive for someone who hasn't been living and breathing this project it gets really hard to follow that's why Chris follows a different approach when crafting a presentation he starts with words not visuals because the reality is that product leaders will probably only remember one I mean maybe two different ideas from your 20 minute presentation so the key is to build your story around a peak moment that emphasizes the exact idea that you want people to cling to if you can figure out what your Peak moment is in your presentation and then do that in pick that intentionally and then design around that's much more effective otherwise what happens is you throw 20 different ideas at me and then I decide which one I want to hang on to that might not be the most important one to you.

**Для Напарника:** Peak-End: на финале сессии и в Мире строить ощущение вокруг одного пикового момента (RevealIsland), а не рассыпать мелкие вспышки.

## 2. Начинать с готового блюда, как на TikTok: пик в начало
`тексты · лендинг · внимание` · [04:08](https://youtu.be/w3CtCzW9G8s?t=248)

**Мысль:** Лучшие создатели не начинают с муки, а сразу показывают готовое блюдо; так же и презентация должна сначала показать, к чему всё приведёт, а затем объяснять путь. Нарастание к кульминации рискует потерять внимание.

**Слова автора:**

> Designers tell project stories linearly here's the context here's the problem here's how we'll solve the problem Tada now it's time for Q&A but remember how Gabe valdia said the best designers are kind of like content creators I think the best designers are probably like Tik Tok creators within their own company who can create some an idea that's sticky and really engaging in the way that's really powerful it's why I love the analogy of a recipe video on Tik Tok the best creators don't start their video by pouring flour into a bowl or by showing a wat of dough lying on a table they suck you in immediately with a gorgeous shot of the finished dish only then do they show their process that's why when Chris is giving a design presentation he often front loads the peak moment most likely the peak moment is like somewhere towards the lat like probably what I'm most excited about is the way I figured out how to solve the problem a really simple thing to do is to just frontload that like one way to do this is to start with this is what I think the world looks like when all of this works really well and the customers are happier and the business is better and then work backwards these are the obstacles that are getting in our way that are preventing us from realizing this and these are all the different this is all the proof that I have that this is actually possible and that we can overcome these obstacles like I've thought through it I've tested some of these things so you sort of like back into this exciting Vision in the same way that a Tik Tok Creator wants to keep you from scrolling to the next video your goal is to capture your executive or product leaders attention right out of the gate they want to know like what does this do for the business what does this do for our customers and so you start there rather than all of the sort of like context building around the research and problem identification and stuff like that like just start with like where does this get us it's tempting to build up to this peak moment with a big Crescendo but if people start catching up on slack notifications halfway through then you've already lost.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг и онбординг Напарника: сразу показать результат (остров, который растёт, кот у костра), а не объяснять механику по шагам.
