## 2. Чат о деньгах: звучать как друг, а не как мать, которая стыдит
`стыд · тексты · ИИ · чат · доверие` · [07:07](https://youtu.be/L8uGT1px9IU?t=427)

**Мысль:** Тема чувствительна и связана с виной; поддержка вида «я тоже ошибался, вот что мне помогло» создаёт доверие, а упрёк ведёт к провалу.

**Слова автора:**

> And then from the user experience side of things when really thinking about the actual content in that we had to think about it from the perspective of what it feels like from the users to how they feel about money is a very sensitive topic and people don't like to really talk about it in person and or there's always this guilt written in it with that with all of that in mind from the secondary research we needed to think about how the personality should be presented and so one of the things was we didn't want it to be like someone's mother nagging and making them feel guilty that would be setting up for failure for us we had to think about it from a perspective of sounding like someone's friend a friend that's very encouraging that you know you can look up to and is relatable to and so what i did with some of the dialogue was whenever the olivia presented the spending how much the user is spending in a certain area olivia would say like you know i've made some don't worry i've made similar mistakes before but here's the way i was able to save money and then introduce a tip accordingly like that.

**Для Напарника:** Тон кота: друг, а не надзиратель; реплика «я тоже так делал» вместо «ты не выполнил».

## 3. Якорь из исследований и заранее приготовленная поддержка, чтобы не вызывать вину

**Мысль:** Среднее значение даёт ориентир, а превышение сопровождается успокаивающей репликой и поощрением, а не упрёком; многое зависит от контекста человека.

**Слова автора:**

> There's also the idea of social proof and authority too where when if the user wants to if the user is presented with the option of how much do other people spend in the spend category then olivia would point out some data from you know based on the secondary research like you know pew research center gallup polls of how much users how much the average user spends so setting an anchor point of how much they are how much how much the average person spends so that they get a good idea of what they should aim for in the target and what did you ever think about if you presented this data and this is the first time they're seeing it let's say the average person spends 300 a month on groceries but this particular user spends a thousand a month how do you preemptively prevent them from feeling guilty about it like i said like it's really important to have like a reassuring thing that you know that you know it don't worry you know i've made similar mistakes before and kind of building that relatability that you can get better at it with through encouragement and not just scolding them.
