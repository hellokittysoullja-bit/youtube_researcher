## 2. Безопасность при сохранённой возможности учиться

## 3. Уютной игре нужен «край» — доля дискомфорта для контраста
`игровой-дизайн · вкус · игровой-дизайн` · [02:09](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=129)

**Мысль:** Немного холодного и резкого делает мягкое тёплое одеяло остальной игры значимым и уютным.

**Слова автора:**

> To me, what a cozy game needs to feel fulfilling is something that gives the game a bit of discomfort, which lets the comfort of the rest of the game feel meaningful. Something sharp, cold. Something that makes the soft, warm blanket of the rest of the game feel comforting and well, cozy. A cozy game needs an edge.

**Для Напарника:** Допустить в тоне Напарника лёгкую иронию или честную каплю несовершенства для контраста (но не стыд и не давление).

## 4. Три критерия уютной игры
`игровой-дизайн · крафт` · [03:21](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=201)

**Мысль:** Простые и понятные механики, медленный методичный геймплей, намеренно заложенное ощущение комфорта.

**Слова автора:**

> Feel free to disagree with these, but to me, a cozy game needs to fit three criteria. One, it has mechanics that are simple and easy to grasp. Two, it has slow, methodical gameplay. Three, the feeling of comfort is common and intended by the developers.

**Для Напарника:** Рабочая проверка для любой новой фичи Напарника по трём критериям: просто, медленно, намеренно тепло.

## 5. Несовершенство арта делает мир человеческим, но не продаёт сам
`вкус · крафт · дизайн-система` · [04:50](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=290)

**Мысль:** Неровная пиксельная графика и «кривые» пропорции показывают живых авторов, поэтому игре верят. Но этот приём стал распространённым, и одного арт-направления не хватает для глубины.

**Слова автора:**

> This aspect of authenticity is essential for a good cozy game to me. The world of the game needs to feel like it was made by real people with their own thoughts and feelings. And if we think of a game as a piece of art meant to express those thoughts and feelings, then the art style itself is a very raw and direct way to engage with the people who crafted it. When you look at Stardew Valley and its highly textured pixel art and wonky building proportions, you can tell that it was made by someone. Someone whose art style uses a lot of dithering and emphasizes shadows and bright colors. Someone who doesn't care that pixels don't all conform to the same pixel grid and doesn't care to limit their color palette size. Someone who was possibly learning it all on the fly and who just wanted to make a game for you and I to enjoy. I think this comes through in A Short Hike as well with its low-poly models at a downscaled resolution, and all the way back in the original Animal Crossing with its highly stylized, crunchy textures. These games were made by real people and this can be discerned, I think, based on the art style alone. The art is imperfect but through those imperfections, those rough edges, the human being behind these games can be seen and felt. The brush strokes, the pixels placed, the models crafted, all done by human hands. I think this sort of human imperfection element has been noticed by game developers, and it's now being utilized as an intentional design choice. In A Little to the Left, there's a hand-drawn vibe conveyed through the rough edges on each of the object drawings. In Potion Craft, it mimics the style of medieval drawings, which are sort of known to be wonky and weird by today's standards. While I do appreciate that this element of human imperfection can help a cozy game be successful, I also think that because it's now a common technique, it means that it alone cannot sell a cozy game. By that, I mean good art direction doesn't necessarily allow a game to feel fulfilling.

**Для Напарника:** Допустить в иллюстрациях кота и острова лёгкую рукотворность, но не рассчитывать на неё как на единственное отличие.

## 6. Медленное не должно тратить время игрока
`время · игровой-дизайн · доверие` · [07:29](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=449)

**Мысль:** В Animal Crossing лишние шаги (перетаскивание инструментов, продажа по одному, сложное сохранение) раздражают; игра должна ценить, что человек выбрал играть, а не заняться другим.

**Слова автора:**

> Feel that some of the gameplay was out of date. I mean, yeah, it is out of date. The game is 25 years old. But what I mean is that the gameplay is at a snail's pace at times. Equipping your tools by dragging them onto your character, selling items one at a time, even saving the game is a whole process. But maybe this was a consequence of the times. Maybe there were technical limitations and concessions had to be made with some of the quality of life features. Well, that's partially true, but there were actually similar complaints about the latest entry in the franchise, Animal Crossing: New Horizons. I remember people complaining a lot about the lack of bulk crafting and about tool durability and the fact that the game feels like a lot of its mechanics are designed with the goal of wasting your time. Like I mentioned in my first video about Silksong, I personally think that a game should always try to value the fact that a player is choosing to play it instead of doing something else. So I think that the fact that even 20 years later, the Animal Crossing franchise is still struggling with this aspect means that it's a good case study of how slow, methodical gameplay can veer too far into point two, where it starts to feel like a waste of time.

**Для Напарника:** Любое действие в Напарнике (старт сессии, отметка шага) должно занимать минимум тапов; медленность только в атмосфере, не в интерфейсе.

## 7. Хорошая медлительность — в действиях, не в меню
`игровой-дизайн · анимация · хаптика` · [08:51](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=531)

**Мысль:** Ловля рыбы, перестановка мебели, разговоры: мелкие мгновенные действия с понятной обратной связью, без трения, где понятно, удалось ли действие.

**Слова автора:**

> And despite all of the negativity I've expressed so far, I do think there are examples of Animal Crossing being very, very good at slow gameplay. I find that the acts of catching fish and bugs, talking to your neighbors, placing and rearranging furniture, and more recently, terraforming are all really well designed. What sets these apart from my earlier complaints though is that these are all part of truly playing the game and not part of tedious menuing. Let's go back to our cozy game framework, to point one. Simple, easy to grasp mechanics. All of Animal Crossing's slow, methodical tasks are performed through tiny microscopic gameplay mechanics that are both grounded in reality and simplified to a degree that feedback is immediate, understandable, and satisfying. Swinging a net and pushing a sofa are instantaneous actions, so there's no friction in performing the action, and it's always clear when you succeeded or how and why you didn't. The onus is on the player to use these small scale actions how they see fit, which respects the player's time and choices to live in the game's world at their own pace.

**Для Напарника:** Все внутренние действия острова и кота делать мгновенными с чётким откликом; меню и подтверждения свести к минимуму.

## 8. Отсутствие контроля делает мир живым
`игровой-дизайн · маскот · мотивация` · [10:10](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=610)

**Мысль:** Нельзя выбирать планировку города или кто переедет: ощущение, что мир живёт с тобой и без тебя, и ты лишь часть его.

**Слова автора:**

> There are plenty of things that can happen that are completely outside of the player's control. There's no control over the town layout, the villagers moving in or away, even what your character looks like. It's abrasive, and some could say it doesn't respect the player. But I personally find that the opposite is true. While it can take this abrasiveness a bit far at times, I think the lack of control adds to the feeling that you're living in a world that's alive. You have agency over yourself, and you choose how to live in a world that moves around you, in a world that lives and breathes with or without you, and where you're just a part of it. That is the magic of Animal Crossing to me. making the most of what you have, the neighbors you meet, the furniture you find, the time of day and year. Living in a town where you participate, can live in, but not dictate.

**Для Напарника:** Остров может сам удивлять небольшими событиями (редкая находка, гость у костра), которые игрок не контролирует, но принимает.

## 9. Лёгкая резкость реплик освежает сладкий тон
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · вкус` · [12:22](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=742)

**Мысль:** Резкие реплики в оригинальном Animal Crossing разбивают монотонность, и игроки по ним скучают больше, чем по безопасным и повторяющимся.

**Слова автора:**

> Harsh dialogue in general is a great way to add some refreshing roughness to a cozy game, I think, since it breaks up the monotony, adding some spice to the normally very sweet gameplay. The original Animal Crossing is sort of infamous for this actually. Practically all of the characters insult you over and over without holding back. It's kind of a lot to be honest, and it's probably another case where the game shows its age. At the same time, I often see that people miss the mean dialogue, and I don't blame them. I also prefer it to the repetitive, safer dialogue of the newest entry.

**Для Напарника:** Не применимо в полном виде: тон Напарника без стыда. Но допустимы шутливые, чуть колючие реплики кота без оценки человека.

## 10. Неидеальный мир, который ты улучшаешь, делает уют значимым
`игровой-дизайн · вкус · мотивация` · [13:16](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=796)

**Мысль:** Мусор на карте и свалка в Animal Crossing и Stardew создают чувство, что мир далёк от совершенства, а игрок на нём что-то меняет; город стартует с низкой точки.

**Слова автора:**

> It's a pretty scrappy experience, honestly. And it helps sell the feeling that you're just barely getting by despite the coziness. I really think that the scrappiness, though, helps sell the coziness. It makes it feel more real and meaningful. Stardew Valley is pretty filthy as well, at least at the start of the game. There's trash found everywhere, like in Animal Crossing, especially the southernmost point of Cindersap Forest where the sewer drips green sludge into the ocean. And you can also find old CDs manufactured by Joja all over the map, the same company that's trying to turn the community center into a warehouse. All of this again adds to the feeling that the world is far from perfect and adds to the feeling that you, as the new farmer, are doing something about it. It adds some depth by establishing the town at a low point.

**Для Напарника:** Остров стартует «бедным и пустым» (без стыда), а рост видимо улучшает мир: так прогресс виден контрастом.

## 11. Смена тона очищает вкус и сохраняет свежесть тепла
`игровой-дизайн · вкус · удержание` · [14:21](https://youtu.be/x-3F1TRYq00?t=861)

**Мысль:** Если механики и арт зафиксированы, разнообразие тона (явное в диалогах, неявное в мире) создаёт контраст, который удерживает уют свежим.

**Слова автора:**

> The point is, I think that if mechanics and art style are locked in, then a game can always add some variety by mixing up its tone every now and then. Whether it's explicit through the dialogue or implicit like the world itself, it adds some contrast that helps cleanse the palette to keep the warmth, the comfort, and the coziness feeling fresher for longer.

**Для Напарника:** Разнообразить реплики и сцены Напарника (рассветы, дождь, ночь), чтобы тепло не приедалось.
