## 1. Тёмные паттерны сильнее бьют по нейроотличающимся: СДВГ покупает весь набор, паника от таймера
`СДВГ · этика · тёмные-паттерны` · [01:19](https://youtu.be/KphVR7Ote30?t=79)

**Мысль:** Дизайн проектируется для «нормы», а люди с СДВГ, тревогой, аддикциями несоразмерно уязвимы к давлению.

**Слова автора:**

> But for neurodiverse users these patterns can cause significant harm neurodiversity refers to a variation in the human brain regarding sociability learning attention mood and other mental functions folks who are more neurodiverse from the quote unquote normal brain we've been socialized to expect might be considered to have adhd autism ocd anxiety dissociation and more even though cognitive diversity is ubiquitous these individuals are considered outside of the majority as a result a lot of designs and designers do not consider them at all and dark patterns are no exception instead of buying a few extra things somebody with adhd might end up buying an entire set of products regardless of if they're able to afford it or somebody with a gambling addiction might be triggered and have a relapse or in my case i could get panic attacks when there's a countdown on a website for me to put in my credit card information.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник — продукт для СДВГ, поэтому обязателен этический фильтр: никаких таймеров обратного отсчёта, давления «купи сейчас» и скрытых платных ловушек.

## 2. Нейроотличающиеся сильнее подвержены фрейм-эффектам: дефицит, неприятие потерь, статус-кво, bandwagon
`СДВГ · этика · нейро` · [04:05](https://youtu.be/KphVR7Ote30?t=245)

**Мысль:** Тревожные люди чувствительнее к страху (дефицит, потеря), а перегруженные — к предвыбранному и популярному; те же приёмы вредят им сильнее.

**Слова автора:**

> Cognitive biases are pretty universal for humans based on our cognitive evolution but people who are neurodiverse may experience them differently for example people with anxiety have on average more active amygdalas which is the part of the brain responsible for risk assessment and fear that increased fear response can make people with anxiety more susceptible to fear-based cognitive biases such as artificial scarcity which makes things seem more valuable because they're rare and loss aversion being more affected by losses than by gaining an equivalent thing individuals with ocd may have disrupted ventromedial prefrontal cortices a mouthful i know which are vital in decision making as a result they might be more vulnerable to biases that put extra pressure on the user's cognitive load or offer an easy way to quickly lighten the cognitive load such as the status quo bias accepting what is already pre-selected or the bandwagon effect selecting whatever is most popular now remember the dark patterns are specifically designed to prey on our cognitive biases because people with cognitive differences can be more susceptible to cognitive biases dark patterns can also be much more harmful.

**Для Напарника:** Дефолт «25 минут рекомендуем» оставлять прозрачным и необязательным (предвыбранный вариант не должен давить); не использовать дефицит.

## 3. Таймеры, confirm-shaming («я скучный»), misdirection бьют по тревоге и СДВГ
`СДВГ · стыд · тёмные-паттерны` · [06:38](https://youtu.be/KphVR7Ote30?t=398)

**Мысль:** Обратный отсчёт запускает страх, пристыжающие кнопки отказа могут быть триггером, запутанные формулировки и отвлечение внимания чаще обманывают людей с СДВГ.

**Слова автора:**

> Somebody with anxiety might have a significantly heightened experience of artificial scarcity if a site is yelling at them to buy quickly before everything is gone they might not just end up buying something they don't want they might have lasting cognitive fatigue as well their threshold for design becomes a dark pattern because their threshold also might be lower than that for an average user the design might have a negative experience for a user with anxiety well before it is for somebody without anxiety another dark pattern is that particularly effective on individuals with anxiety is timed interactions this is common when a website has a promotion or a sale the urgency associated with the countdown triggers that fear response again inhibiting the user's decision making confirm shaming is when the site tries to shame you into doing something usually signing up for their mailing list the decline option usually appears to be making some judgment about the user cosmo magazine has one which requires you to click on the message i'm boring some people might scoff at the clear pettiness of that message but on a bad social anxiety day that message can be hugely triggering or somebody with certain expressions of autism might take that message as literal instead of seeing it as unnecessarily aggressive lots of dark patterns just focus on making information as difficult as possible to understand trick questions use difficult language to prevent you from making informed decisions since you don't understand the choice you're making this can be really hard for people with language processing difficulties or a site might use misdirection which distracts you away from important information to trick you into accepting terms or extra costs people who are easily overwhelmed or have difficulty reading instructions such as folks with adhd or anxiety will fall for this trick much more frequently.

**Для Напарника:** Кнопки отказа и пропуска в Напарнике нейтральны и добры («Не сейчас»); никаких пристыжающих формулировок; чёткий простой язык.

## 4. «Грамотность манипуляций» перекладывает ответственность на человека вместо системы
`этика · доверие · доступность` · [09:20](https://youtu.be/KphVR7Ote30?t=560)

**Мысль:** Просить пользователя распознавать тёмные паттерны несправедливо; свободный информированный выбор и безопасность — права, которые обязан обеспечить дизайн.

**Слова автора:**

> Majority of solutions presented by dark pattern scholars for users to protect themselves is manipulation literacy manipulation literacy encourages users to educate themselves on dark patterns and learn to recognize them in the wild much like problematic historical views of disability the onus of responsibility is placed on the individual and not on the system and place which discriminates against the disabled folks making free and informed choices is a human right protection of safety is a human right.

**Для Напарника:** Ответственность за безопасность выбора на продукте: не рассчитывать, что человек распознает манипуляцию.

## 5. Продукт должен нравиться, потому что хорош, а не потому что обманул; проектируй для краёв
`доступность · этика · СДВГ` · [13:03](https://youtu.be/KphVR7Ote30?t=783)

**Мысль:** Прозрачность и честность — ключ; проектировать нужно для всего спектра людей, не для «нормального» пользователя.

**Слова автора:**

> Think critically about your design choices and who is represented in your design space users should buy your product because it's a good product not because you've tricked them into wanting it transparency and honesty are key and always think about the human at the other end of your design and consider the full spectrum of humans not just who you think is a quote-unquote normal user design for the edges and ensure that you are creating a safe user environment for everyone.

**Для Напарника:** Принцип «design for the edges»: Напарник проектируется для людей с плохим фокусом, и это улучшает опыт для всех.
