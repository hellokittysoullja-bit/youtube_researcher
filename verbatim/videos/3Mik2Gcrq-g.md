## 1. Простое движение как тест дизайна: выразителен ли он и легко ли анимируется
`анимация · персонаж · маскот · крафт` · [00:00](https://youtu.be/3Mik2Gcrq-g?t=0)

**Мысль:** Поворот головы показывает, оживает ли персонаж и не слишком ли дизайн сложен и жёсток; он помогает выбрать стиль движения под пропорции дизайна.

**Слова автора:**

> A head turn is a basic animation movement that should be an early exercise when learning animation in film. It's mainly used as a reaction shot. Because it's a very simple movement, it's often used as a way to test the static design for an actual animation pipeline. When you do this exercise, your character will not only come to life for the first time, but also you'll be able to recognize if the design is expressive and easy to animate or if the design is over complicated and stiff. You will be able to decide what type of movement will look best with your design. A character with realistic proportions may look bizarre if it is moving with a ton of smears or squashes and stretches like a Looney Tunes character.

**Для Напарника:** Прогонять кота через простой цикл (поворот головы, моргание) перед тем как дорабатывать выражения: если дизайн «не оживает», упростить.

## 2. Убери мелкие детали, они не работают в анимации
`анимация · персонаж · крафт` · [02:03](https://youtu.be/3Mik2Gcrq-g?t=123)

**Мысль:** Анимацию приходится перерисовывать 24 раза в секунду, поэтому веснушки, лишние линии и тени хороши для иллюстрации, но мешают в движении.

**Слова автора:**

> Animation is a handmade medium that has to be redrawn 24 times a second, and characters with lots of details are really difficult to animate. And Willow here has a ton of tiny extraneous details that look good for illustration, but would not work well for animation. The freckles, the extra ey lines, and the nose shadows all need to go. And we're going to simplify her hair a bit, too.

**Для Напарника:** Спрайты кота и ориентиров на острове держать без мелкой детализации, чтобы они читались и плавно двигались на мобильном.

## 3. Планирование и тамбнейлы: идея движения, замах для отскока
`анимация · процесс · крафт` · [02:36](https://youtu.be/3Mik2Gcrq-g?t=156)

**Мысль:** Планирование — основа качественного исполнения: сначала черновые рисунки, затем поза-замах (anticipation), добавляющая подскок, и преувеличенная реакция в нужном кадре.

**Слова автора:**

> They are two things that are at odds with each other. But in animation and other art forms where there can be objective quality, planning is a fundamental component of good execution. So, we need to come up with an idea for the motion we want and how we're going to get there. Like, there are so many options for how she could turn her head. For example, in a very mechanical sense, she could just move her head from A to B without emotion or feeling. She could ease super slowly at first and then snap. Or we could have her sort of jump up and notice something and then kind of snap turn. I kind of like the last one. So, let's try to figure out how to convert this motion into drawings. I'm going to do an initial series of exploratory drawings where I'm just going to try to figure out how it's going to work. This is called thumbnailing, and it's how animators eloos plan the acting. We need to go from here to here. And I want her to jump up a little bit. So, I'll add a drawing in here and then add some space on the timeline before she goes to pose B. This new drawing here is going to be the frame where her eyes are kind of wide and it'll be just a little bit exaggerated. As you can kind of see, Brent is drawing on top of other drawings so that he can keep track of things. This method of drawing over top of barely visible layers is called potato skinning. Well, it's not actually called that, but if you do want to correct me in the comments, go for it so that we can get that engagement up. Okay, let's add two more drawings to this. The first one will be the anticipation pose where she actually moves down first before coming up so that we get sort of a bounce up motion.

**Для Напарника:** Реакция кота на находку: сначала лёгкое приседание, потом подъём; продумать идею движения до CSS-реализации.

## 4. Не ставь промежуточный кадр ровно посередине: динамичные интервалы живее
`анимация · вкус · крафт` · [04:14](https://youtu.be/3Mik2Gcrq-g?t=254)

**Мысль:** Равномерное расстояние между кадрами выглядит механически; ближе к первому кадру ставь промежуточный, чтобы получить плавный вход и щелчок. Мозг достраивает дугу по одному кадру.

**Слова автора:**

> Breakdown drawings is to put a drawing exactly in the center of the animation. That creates a situation where you have even spacing between your drawings and it looks very mechanical. What we want to do is put a drawing somewhere close to the first frame so that she kind of eases into the turn and snaps. This is what it looks like on a graph. Imagine this ball is the movement of her face. The frames are all spaced together super evenly. Like nobody moves like this. And on this graph, I've got a lot more areas of close together frames and dynamic spacing. This motion is going to look a lot more interesting and breathe life into the character. Also, check this out. Even though there is only one frame at the bottom, we still read the motion as a smooth arc. Your brain fills in the implied movement. Think about this stuff the next time you animate. Like if a character is reaching down to grab something, does it happen in a bunch of super evenly spaced drawings like a robot? Or is there an opportunity to have dynamic motion?

**Для Напарника:** Кривые easing для кота и острова: неравномерные (cubic-bezier с мягким стартом и резким финалом), а не linear.

## 5. Ключевые позы держат движение; вторичная анимация убирает «пластик»
`анимация · персонаж · крафт` · [06:47](https://youtu.be/3Mik2Gcrq-g?t=407)

**Мысль:** Ключевые позы рисуют лучше всего, промежуточные могут выглядеть странно, но служат плавности. Волосы, которые запаздывают и успокаиваются после движения, не дают персонажу выглядеть жёстким пластиком.

**Слова автора:**

> Trace the character model to keep the design consistent and I will spend more time roughing out the key poses which are here to make these drawings the best. The key poses of animation are what really hold the motion together but the other drawings like the breakdowns and the in-betweens are more about facilitating the movement. So often times they may look a bit strange because they were trying to fill in from A to B while maintaining the right spacing. So, people that screenshot these frames in order to make fun of them are just revealing that they don't know how animation works. Wow, look at that. We have our rough pass complete. Also, check out these frames here. The hair drags behind and then settles after the head movement is complete. This is called secondary animation. And it's good to have on parts of your characters that would move independently so that you can avoid having your characters feel like they're made out of rigid plastic.

**Для Напарника:** Уши, хвост и усы кота запаздывают относительно тела при поворотах и реакциях (overlap), а не двигаются как один блок.
