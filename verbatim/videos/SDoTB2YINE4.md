## 1. CRO — это не смена цвета кнопки, а поиск трения с точки зрения пользователя
`исследования · процесс · метрики` · [01:38](https://youtu.be/SDoTB2YINE4?t=98)

**Мысль:** Оптимизация без исследования — это ярлыки; суть в выявлении барьеров и фрикции у клиента.

**Слова автора:**

> Cro is often mistaken it's just a b testing experimenting with landing pages directly changing the conversion page or the most common would be changing the ca CTA buttons color or text yes there are tactics we can try but it's not just that cro is far more complex and beautiful than that believe it or not when conversing during a strategy alignment or a performance review I've heard people say a lot of things about their understanding of cro as you can see right there those are some of the common insights they are all more focused on providing shortcuts for their users that benefit their business without fully understanding why and what the problem is well their tactics may not be entirely wrong the core issue is that the approach to optimization is not grounded on Research yes I agree that we want users to perform a specific action to bring us business but cro is really concerned with determining the blockers and friction points from the perspective of your users and customers and we try to address it these are the things that impede businesses from getting that sale and nurturing a relationship with customers.

**Для Напарника:** Любые правки онбординга и Фокуса начинать с наблюдения за реальным пользователем, а не с догадок о цвете кнопки.

## 2. Пройди продукт свежим взглядом и зафиксируй боли, которых команда не видит
`исследования · процесс · онбординг` · [07:04](https://youtu.be/SDoTB2YINE4?t=424)

**Мысль:** Клиент не замечает собственных сложных и избыточных шагов, пока их не визуализировать.

**Слова автора:**

> First thing I like to do is experience the business in their website without much previous information about it I come in with a fresh perspective a fresh pair of eyes I will try to experience the whole thing with usually just the knowledge of what they do or what their safe the service they provide this way I can also see if there's something in the process that just doesn't make sense to me as a user and probably there are other users as well this will allow me to make sense of the data I will be seeing when I check their data points later on I will also try to determine what are the selling points of the company from my perspective as a potential buyer I have to be careful though because I want to avoid bias as much as possible I just need to make sure that I take note of the pain points they have because more often than not our clients are not aware of it until it's explicitly pointed out take for example the screenshot I have there it's there an e-commerce company that didn't realize how long and redundant their process is until it was visualized.

**Для Напарника:** Регулярно проходить путь «первый визит → первая сессия» как новичок, записывая моменты, где неясно.

## 3. Не лепи заплатки: спроси пользователей и бенчмаркни конкурентов критически
`исследования · процесс` · [09:40](https://youtu.be/SDoTB2YINE4?t=580)

**Мысль:** Ограничиваясь интуицией, получаешь пластыри; опрос и юзабилити-тест дают ответ; копируя конкурента, не знаешь, работает ли у него.

**Слова автора:**

> My fourth step is to spend time researching don't just throw well at this it just doesn't work like that sure we've determined the initial friction points and opportunities but if we limit ourselves to just lose information we increase the chances of just doing Band-Aid Solutions sometimes the answer is just there if we ask our users so we do surveys or user testing sometimes we just get blinded by beautiful layouts so we forget to design things to allow the brain to make connections and shortcuts so that's where heuristics come from often we're missing out on opportunities just because we wanted to copy the competition doing competitor benchmarking the most important question we need to ask is how we can improve on what they are doing why because we don't really know if what they're doing is actually working so we want to improve on that.

**Для Напарника:** Перед переработкой экрана Дом задать 3–5 пользователям с СДВГ один вопрос, а конкурентов (Finch, Forest) не копировать вслепую.

## 4. Перед A/B-тестом посчитай MDE, длительность и выборку
`метрики · исследования · процесс` · [10:35](https://youtu.be/SDoTB2YINE4?t=635)

**Мысль:** Без предварительных расчётов можно запустить недостоверный эксперимент и потратить ресурсы впустую.

**Слова автора:**

> Help decide if experimentation is possible and how you should design it in last year's conference this was one of my key takeaways from Haley Carpenter so be mindful to be mindful of your minimal minimum detectable effect test duration and sample size there are pre-test calculations or a sample size calculator available online just check but this step is extremely helpful to stop you from doing experiment statement that may not be really trustworthy and fleshing out resources that could have been allocated elsewhere.

**Для Напарника:** При малом трафике не гонять A/B-тесты; лучше качественные интервью.
