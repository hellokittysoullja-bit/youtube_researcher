## 1. Антипаттерн: идеальное локальное решение с «делайтом» сверху
`процесс · дизайн-система` · [00:58](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=58)

**Мысль:** Дизайнеры по умолчанию берут одну проблему, строят идеальную модель внутри пузыря и добавляют красоту сверху, не думая о связях.

**Слова автора:**

> Mode for a lot of designers when they work on problems is they want to kind of okay let's focus on this one problem and then make a really good solutions for that and then we add all the delight and all the Crazy Craft on top of it they focus on this thing they come up with like a ideal sing Single model that does everything within that bubble.

## 2. Решение каждой проблемы отдельной фичей усложняет систему
`процесс · дизайн-система` · [01:29](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=89)

**Мысль:** Продукт можно видеть как набор блоков-примитивов; точечные фичи сверху множат сложность.

**Слова автора:**

> It was mostly system thinking building the blocks when you're building a product that serves so many different groups of users so many different use cases you can't think in one way or the other way you need to come up with good systems to tie up all this different people's needs people's mental models of how their tools work different variations of doing say project management but then it's like still kind of built with the same ideas Primitives building block you can think of most digital products as a collection of blocks but if you solve each product problem in isolation then you'll end up creating a new feature or in this case new block for each solution you can't build single purpose features in notion and try to slap it on top of it cuz it will make the systems more complex.

**Для Напарника:** Новые механики Напарника (реплики, чек-ин, ориентиры) строить из общих примитивов (пузырь, награда, ориентир), а не добавлять отдельные виджеты.

## 3. Успех: минимум уникальных блоков, которые делают максимум
`процесс · дизайн-система` · [02:26](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=146)

**Мысль:** Лучшие дизайнеры решают корневые проблемы и предвидят будущие нужды, добиваясь системы с наименьшим числом частей.

**Слова автора:**

> Even if you're not designing a general purpose tool like notion the best designers dig deep to understand root level issues instead of solving each new problem as it sprouts that's what allows systems thinkers to see the entire set of blocks as well as anticipate future product needs from that vantage point success isn't just solving all the problems it's figuring out the broader product puzzle that using the least number of unique blocks ideally the system has the fewest parts that does the most things you want.

**Для Напарника:** Перед добавлением нового экрана проверять, нельзя ли решить задачу существующим примитивом (чат, награда, остров).

## 4. Локальное решение против примитива (пример с артефактами Claude)
`процесс · ИИ · игровой-дизайн` · [03:11](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=191)

**Мысль:** Кнопка «свернуть» решает локальную проблему, а новая абстракция вроде артефактов растёт и решает будущие задачи; дизайнер должен увидеть и отстоять примитив.

**Слова автора:**

> Best features are the ones that solve multiple problems at once that are simple abstractions that can grow into ones that solve future problems and so part of shaping strategy I think is proposing a solution that is not just let's take artifacts as an example because we just talked about it there is a user problem which is that it's hard to write documents with Claude because it takes up your entire screen you can't actually see claude's commentary Etc you could find 10 different solutions that would solve that problem some of those Solutions are the seeds of A New Concept a new abstraction a new thing that you can build on and I think it's designer jobs to see that and advocate for it so you know to paint stupid ends of the spectrum you could just have a collapse button that takes long documents and hides them in the chat that would be like a local solution to a problem but it is not a of compounding abstract concept that you can build off a primitive in the product.

**Для Напарника:** Остров как примитив прогресса может нести будущие механики (редкие находки, ориентиры), вместо отдельных счётчиков и значков.

## 5. Проектировать настройки как интероперабельные компоненты
`процесс · дизайн-система` · [05:11](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=311)

**Мысль:** Автор проектировал экран настроек, думая, что те же действия потом появятся в чек-листах онбординга и модалках, поэтому сделал переиспользуемую систему.

**Слова автора:**

> Zooming out to consider the full system is how you preserve Simplicity as your fature surface area grows an example for my time at Maven is when I was working on settings now I could have designed that set of screens in isolation but in the back of my head I knew that we would probably want to surface some of these actions in future onboarding checklists and maybe even in modals on different feature pages and so I thought through this interoperable component system that would allow us to easily showcase each configuration in ways that I hadn't even thought of yet.

## 6. Продавать идею через то, что она открывает для бизнеса
`процесс` · [08:21](https://youtu.be/MC5jAxh0ymA?t=501)

**Мысль:** Редизайн два года не проходил, пока автор не показал, что он открывает маркетинговые возможности; чем больше поверхностей затрагивает идея, тем больше людей заинтересовано.

**Слова автора:**

> But you know what's really effective when you can lead with the ROI of a feature and show how it unlocks new opportunities in the product for instance I wanted to redesign the syllabus editor at Maven for like two whole years and I failed multiple times to get buying my breakthrough though was showing how it could unlock new types of marketing opportunities for instructors like maybe allowing students to preview lessons on the landing page now not only is the team getting more bang for their Buck but the more surface area that your idea touches the more people have the potential to be excited about it because all of a sudden it's easier for them to see how it impacts their slice of the product.
