# What Makes a Game Cozy? ~ Design Doc

**Канал:** Design Doc · 11 мин · субтитры: en (ручные) · https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.


---

## 1. Ключи уюта: низкое давление, простота, персонажи, лёгкий тон
`игровой-дизайн · стыд` · [02:04](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=124)

**Мысль:** Без последствий за провал и без сложного выживания, простые стилизованные визуалы, симпатичные персонажи, лёгкий сюжет; можно вообще без сюжета.

**Слова автора:**

> I think the keys to coziness are low-pressure gameplay, without a lot of consequences for failing at a task or difficult survival elements, simple, stylistic visuals, fun characters, and a light story tone. You could even get away with basically no story if you want.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-лист тона Напарника: без последствий за пропуск, простые формы, милые персонажи, лёгкие реплики.

## 2. Нет заданного пути: ключевые предметы разбросаны в нескольких местах
`игровой-дизайн · СДВГ · мотивация` · [03:10](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=190)

**Мысль:** Цели можно достигать в любом порядке, а предмет найдется в нескольких местах, поэтому прогресс возникает в ходе того, что интересно человеку; ничто не закрыто.

**Слова автора:**

> As you go from place to place in the park, you’ll stumble into something new around almost every corner. You’ll find key items scattered throughout, unlocking new mechanics to use, but the game doesn’t limit you to only one place to find the items. These buds bloom and act as a trampoline to let you glide around easier, but the bucket you need to activate them can be found in a bunch of places throughout the mountain. Not just one. There’s a kid that needs a shovel to finish his sandcastle, but there are 5 different places where the game will let you find one. Scattering key items like that helps you complete tasks in the middle of doing whatever exploring you found most interesting. There is no set path up the hill and no set order in which you’ll meet people or complete tasks. It’s entirely at your own pace, and you can take or leave the elements that most interest you. You don’t have to complete everything either, there’s nothing really to gate you off from achieving your goals, save for one clearly marked place, and you just collect what you want to in whatever order feels right. Whatever you want to do is totally fine with the game.

**Для Напарника:** Дать несколько путей к одной цели на острове и в очереди шагов, чтобы начинать можно было с того, что тянет.

## 3. Падение без штрафа и с поддержкой
`стыд · игровой-дизайн · доверие` · [04:11](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=251)

**Мысль:** Нет наказания за промах, а у птицы есть планирование для выхода из ситуации; ошибка воспринимается как нормальная часть исследования.

**Слова автора:**

> Aw, you fell off a cliff while exploring? Well, that’s very good… for… a first… try. You keep at it! There’s no real penalty for missing a jump or gliding too far or falling off a cliff. You’re a bird, after all, so you can glide your way out of most things anyway.

**Для Напарника:** Срыв сессии или пропуск дня в Напарнике должен иметь «планер» в виде мягкого возврата и тёплой фразы кота.

## 4. Смотровые точки показывают, как далеко ты зашёл
`игровой-дизайн · цвет · мотивация` · [04:35](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=275)

**Мысль:** Дымка на среднем расстоянии и обесцвечивание далёкого дают глубину и намёк на то, куда идти дальше; на вершине видно пройденный путь.

**Слова автора:**

> The sense of depth that the game plays with is outstanding. From the game’s usual isometric angle, you don’t get a sense of it. You’re focused on the local area and what you can do, and where you can go. But every so often, you’ll reach one of these lookout points, and you can see just how far you’ve come. There’s a haze effect at medium range and a really smart desaturation and outline technique the game uses for things far off in the distance, hinting at something that might pique your interest. Maybe you should go over there, too?

**Для Напарника:** Мир/AppBackdrop: гряда на горизонте как дымчатая даль, а экран Мира как смотровая точка, показывающая пройденное.

## 5. Свой темп: задержаться или рвануть, оба варианта верны
`игровой-дизайн · СДВГ · мотивация` · [05:07](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=307)

**Мысль:** Игра принимает и медленное общение со всеми, и быстрый подъём на гору: это ваш отпуск.

**Слова автора:**

> Or not, whatever you’re feeling. The whole game might only take you an hour or two to complete, but that’s really up to you. If you want to linger around talking to everyone you find and helping them with their problems, be my guest. If you want to blitz up the mountain, have at it. It’s your getaway. Do whatever feels right to you.

**Для Напарника:** Позволять Напарнику вести и спокойные 10 минут, и 50 минут, без оценки темпа.

## 6. Уют и ясная цель совместимы
`игровой-дизайн · мотивация · звук` · [05:34](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=334)

**Мысль:** Grow Home имеет одну чёткую цель, но остаётся расслабленным: нет времени давления, нет боя, падение почти ничего не стоит, подбадривает бортовой компьютер, и можно останавливаться на видах.

**Слова автора:**

> Games tend to feel cozier when they have low consequences, but that doesn’t mean they have to be these squishy, aimless things. They can still feel cozy while being focused and having clear goals. Grow Home is a very simple physics-based platformer. You’re a clumsy little robot named BUD who has to jump, climb, and glide his way around a huge plant to get back to his mothership floating high above the planet. The plant is filled with these branching buds. If you grab one, you’ll ride it as it grows and you can sort of steer the plant towards other things, like these glowing landmasses that give energy to the plant. Along the way you’ll find and collect some crystals that give BUD upgrades that help you traverse the world easier. That’s about it. Grow Home has a very single-minded overarching goal, but stays cozy with its relaxing cycle of jumping, climbing, growing plants, and collecting as you get closer and closer to the mothership. It’s more single-minded than A Short Hike, but it’s still very relaxed and lets you go about that goal at your own pace. The game uses a calm, ambient soundtrack, a low-poly art style, the occasional soothing song, and the mothership’s computer cheering you on to keep it’s relaxed vibe. There’s no time pressure, no combat, and hardly any enemies to get in your way, save for the occasional creature. Even if you died to it, though, there’s not much consequence, or much consequence from falling off of the plant for that matter. You’ll just get teleported back to the nearest beacon. It’s just you, the plant, and a ton of crystals to collect as you climb your way back home. But as you go up, pause for a second every once in a while and take in a vista. You’ve earned it.

**Для Напарника:** Цель сессии может быть чёткой (один шаг), а окружение расслабленным; кот подбадривает, а не оценивает.

## 7. Уют не обязан быть сладким
`игровой-дизайн · вкус · игровой-дизайн` · [07:10](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=430)

**Мысль:** Untitled Goose Game уютна, хотя вы злодей: минимальный саундтрек, плоские цвета, возможность пробовать на NPC, почти нет шанса провала, легко вернуться.

**Слова автора:**

> A cozy game doesn’t have to be sugary sweet, either. A Short Hike and Animal Crossing are known for being as pleasant as possible all the way through, but you can still have a nice, low-key time being an absolute jerk. It worked for Dennis the Menace, why can’t it work for a goose? Untitled Goose Game is a cozy game where you play as the villain. You’re a goose working your way through a small neighborhood and going through a literal checklist of mayhem, ruining the day of everyone in your path. It sounds mean-spirited, and it is, but it’s still so cozy and endearing. The presentation and scope of the game do a lot of work here. It’s a puzzle-focused adventure game where you operate on the world by manipulating NPCs to complete tasks. It’s all about exploring the different ways you can affect the environment and mess around with the townsfolk in specific ways. Actually, it’s kinda like a cozy version of Hitman if you think about it. Honk at this kid over and over and you’ll scare him. Chase him around and you might trap him in the phonebooth. Or you could steal this gardener’s prized rose and while he’s distracted, you can snatch his hat and force him to wear THIS. There’s plenty to poke at in the environment and lots of things to experiment on to see what different behaviors everyone will have in different scenarios. Like A Short Hike, there’s basically no chance to fail and it’s easy to get right back to where you were if something goes weird. The minimalist piano soundtrack, flat colored art style and the premise all lend themselves to a delightful low-key experience of being an absolute nuisance.

**Для Напарника:** У кота может быть озорной характер, не обязательно только милый; важны низкие ставки и лёгкий возврат.

## 8. Эстетика не делает игру уютной: Night in the Woods
`игровой-дизайн · вкус` · [08:39](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=519)

**Мысль:** Игра выглядит уютной, но тяжёлые темы упадка и перемен выводят её из категории. Уют — результат согласованных факторов, а не только внешнего вида.

**Слова автора:**

> You might think you see a pattern here. Lots of cozy games seem to have fairly similar aesthetics, but be careful. Focusing on aesthetics to determine whether or not a game is cozy can trip you up. On the surface, Night in the Woods looks extremely cozy. Look at that art style. Animal characters. The dialogue is super casual and relatable. The music’s great. It’s not mechanically demanding. It’s just this cozy little adventure game where you go around your small town and do stuff, right? Oh. Oh no. Well yeah sort of. But no. Night in the Woods’ story I’d say takes it firmly out of the ‘cozy’ camp. You play as Mae, who’s coming home from college under… mixed circumstances. Your hometown is there, but nothing’s quite how you left it. There’s the natural change over time, especially in that post-high school way where all your friends drift apart a little bit to do their own thing. But the town’s not doing so hot, either. Work is hard to come by and the town is in this slow decay cycle. The people in it are a little more standoffish than you remember. It’s all sorta the same, but not… quite. You’re gonna meet back up with your best friends, catch up, and see if there’s something else going on. The game explores personal growth and change, urban decay, social and psychological trauma, and whether or not you CAN go home again. It’s not a HUGE downer, but these are some heavy themes that the game plays up.

**Для Напарника:** Не полагаться только на милую визуалку: тон текстов и механик должен соответствовать уютному обещанию.

## 9. Уют — продукт множества согласованных факторов
`игровой-дизайн · вкус` · [10:32](https://youtu.be/g4yT-uAUcSA?t=632)

**Мысль:** Уютная игра не только эстетика, она результат совместной работы множества факторов, направленных на расслабление.

**Слова автора:**

> Cozy games aren’t just about an aesthetic, they’re the product of lots of factors working together to help you relax.

**Для Напарника:** Рассматривать звук, цвет, тексты, механику и ритм Напарника как единую систему уюта.
