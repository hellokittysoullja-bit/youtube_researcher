## 1. Point of performance: подсказка ровно там, где срываешься
`СДВГ · процесс · внимание` · [00:25](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=25)

**Мысль:** Напоминания и инструменты нужно ставить в точку, где человек отвлекается или перегружается (чеклист на двери гаража).

**Слова автора:**

> This is where the concept of point of performance becomes invaluable point of performance means setting up reminders or tools in the exact spot where you're likely to get distracted or overwhelmed an example of this would be like posting a checklist on the garage door of things that you need to bring to work each day that ensures that you remember everything before leaving by using this technique you're giving yourself tools that help you stay focused exactly when distractions are most likely to occur.

**Для Напарника:** Подсказка Напарника в момент застревания внутри Фокуса, а не заранее в настройках.

## 2. Раздели и победи: пример тренировки из пяти микрошагов, каждый даёт чувство выполненного
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · награды · процесс` · [04:52](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=292)

**Мысль:** Каждый завершённый мелкий пункт даёт ощущение достижения и подпитывает мотивацию; отвлекающий пункт (плейлист) лучше решить заранее.

**Слова автора:**

> Strategy number three divide and conquer for those of us with ADHD breaking tasks down into smaller bite-sized steps is one of the most effective ways to stay motivated and productive instead of looking at a giant task that feels overwhelming divide and conquer it by turning it into a series of small achievable steps for example if you want to work out your list could look like number one I've got to find my headphones number two I got to put my workout clothes on number three I'll pick my playlist now this one could be tricky and we could get distracted so maybe pick that out ahead of time but number four put your shoes on number five start your workout as you finish each one of those items you'll feel a sense of accomplishment which helps keep you motivated.

**Для Напарника:** Пример-шаблон для breakdown (найти наушники, надеть форму, выбрать плейлист заранее) и маленькая награда за каждый шаг.

## 3. Будильник нельзя выключить, пока не начал: правило с собой
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · время` · [05:48](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=348)

**Мысль:** Назойливая немедленность будильника создаёт срочность для старта.

**Слова автора:**

> If you find you have trouble getting started on one task or a piece of a task you can also set alarms for things that you want to accomplish pick a time and set the alarm and then make a rule with yourself you cannot turn off that alarm until you've actually started the task the immediacy of that alarm also kind of the Annoying piece of it works with our ADHD brains giving us that little push of urgency that we sometimes need to get started.

**Для Напарника:** Не применимо в виде назойливого сигнала; можно мягкий вариант: напарник тихо зовёт, пока шаг не начат.

## 4. Почему body doubling работает: присутствие как якорь и тихий болельщик
`СДВГ · body-doubling · маскот` · [07:32](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=452)

**Мысль:** Присутствие другого создаёт подотчётность, внешнюю структуру, зеркалирование и ощущение команды, даже без общей задачи.

**Слова автора:**

> Why does this work so well for ADHD brains for starters ADHD often comes with issues around Focus accountability and motivation it can feel like we have a million tabs open in our heads and it's easy to get distracted overwhelmed or lost in procrastination when someone else is there even if they're not working on the same task just their presence can help anchor us to the task at hand it creates like a sense of accountability even if no one is directly monitoring what we're doing it's like having a silent cheerleader next to us reminding us to stay on task body doubling helps create structure many of us with ADHD Thrive when there's external structure and cues to help us Focus knowing that someone else is there creates a natural boundary reducing the temptation to drift off into distractions there's also this bonus of social pressure like in a positive way if someone else is working it can Inspire us to keep going too almost like we're mirroring their productivity body doubling Taps into the idea that we're more motivated when we feel like we're part of a team even if we're doing separate tasks this technique helps break through the isolation and difficulty in self-starting that can often come along with ADHD in short body doubling turns a solo task into something shared making it easier and often more fun to get things done.

**Для Напарника:** Главное обоснование кота у костра: молчаливый болельщик рядом; можно усилить мелкими реакциями, когда человек начал шаг.

## 5. Мостовые утверждения: от «я тупая» к «возможно, не самая тупая»
`СДВГ · стыд · тексты · ИИ` · [08:59](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=539)

**Мысль:** Заметить самокритику, проверить факты и постепенно смягчать формулировки шаг за шагом - реалистичнее резкой смены на позитив.

**Слова автора:**

> Strategy number six practice self-compassion this is one of the most important things you can do if you have ADHD and here's why many of us with ADHD struggle not just with Focus but with low self-esteem and deep feelings of Shame we've likely spent years hearing things like why are you so lazy or you're just not trying hard enough over time this can lead to a harsh inner voice that criticizes us every time we make a mistake or fall behind this self talk can be incredibly damaging to practice self-compassion I want you to do these three things notice when the negative selft talk is happening check your facts and use Bridge statements noticing when it's happening means that we pay more attention to the thoughts we're having about ourselves at this step you don't really have to take any action you're just turning your attention to the thoughts that you have regularly checking the facts means that we don't accept our thoughts at face value an example of this would be that instead of thinking I'm so lazy and accepting that as true I would look to see if I had any evidence to support it and remember thoughts are not facts utilizing Bridge statements means that we're slowly challenging our thoughts and moving them into a more positive place an example of using a bridge statement would be that instead of thinking GH I'm so stupid I would say it's possible that I might not be the dumbest person in the world see not as positive but none near nearly as negative and trust me when I tell you that you will feel that difference each Bridge statement we make should be a little less negative than the one before it's like we're building a bridge plank by plank towards a more positive place.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики напарника, когда человек ругает себя: не «ты молодец!», а мягкий мостик («возможно, сегодня просто тяжело»).

## 6. Самосострадание не снимает ответственность, а создаёт пространство для роста
`СДВГ · стыд · мотивация` · [10:41](https://youtu.be/Mx6YlI19bL0?t=641)

**Мысль:** Без груза вины быстрее возвращаешься в колею; доброта к себе повышает устойчивость.

**Слова автора:**

> Self-compassion isn't about letting yourself off the hook it's about being kind to yourself in the process when you stop shaming yourself for every small mistake you create the space to grow and improve without the weight of guilt this in turn boosts your resilience and helps you get back on track faster it's like learning to be your own biggest supporter rather than your harshest critic.

**Для Напарника:** Подтверждает принцип «без вины»: возврат после срыва должен быть тёплым и быстрым.
