# The Best Landing Page Structure for 2026

**Канал:** Arsh Sanwarwala | ThrillX · 17 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.


---

## 1. Для «неосведомлённой» аудитории сначала боль, потом «как это работает»
`лендинг · тексты · конверсия` · [02:35](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=155)

**Мысль:** Холодная аудитория из рекламы не ищет решение; логистика без картины, зачем это нужно, не цепляет. Нужно сначала показать и выстроить ощущение проблемы.

**Слова автора:**

> The very first section below the fold is a how it works section. The issue with this fundamentally is, you know, this client is predominantly running meta ads, right? So this audience tends to be a little bit more unaware, right? They're not actively searching for, you know, luxury closets. They're just coming across an ad on Facebook and then clicking on it. And so the issue with this approach is we're going straight into how it works and going into the logistics without really painting a picture of why the person should care in the first place and really educating and building that sense of pain within the customer.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг: перед «как это работает» блок узнавания («знакомо: сел за дело и пропал в телефоне»), без стыда.

## 2. Карусель отзывов смотрят менее 1% людей; текстовые отзывы приелись
`лендинг · доверие` · [03:19](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=199)

**Мысль:** Статистически менее 1% доходят до второго-пятого отзыва; текстовые отзывы стали стандартом, и люди к ним нечувствительны.

**Слова автора:**

> When you look at the testimonials here, one big issue is they're hiding the testimonials in a carousel. They're making you scroll through the testimonials one by one. And for this section underneath, right, there's five testimonials, but there's still arrows where you have to scroll through multiple testimonials in this carousel format. The big issue with this approach is carousels statistically have a very low engagement rate. Normally, it's less than 1%. Right? So less than 1% of people will actually go and see the second, the third, the fourth, the fifth testimonial, let alone engage with it in the first place. The other big issue with this is text testimonials are so overdone. Everyone does it these days. So it's kind of table stakes and people have become very desensitized to just text testimonials.

**Для Напарника:** Показывать отзывы сразу, не каруселью; добавлять визуальный контекст (скриншот, остров).

## 3. Скелет лендинга: первый экран, боль/доказательство по уровню осведомлённости, 5–8 выгод, как работает, FAQ, закрывающий блок
`лендинг · конверсия · процесс` · [04:13](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=253)

**Мысль:** Порядок зависит от осведомлённости: кто знает решение — доказательство раньше боли, кто нет или предложение сложное — сначала боль. Каждая выгода с картинкой или видео; в конце «закрывающий» блок.

**Слова автора:**

> This right here is what we typically follow for any given project. And this is basically the blueprint of how to create a high converting landing page. I'll put a link in the description for this below. So, make sure you grab that. So, at a high level, right, it all starts at the very top with a very action-packed above the fold where we have a benefit- driven headline, benefit driven sub headline. We typically have two to three different forms of social proof. So, really building a ton of trust and credibility up front. We try to reduce fear and uncertainty around buying which we call FUDs. And then we have an image or video or sometimes a form that typically helps to reinforce your core value proposition and articulate what it is you do. All of this is just the above the fold. Following that section, we have what we call the painoint section, which is basically where you're trying to explain what the old way is and kind of meet people where they are right now and then also tie in your own unique benefit, right? and what makes you actually unique. We'll either have this painoint section or we'll have the social proof section, right? It typically depends on the awareness level of your audience. So, if they tend to be more solution aware, like they know exactly what they're looking for, then we will actually put the social proof section before the pain proof section. But if they tend to be more unaware, right, or the offer is more complex, then we always typically lead with the painoint section first, followed by social proof afterwards. Then as we scroll down, right, these are what we call the value prop sections, right? So this is basically going to take up a huge portion of the page and is really kind of the meat of the page. And we typically have, you know, anywhere from five to eight value props for any given company. For each of these different value props, right, we have an image or video that helps to complement what we're actually talking about. We typically have a how it works section. So just showing people the process from step one to step three or step four and just showing them how easy it actually is. We have an optional about us section to meet the team and then we have more social proof FAQs right towards the end of the page and then what I call the closer section which is basically trying to seal the deal and give them that one final push to get them over the edge to convert if they're still not convinced.

**Для Напарника:** Структура лендинга Напарника: hero, узнавание боли, 5–6 выгод (кот, остров, мягкий старт), как работает, FAQ, финальный CTA.

## 4. Люди не хотят остроумно, они хотят ясно: прямой заголовок о том, что им важно
`тексты · лендинг · конверсия` · [09:33](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=573)

**Мысль:** Direct response: длинный конкретный заголовок о выгоде работает лучше короткого слогана-крючка.

**Слова автора:**

> One thing I just want to highlight here, a lot of people like that are not familiar with landing pages and copywriting will just look at this and say, "Okay, this looks way too long. This looks way too bulky. Like, who the heck is actually going to read all of this?" This is a strategy called direct response where you really want to be aggressive in your copywriting and particularly in your headlines where you're talking directly to what people care about. Yes, it's not pretty. Yes, it's not like the super shortest thing that's like a catch line or like a tag phrase or anything like that, but that's not what actually gets people to convert, right? People don't want clever. People want clear.

**Для Напарника:** На лендинге и в онбординге предпочитать ясные фразы красивым слоганам.

## 5. Длина блока боли зависит от сложности предложения и осведомлённости; приём PAS
`лендинг · тексты · конверсия` · [11:10](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=670)

**Мысль:** Для простого предложения блок боли короткий и тонкий; для нового подхода дольше: назвать проблему, усилить ощущение боли и дать решение как выход.

**Слова автора:**

> Length of this and the way that you execute this is going to depend on how complex your offer is. It's a little bit of intuition and gut feeling, which you just kind of get naturally with experience, but to kind of articulate it for you. So, for our luxury closet client, you can see over here it's relatively straightforward and kind of subtle where we just have one headline like get premium quality without paying marked up prices or waiting months cuz that's typically like the pain point with this target market. But then the body text over here is not super long or lengthy like it's just kind of very subtly explaining the pain point and then just showing a couple different use cases where people can have their closets. Now, if you compare that to our other client, which is a horse coaching program, they're also running meta ads. People are not super aware of like what their solution actually is, right? Because they're kind of challenging conventional norms of how people train their horses. And then we're showing people that there's actually a new and better way of doing that. Right at the very top, this is the above the fold. And then beneath the above the fold, we have this pain to proof section here. Right? So, this is a bit longer compared to the closet client. It's because the awareness level of the audience is very different and the offer is a bit more complex. Right? So we want to basically use what I call the problem agitate solve framework is basically where you kind of articulate what the problem is. Right? So dear horse owner are you scared every time you ride? Does your horse spook? Told to just write through it, right? You kind of show people, okay, I know what problem you're dealing with. Then you kind of agitate the problem, right? you really turn up the heat and make them feel the real pain that they're associated with that problem. Right? So like for this client, we're saying you're missing on trail rides with friends, avoiding competitions, and losing the joy of riding. And then we complement all of this with the actual solution, right? So the solution is all of this information here, right? We discovered a better way, a new way. You know, it's quick. You can do it without having to do all of these things that you traditionally do. And this is how you do it, right? Get instant access now. So it's it's really painting that picture and educating people.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг для людей, которые не думают, что у них «проблема с фокусом»: подробнее объяснить и показать выход, мягко.

## 6. Визуализируй доказательство: отзыв + фото результата, видео-отзывы, «стена любви» без карусели
`лендинг · доверие · мобильный` · [13:12](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=792)

**Мысль:** Совмещай текст отзывов с изображением результата; видео и текст вместе строят доверие; всё показывается сразу и на мобильном.

**Слова автора:**

> The main thing you want to do with your social proof is you want to help people visualize the proof, right? So help to basically visualize the transformation. So what I mean by this is for our closet client instead of just showing generic text reviews which again everyone is so desensitized to this like everyone just tries to put text reviews. We're actually combining the text reviews from Google, right? And putting an image of the closet that this client did for them, right? So, it's kind of killing two birds with one stone. We're showing the reviews, but we're also showing photos to help people visualize the review and visualize the impact. The final best way you can help people visualize the review is combining video testimonials with text testimonials. So for our laser eye surgery client, we combined, you know, video testimonials and kind of showed it in this wall, which I call the wall of love. Basically, we're showing a bunch of social proof up front and really, really building so much trust and credibility. The other big mistake that people make is, you know, putting reviews in carousels, right? So you can see on mobile, we don't have it in the carousel. We're showing all of it up front and we're making people consume all of this proof, right? We're forcing people to see all this proof, right? And on desktop as well, it's the same notion, right? All of the reviews are shown up front.

**Для Напарника:** Отзывы Напарника: цитата + скриншот острова/недели; стена коротких историй.

## 7. Ключевое возражение получает отдельную секцию; фича + выгода в одном заголовке
`тексты · лендинг · СДВГ` · [14:42](https://youtu.be/vwhR2M0SQtI?t=882)

**Мысль:** Главный страх покупателя (будет ли больно, долго ли) заслуживает своей секции; заголовок объединяет функцию и выгоду.

**Слова автора:**

> Painfree 10-minute treatment for a lifetime of clear vision. This was one of the more critical value props, which is the fact that it's painfree cuz a ton of people have anxiety around is it actually going to hurt? Is how fast is it going to be? I feel like it's going to take forever. So, this deserved its own separate section. Same thing with this one over here. Relax with safety first care and ongoing support for full peace of mind. But we had smaller value props that you know were not as critical. Like for example, no more low light halos. You know, only clinic in the UK offering rate tracing. See better than ever with superior 2012 vision and virtual eye modeling for precise and safe surgery. When we go back to the headlines, right? Fast and pain-free 10-minute treatment, right? That is the feature, but the benefit is a lifetime of clear vision. So, we're combining both into one.

**Для Напарника:** Выделить блок про главный страх Напарника («опять не получится / будет давить») и ответить на него тёплой формулировкой.
