## 1. Касание, а не курсор: элементы на телефоне нужно делать крупнее, а не мельче
`мобильный · доступность · крафт` · [01:05](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=65)

**Мысль:** Область пальца около 30×30 пикселей, поэтому при уменьшении экрана элементы должны быть больше, чтобы попадать без ошибок.

**Слова автора:**

> On an iPhone, that won't work because interaction happens through touch. That is why all interfaces on mobile devices are called touch interfaces. Your finger's interaction area can be around 30 by 30 pixels. As you can understand, that is a lot. That is the tip of your finger. Therefore, elements need to be enlarged so that we can hit them without mistakes. And it would seem that the iPhone has become smaller. Meaning, the display of our mobile device has become smaller. Elements should be made smaller so they all fit. But no, actually, it is the opposite; elements must be made larger so that we can interact with them.

**Для Напарника:** Проверить на 390px, что чипы, кнопки и иконки AppNav не уменьшены ради упаковки, особенно для людей с СДВГ, у которых моторная точность нестабильна.

## 3. Вес шрифта: bold при слабом контрасте, thin для лёгкости; тело Regular, заголовки Bold
`типографика · иерархия · внимание` · [05:03](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=303)

**Мысль:** Тонкий текст выглядит серым и почти невидимым, жирный яркий и заметный, хотя цвет тот же. Вес управляет вниманием.

**Слова автора:**

> Why do symbols even need different weights at all? It is needed for cases where, for example, you have insufficient contrast with your background. If you have insufficient contrast, you use Bold to make your characters slightly wider. If you have good contrast and want to give your font a different mood, you take and use Thin, because Thin, as you can see, is very slender, very light, low-contrast, and radiates a kind of airiness and calm. A certain lightness. Bold, on the contrary, is monumental, heavy, and complex. So, as you can see, the weight allows you to work with the visual hierarchy of your text, as well as convey additional moods, like the ones I just used. Which weight should you use for body text? The answer is simple . It is Regular. And then, depending on your situation, you can deviate, for example, by using Light or Medium. Of course, I do not recommend using Bold for body text. Bold, Extra Bold, Black, and Medium are mainly used for headings . Regular and Light are used for body text. There are also other weights, such as Regular Italic, Thin Italic, and Medium Italic. The suffix "Italic" means that this weight is available in a slanted style. As you can see, it is all very simple, but pay attention, be sure to pay attention, that the difference between Thin and Bold is huge. Even though I used the same color everywhere and did not use any transparency settings. I wrote everything the same way, I just changed the weight. But look, it seems like the Thin text is already gray and almost invisible. Bold, it is very bright, white, and very noticeable. With the help of this principle, the principle of visual hierarchy, you can control the attention of your users.

**Для Напарника:** Для основного текста реплик использовать Regular/Light, а заголовки и ключевые числа Medium или Bold; не использовать Thin на тёмном фоне.

## 4. 16px для мобильного тела текста, 18 для десктопа, 24 при слабом зрении
`типографика · мобильный · доступность` · [06:58](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=418)

**Мысль:** Телефон близко к глазам, поэтому 16px комфортны; для аудитории со слабым зрением основной текст увеличивают до 24px.

**Слова автора:**

> Here I have listed the most popular font sizes. The first is 16 pixels for mobile devices, so that it is very easy and comfortable to read. Why? Because a mobile device is literally right in front of your user, so the letters on it can be very small. 18 pixels is the font size for computers, or rather, for body text on your computers, because the display on a computer is very large , and the size of the elements is very large. It is very easy for us to perceive them. But sometimes there are cases where the target audience of your resource might have poor vision. Perhaps these are elderly people, or people with, perhaps, visual impairments. Then you need to make the letters larger, and in that case, you use 24 pixels for your body text. And then come the sizes you can use for subheadings. These are 48 pixels and 72 pixels for headings. Starting from 2018 , probably from the end of 2018, brutalism came into design. This is when everything becomes large, everything stands out, and everything is very strongly emphasized. And so, you can make your font sizes whatever you like, for example, something like this. 365 pixels, like the days in a year. Why not? You can use it, of course, if it fits your design and if it is comfortable to read. If your user understands why it is that way, if they like it, then use 360 pixels, 1,000 pixels, 200 pixels—it absolutely doesn't matter. But keep in mind that 16 pixels is for mobile devices, a minimum of 18 pixels for desktop devices,.

**Для Напарника:** Основной текст 16px и выше на Доме и Фокусе; рассмотреть опцию увеличенного шрифта.

## 5. Не используй auto для интерлиньяжа: 150% минимум, лучше 170–180%
`типографика · доступность` · [08:50](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=530)

**Мысль:** Большой интерлиньяж облегчает сканирование и запоминание, а auto слишком мал.

**Слова автора:**

> By default, Figma and Photoshop set it to auto, and then our text turns into this little mess that isn't particularly pleasant to read. To make it more pleasant to read, we increase the line spacing. As you can see, our text is starting to become much clearer. It is easy for us to read and easy for us to perceive. But honestly, I don't recommend using 150%; or rather, I recommend using that only as a minimum value, because it is better, of course, to use 170-180%, because at that line height value our text becomes more spaced out, making it easier for us to read, to scan specific sentences or read specific words, pick them out and remember them, which means it is easier to interact with the text. Therefore, I recommend that you use 170-180%, with 150%as a minimum. And never use auto, because it is too small a value.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики напарника и длинные тексты лендинга задавать с line-height около 1.6–1.7.

## 6. Интерлиньяж зависит от длины строки: длинная строка требует большего
`типографика · доступность · внимание` · [10:52](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=652)

**Мысль:** Если читатель теряет начало следующей строки, дизайнер совершил серьёзную ошибку. Длинные строки получают больший интервал, короткие можно сжимать.

**Слова автора:**

> There is a small rule for this. Line height depends on line length. Let's repeat it . Line height depends on line length. The shorter your text, the smaller the line height. The longer your line, the larger the line height. I think you've encountered a moment when you were reading a book and reached the end of a line. And after that, you were very careful, afraid of losing the thread of the narrative, carefully returning to the beginning of the line and jumping to the next one, or tracing it with your finger or using a ruler. So, if your user has to perform such manipulations, then as a designer, you have made a very big mistake that must not be made. Therefore, if the line length is very long, we increase the line spacing so that it is very easy for us to jump between lines. If the line is very short, we can compress our text to increase reading speed.

**Для Напарника:** Ограничить ширину читаемого текста (max-w) и подбирать интерлиньяж под неё, особенно на лендинге.

## 7. Трекинг: широкий только для заголовков, в основном тексте ±5% (максимум ±8%)
`типографика · иерархия` · [12:19](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=739)

**Мысль:** Раньше бумага стоила дорого, поэтому широкие надписи воспринимаются как более важные и дорогие. В тексте это мешает чтению.

**Слова автора:**

> By increasing it, we give it more importance because our text takes up more space. And as I told you earlier, or rather, as I just told you, people used to have to save space on paper to convey as much information as possible. And that means if a word or phrase takes up more space than it should, it means they are very important, because it was simply expensive to do that before. Therefore, such wide inscriptions seem more expensive and more important to us. Of course, I recommend using this technique only with headlines, perhaps with subheadings, for body text that the user will be reading. It is better not to do this trick, because if you space the letters out that much, reading will become very uncomfortable. There is a practical recommendation. This recommendation is to use plus or minus 5%for body text, or a maximum of plus or minus 8%. I think that is the interval you can afford. No more. Some people extend this interval up to 10%, but it all depends on your font. I, of course, recommend up to plus or minus five. And in the most difficult cases, plus or minus 8%. And set larger distances between letters only in headlines to give them more significance or importance.

**Для Напарника:** Разрядку применять только к маленьким заглавным лейблам и заголовкам, а основной текст оставить с нулевым трекингом.

## 8. F-паттерн: заголовки с цифрами, разделители и короткие строки ведут чтение
`иерархия · типографика · внимание` · [14:29](https://youtu.be/GOV4FVwka9c?t=869)

**Мысль:** Люди сканируют, а не читают, и цифры привлекают внимание. Хорошие заголовки позволяют решить, читать ли дальше, и формируют ощущение структурированности продукта даже у ушедшего пользователя.

**Слова автора:**

> Our task as designers is to lead the user to the F-pattern of reading. And for that, we need to work with the text. For example , add headings, add a divider line like this, add our text, increase the letter spacing, work on the line length for comfortable reading, add numbers. to each heading. Why add numbers? There is research confirming that people very often pay attention to numbers. Perhaps it's because all prices have been indicated in numbers for thousands of years, so a person instinctively pays more attention to them, because money is one of the main resources a person has. Well, of course, there are more important ones, for example, time, which is an irreplaceable resource. But money is also a very important resource that allows you to acquire, or rather, buy or obtain other resources. But compared to time, of course, money is a replaceable resource. Anyway, we’ve veered off track. The most important thing I wanted to tell you is that numbers attract attention. As you can see, I made the numbers and headings in a different font, a bolder one, that forces, that attracts attention and compels them to read it and pay attention to it. And the text itself is very neat and well-formatted. Here is our entire text broken down by headings . It must be said, if your headings are well-written, the user, by reading only the headings, will already understand what the text is about and be able to decide whether they need to read it all or not. Thus, we arrive at the F-pattern of reading your text. As you can see, we first read, perhaps, the number, catching it with our gaze. Then we read the heading. If it caught our interest, if it’s written correctly and conveys the essence of the following paragraph, we start reading the first sentence. If a paragraph interests us, we can read it in its entirety. Then we interact with it in a similar way again, but perhaps it won't interest us here, and we will move on to the next paragraph, or rather, to the next heading. And thus, our reading pattern begins to resemble the Latin letter F. It must be said that even if you have done all this and the user still did not want to read your text, that is very good, because it seems your text's topic does not meet the user's request. Even though they decided to leave, it is still good because they will remember that on this resource or in this application, first of all, I can filter information very quickly. That is, I can jump to the information that I need. It is well-structured here, and it is very clear. If I am not interested in something, I do not waste time on it, but if something is of interest, I will find it very quickly.

**Для Напарника:** В лендинге и длинных ответах напарника делать заголовки, по которым понятна суть, и не прятать главное в абзац.
