## 1. Calm tech 1–3: минимум внимания, тихая обратная связь, периферия
`внимание · уведомления · звук` · [04:06](https://youtu.be/2ngmtV3_TxI?t=246)

**Мысль:** Индикаторы статуса (света, мягкая вибрация) информируют без прерывания; люди умеют периферийное внимание, как со светофором.

**Слова автора:**

> The first principle holds that technology should require the least possible amount of attention today's users often interact with multiple screens and apps simultaneously so technology should communicate without interrupting indicators such as status lights that convey power or connectivity allow users to remain productive while staying updated the second principle is that technology should inform and create Comm technology uses understated quiet mechanisms to inform users of their status for example a ride sharing app might vibrate when your driver is nearby eliminating the need for constant checking anxiety inducing notifications should be avoided unless necessary third technology should make use of per periphery attention people are capable of both direct and peripheral attention the latter doesn't compromise our ability to focus but Keeps Us aware of our surroundings technology designed to accommodate peripheral attention is more likely to achieve a seamless user experience for example traffic lights inform drivers of their status without creating disruption.

**Для Напарника:** Статус в Напарнике — через живой фон и позу кота (периферия), а не всплывающие сообщения.

## 2. Calm tech 4–8: дополнять человека, не говорить, работать при сбое, минимум
`этика · доступность · крафт` · [05:13](https://youtu.be/2ngmtV3_TxI?t=313)

**Мысль:** Технология дополняет, а не заменяет; даёт резерв при отказе; решает проблему минимумом средств; уважает социальные нормы.

**Слова автора:**

> Fourth technology should amplify the best of both technology and Humanity the goal of well-designed technology should be to complement not replace human abilities computers must also understand context to facilitate communication effective technology uses human emotional cues to improve user experience fifth technology can communicate but it shouldn't need to speak technology must communicate with users to achieve its goal but it should engage only peripheral attention voice activated assistants often require a user's full attention making them a problematic area for Cal technology designers should use human senses effectively such as sight sound or touch to minimize intrusion sixth technology should work even when it fails heavily engineered passenger Jets can glide even if their engines fail similarly smaller technology must provide backup options to mitigate disappointment when it fails for example users should be able to silence a smoke detect even if it's difficult to turn off seventh the right amount of technology is the minimum required to solve the problem technology should simplify solutions to improve Effectiveness and minimize complexity this allows technology to disappear into our environment and become unobtrusive finally technology should respect social norms users expect technology to make their lives easier and not disrupt their routines designers should create products that enhance existing social norms rather than displace them.

**Для Напарника:** Когда ИИ недоступен, кот остаётся полезным по заготовкам; не плодить экранов и функций сверх нужного.

## 3. Каналы: цвет для периферии, тихий звук, хаптика для отдельного сигнала
`хаптика · звук · цвет` · [07:28](https://youtu.be/2ngmtV3_TxI?t=448)

**Мысль:** Цветовые индикаторы хорошо работают с периферийным вниманием; звук должен быть тихим и успокаивающим; хаптика сообщает безопасно.

**Слова автора:**

> Channels can interact with people in context and enhance their experiences in Cal technology designers use awareness sense and cognition to respect human communication patterns and avoid high resolution disruptions C technology focuses on minimizing intrusions and leveraging ambient awareness to enrich interactions visual communication through color indicators can work well with users peripheral attention sound communication when necessary should be unobtrusive and soothing haptic communication through various touch Sensations can convey information in a safe way and reduce potential distraction.

**Для Напарника:** Небо по часу суток и очаг как цветовой канал; хаптика на события, звук тихий.
