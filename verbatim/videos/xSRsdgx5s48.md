## 1. Иерархия задаёт порядок чтения и позволяет сканировать (заголовок, подзаголовки, текст)
`иерархия · внимание · типографика` · [00:28](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=28)

**Мысль:** Иерархия говорит, куда смотреть и что важно; дизайнер может предсказать, что прочтут первым; заголовок - самый большой, по нему решают, читать ли дальше, подзаголовки дают ключевые мысли.

**Слова автора:**

> So a visual hierarchy is an important element in any design project it's going to tell where to look and what things on the screen of printed page are most important hierarchy gives readers a sense of how to actually read the material from start to finish with visual cues and flow type hierarchy organizes and gives order to the text elements in your design just as web designers and web developers use something called header tags which are like h1 h2 and h3 and so on to organize the importance of the text visual hierarchy uses visual cues in addition type hierarchy helps reader scan text reading bits of type faster in chunks that look alike generally english-language readers start at the top left and read across and down type is often organized to mirror this behavior but what if the biggest and boldest text is midway down the page well then a reader will start there and go back to the top of the page and continue the normal reading behavior hierarchy is important because it allows the designer to determine what someone will likely read first what they will read second and so on because of this the designer can create type in such a way that he or she knows what information is likely to be received and in what order as creating hierarchy is important to designers it is equally important to readers good visual hierarchy tells us what is important making reading much easier for example you know that the headline is the most important part of a story it's its attention-grabbing because it is the biggest followed by sub heads then body text for scalability purpose you could read the big text to get an idea if the article would interest you before investing a bunch of time and the copy and then the sub heads lets you figure out you know some key points within that text and then if you decide at that point to read it then you can read the text.

**Для Напарника:** Три уровня считывания на каждом экране Напарника: сначала главное (что делать сейчас), потом детали.

## 2. Иерархия из размера, веса, цвета, текстуры, начертания, ориентации и пространства; размер должен заметно различаться
`иерархия · типографика · крафт` · [03:10](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=190)

**Мысль:** Читатели видят самое крупное первым, но разница размеров должна быть значимой, а разные шрифты масштабируются по-разному.

**Слова автора:**

> No one-step solution to creating visual hierarchy with text it can be created with size weight color texture typeface choices and combinations orientation and space were a combination of all of that we will start with size one of the simplest ways to establish hierarchy with type is through size readers will often see the biggest type first and start reading from there in relation to size scale is equally important to create distinctive hierarchy sizes must have significant variance in relationship to other type on the page the thing to keep in mind is that different typefaces may scale differently so just changing the point size may not be enough you need to look at how typefaces work together to ensure the size and scale are properly balanced.

**Для Напарника:** Контролировать, чтобы ступени размеров заголовок/текст были заметными, а не 2 px разницы.

## 3. Размеры от основного текста в процентах: заголовок +250%, подзаголовок +175%, меню +140%
`типографика · иерархия · дизайн-система` · [04:19](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=259)

**Мысль:** Строить шкалу от базового текста в процентах, а не в абсолютных значениях; (цифры для навигации в расшифровке искажены).

**Слова автора:**

> Well you start with the main body text and go from there and for the text elements that are used to really draw people in go big or go home here are a few percentages that can help you get started with main body being somewhere around you know 11 points the main headers are supposed to be about two hundred and fifty percent bigger secondary headers 175 percent bigger navigation elements six hundred and sixty-five percent bigger secondary navigation or menus is a hundred and forty percent bigger so of course those are percentages because we're not basing it on a particular font size because we know that body copy is supposed to be around 11 or 10 points.

**Для Напарника:** Использовать как ориентир для множителей шкалы заголовков относительно базового размера.

## 4. Пары шрифтов: контраст по весу при одном тоне и x-height; толщина создаёт иерархию
`типографика · иерархия · вкус` · [05:31](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=331)

**Мысль:** Гармоничная пара - разные по весу, но близкие по характеру и высоте строчных; жирный выглядит важнее и крупнее, тонкий - меньше.

**Слова автора:**

> Typefaces are the most used element in terms of creating typographic hierarchy the key is to have contrasts between typefaces this is one reason that designers often select one serif and one sans-serif typeface for a project for the best combinations look for typefaces that are different in weight but have a similar tone or feel if you were using rounded typefaces stick to letter forms with similar shapes in the O's for example opt for typefaces with similar X Heights if the style will be used in line with one or another throughout the entire project and we have talked about this in the past where like palatino and Helvetica or a good combination palutena are etiquette and Garamond are a good combination okay because the letters kind of match the x-height it has a good feel but it has stark contrast in what you're trying to get across wait the thickness of strokes in the type you select for a project can create hierarchy as well the thicker the typeface the more bold and bigger it appears light condensed and thin typefaces often appear smaller than they actually are bold ultra and hammerhead typefaces can appear larger than the point size may represent how large or small a typeface appears by weight is also relative to the other typefaces used in the project so pairing thick and thin typefaces immediately causes a sense of hierarchy with the thick style carrying the look of more important often even though it is actually smaller on the display.

**Для Напарника:** Подбирать второй шрифт или начертание с похожим x-height; вес использовать как инструмент вместо увеличения размера.

**Слова автора:**

> Also add emphasis and increase weight to type think about some of the color rules you learned as a child warm colors the Reds the yellows the Browns will have more pop while cool colors like blues purples and grays will fade to the back warm to the front cold to the back when using color to establish hierarchy consider the hues used for the foreground type and background color contrast will also play an important role the more saturated or brightest colors will often lift off the screen against more muted tones.

**Для Напарника:** Акцент на главную кнопку Фокуса тёплым насыщенным цветом на холодно-приглушённом фоне.

## 6. Пространство как инструмент иерархии: группируй близко, крупному - больше воздуха
`отступы · иерархия · типографика` · [09:39](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=579)

**Мысль:** Воздух делает текст крупнее и читаемее; похожие элементы держат ближе, чем чужие; жирному, большому нужно больше места.

**Слова автора:**

> White space can make type appear bigger and more readable lack of white space can make it feel tighter and smaller use space to your advantage when creating a sense of hierarchy think about the space you use between lines of text think about kerning for the biggest type styles think about the spatial relationship between letters and the edge of the canvas think about the relationships between type of different sizes styles and colors every single space can impact your hierarchical scale keep similar-type grouped closer together and with less space than unrelated items and design your space or the text falls in a distinct order bigger bolder brighter text aalam elements often need more space than smaller simpler text blocks.

**Для Напарника:** Крупные элементы Дома (reward-карточка) окружить большим воздухом; близкие элементы - ближе друг к другу.

## 7. Три уровня типографики: первичный (вход), вторичный (подписи), основной текст обязан читаться
`иерархия · типографика · доступность` · [11:52](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=712)

**Мысль:** Достаточно трёх уровней: крупный привлекает, вторичный - подзаголовки и навигация, тело текста главное - чтобы читалось.

**Слова автора:**

> Most designs can be successful with three levels of typographic high ography ID hierarchy vanilla those levels are primary type secondary type tertiary type okay primary type is often the typography on the page with the most visual way such as main headers or display quotes the purpose of primary type is to bring readers into the overall design secondary type is everything else that is not the main content this can include captions sub heads and navigational or static type elements tertiary type is the main body copy there is one thing to remember about tertiary copy it must be readable little else matters when it comes to the design.

**Для Напарника:** Не плодить больше трёх уровней текста на экране Фокуса.

## 8. Первичный крючок - вторичный - третичный: что видно за секунду, затем детали
`иерархия · внимание · онбординг` · [14:29](https://youtu.be/xSRsdgx5s48?t=869)

**Мысль:** Композиция: что я вижу первым, следующим, и последнее, что возвращает к идее; сначала привлёк внимание, потом объясняешь.

**Слова автора:**

> A composition has to have a primary draw a secondary concept and a tertiary concept what am I supposed to look at first what am I supposed to see next and what is the final thing I'm supposed to see it's supposed to hook me all the way back in to the original concept when we look at posters what makes us see the poster on the board then what draws us into once we're at the toaster what's the one thing that we can see in eight seconds or not even eight seconds just in an instant okay that's the next most important thing exactly when and where it is those are tertiary concepts you got our attention you gave us something to do now tell us a little bit more about it.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг: за секунду - кот и остров с одной фразой, дальше объяснение механики.
