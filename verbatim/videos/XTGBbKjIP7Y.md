## 1. Показать боль наглядно: распечатанные сайты на стене убедили босса
`процесс · метрики · исследования` · [02:31](https://youtu.be/XTGBbKjIP7Y?t=151)

**Мысль:** Менеджер распечатал сотни сайтов на досках и показал руководителю, сколько денег тратится зря, и вышел с бюджетом.

**Слова автора:**

> Adventist Church to help them build a design system the web manager there Brent he did this great exercise that I love referencing he what he did was he printed out every website that they made on these little 3x3 in cards and every website that they made that year and he printed out they did a couple hundred and then he posted he pasted those onto mounting boards black mounting boards and then he brought them into his boss's office there's about six or seven mounting boards and he just showed them and he goes this is how much money we're wasting and what he was doing was really just kind of showing like visually literally showing the pain as like we could be doing this a lot more efficiently we're wasting a lot of money and this is why I need a budget to do a design system and he walked out of that room with a budget.

**Для Напарника:** Принцип для Мира: не рассказывать о прогрессе, а показывать его видимо (остров, гряда ориентиров), эффект Пик-Энд.

## 2. Jobs to be done: никто не хочет дрель, все хотят дыру в стене
`процесс · мотивация · лендинг` · [07:07](https://youtu.be/XTGBbKjIP7Y?t=427)

**Мысль:** Люди покупают не инструмент, а результат (картину на стене). Продавать надо не систему, а то, что она позволяет получить.

**Слова автора:**

> The common stories about the let's say the jobs to be done framework is that is that when people are trying to buy a drill nobody wants a drill nobody wants a quarter inch drill they want a quarter inch hole right and you take that even further nobody wants a quarter inch hole they want a picture hung on the wall you know and I think that often times we forget and the industry forgets because Design Systems are becoming more popular and more prevalent we forget that no one needs a design system they need the thing the design system makes it'd be like walking in and trying to sell someone on the virtues of Sublime Text Editor and it's like well no one cares about Sublime Text Editor you know developers do but really people want what you can the code that you write and the thing that you make.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг и онбординг Напарника: говорить не про «таймер фокуса», а про результат: начать, довести шаг, почувствовать себя спокойнее.
