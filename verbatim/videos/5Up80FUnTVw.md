## 3. Близость: расстояние создаёт группы (заголовок к тексту, поле к подписи)
`отступы · иерархия · сетка` · [03:36](https://youtu.be/5Up80FUnTVw?t=216)

**Мысль:** Чем ближе элементы, тем сильнее они воспринимаются как группа; отсутствие близости заголовка и подписи к полю превращает 2 минуты в 10.

**Слова автора:**

> Let's take a step back and understand how do we even create groups how do we make things look like they are associated to each other well it's all about spacing the closer you place items the more they are tend to be perceived as a group now for instance let's look at the marker example again even if these two markers look very similar and these two markers look different from this group of markers now the way I place them changes everything now you see how two of these tend to look like a group whereas two of these tend to look like a group The closer the things are the more they are tend to be in a group whereas now if I separate them away and make these two in a group you will see suddenly the group has changed pacing pauses or gaps changes everything now let's look at some great examples in our UI when you look at a heading and when you look at a body content if they are further away from each other do you think you can perceive them as one group where do you think this heading belongs where do you think this text belong do you think it belongs to the group above it or do you think it belongs to the content below it hmm that's difficult right but let's say we fix the space station let's say we bring it closer to the contents that are below now suddenly they look like a group how close you place items to each other are tend to be perceived as a group for instance if I'm designing a form if the input Fields versus the titles are not placed close to each other this can be the exact difference between does it take me two minutes to fill a form or does it take me 10 minutes to fill a form now in this competitive world we do not want our users to spend so much time on small tasks because what is stopping them from going to our competitors so let's save their time as much as we can.

**Для Напарника:** Подписи и заголовки прижимать к своему содержимому; отдельные блоки разводить отступом (как разделитель между переключателями на Фокусе).
