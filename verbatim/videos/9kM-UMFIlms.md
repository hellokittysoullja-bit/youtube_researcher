# 5 Best Practices for Better SaaS User Onboarding

## 1. Каждое слово онбординга говорит о желаниях и тревогах, а не о функциях
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · онбординг` · [00:55](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=55)

**Мысль:** Тексты онбординга часто описывают фичи, но мотивирует показ того, как продукт улучшит жизнь, успокоит тревоги и напомнит, что привычки можно преодолеть.

**Слова автора:**

> First is to speak to your user's desires. Often onboarding teams approach the content of cyber screens and onboarding elements like tool tips and prouduct tours as low priority. And it shows. Even if it's well-written it's usually focused on product features rather than communicating the value of those features. And this is a huge mistake. The ultimate motivation really is to show users how the product can help improve their lives. Every word in that entire user onboarding experience is an opportunity to speak to users needs and desires. You need to use content to amplify the solution to their current pain points, calm their anxieties and remind them that they can overcome their existing habits.

**Для Напарника:** Тексты кота говорят про состояние человека («можно начать с минуты»), а не про механику приложения.

## 2. Прогресс и эффект Зейгарник: незавершённое тянет закончить
`геймификация · мотивация · привычки` · [03:22](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=202)

**Мысль:** Люди устроены ставить цели и приятно их достигают; чек-лист или прогресс-бар, выглядящие незавершёнными, создают внутреннее напряжение, побуждающее закончить.

**Слова автора:**

> Now progress indicators work so well because humans are wired to set goals. And we feel inherently good when we accomplish them. It does help that when you finish a complex task your brain releases massive quantities of endorphins and you just get happy. There's also an internal tension that occurs when a checklist or progress bar appears incomplete. This is called the Zeigarnik Effect. When people feel the need to finish and complete a task. This is a massive win for user onboarding. Simply framing to do items or assign a process as incomplete can be a huge win.

**Для Напарника:** Совпадает с очередью шагов (§4). Следить, чтобы незавершённое подавалось как «следующий шаг», а не как долг.

## 3. Приветствие новичка создаёт связь и задаёт тон отношений
`онбординг · доверие · персонаж` · [03:59](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=239)

**Мысль:** Мы чаще соглашаемся с теми, кто похож на нас, делает комплименты и сотрудничает; без приветствия человек чувствует себя как гость, которого не встретил хозяин.

**Слова автора:**

> Third is welcome new users. Usually we're more likely to say yes to requests from people we like and are attracted to, whether they're our closest friends or strangers. What exactly causes attraction? (indistinct) social science tells us that there are three important factors. We like people who are similar to us, who pay us compliments and people who cooperate with us to obtain mutual goals. And one way to harness this powerful principle in the user onboarding is to welcome new users. Many believe that this introduction is a massive waste of time. But if you create a common bond, build a connection, relate to a shared mission, it can be an enormous boost of motivation for new users. It doesn't need to be anything fancy at all. We had a short video from Three Founders. User lists creates a bond with users thanks to the personal message. Now, many products ignore this critical step. But imagine walking into a dinner party without the host greeting you at all and giving you a tour. Most likely you feel snubbed and hurt. And welcome messages also set the tone. They give customers a sense of how they'll be treated during their relationship with the product.

**Для Напарника:** Первый вход: кот встречает человека короткой личной репликой, задавая тон «я рядом, без оценок».

## 4. Продуктовый тур как пластырь для плохого UX: вредит импульсу
`онбординг · процесс · доверие` · [07:28](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=448)

**Мысль:** Тур, объясняющий кнопки без «зачем», прерывает моментум и часто признак отсутствия стратегии онбординга.

**Слова автора:**

> Now, I have to warn you, be careful using product bumpers as a band-aid for bad user experience. One of my biggest pet peeves of all time with product tours is that it's often used to point out all the bells and whistles of an app. They tell you what the button or feature does. Click here to do X, and not explain why they are important to helping users achieve their desired outcome. When they're added on as an afterthought, product tours can do more damage by disrupting the momentum for users rather than getting them excited to use a product. Regrettably, they have become deeply associated with user onboarding to the point that many companies believe that the way to improve onboarding is to just add a product tour. This is flat out wrong. Ironically, it's often a sign that someone just slapped on onboarding experience without much talk or strategy.

**Для Напарника:** Не делать обучающий тур по экранам; вести к первому действию и пояснять по ходу.

## 5. Пустое состояние должно показывать будущую пользу и вести к действию
`онбординг · тексты · дизайн-система` · [08:24](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=504)

**Мысль:** Пустые страницы с нулями и заглушками «разбивают сердце»; нужно нарисовать, как это будет выглядеть при использовании, и дать явный основной призыв.

**Слова автора:**

> Number five is, show a helpful empty state. When users are just starting out, they'll often see pages within a product without any activity, history or data because it's their first time interacting with it. These moments are called empty states. Empty States are often overlooked as a helpful way to guide users to achieving their desired outcome. This happens because interfaces are typically designed with data already in place. So, the layout looks clean and organized. So when you used to sign up for the first time it can be heartbreaking to see a bunch of zeros and placeholder images on the main page, which is what you see when you sign up in the beginning for MailChimp. They've since changed this. Instead, you want to paint a picture of what it will look like once a user is actively using a product. Emphasize the value of taking action. Go beyond showing users the benefit of your app. Direct them to the desired action as well.

**Для Напарника:** Пустой Дом и пустой остров: тёплый текст («плана нет, и это ноль, а не минус») плюс одна кнопка первого шага; ориентиры-заглушки не рисовать.

## 6. Не маскируй пустоту фейковыми данными: это путает
`онбординг · доверие · этика` · [09:31](https://youtu.be/9kM-UMFIlms?t=571)

**Мысль:** Выдуманная активность вызывает вопросы «откуда это», «что тут делать»; фейковые данные допустимы только если они активно обучают (Basecamp).

**Слова автора:**

> A word of warning here, though, avoid using dummy data to generate faked activity and statistics into empty areas. (indistinct) attempting to cover empty states with fake data to bring the dashboard to life. It presents an entirely new problem of over warming users. You're opening up the door to questions such as, am I supposed to do something here, or do I just look at this? Where did this data come from? Where do I put in my data? How do I know it's my stuff that I'm looking at? There are times when dummy data can work. When it actively instructs instead of merely being seen. Basecamp does an excellent job of doing this, to explain how the product works. Each piece actively guides you through using the product.

**Для Напарника:** Не показывать выдуманные прошлые сессии или острова для «оживления»; пустота должна быть честной.
