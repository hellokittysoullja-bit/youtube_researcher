## 1. ИИ убирает чистый лист: реагировать на черновик легче, чем создавать с нуля
`ИИ · СДВГ · запуск-задачи · чат` · [02:46](https://youtu.be/ZssxnYgc2wI?t=166)

**Мысль:** Старт из пустоты парализует; полу-мысль, возвращённая ИИ в виде варианта, даёт за что зацепиться и что поправить.

**Слова автора:**

> The single hardest thing for an ADHD brain to do is usually starting something from nothing, a blank page. It's just intimidating. For a lot of us, it's genuinely paralyzing, and there's no edge to grab onto, no momentum to borrow. It's just white space and the sound of your own brain refusing to capitulate. Or, there's white space, and you fill it up for 12 hours, and then you drop it. So, what does AI do? And this sounds almost too simple. It eliminates that blank page. I don't have to generate from nothing anymore. I give Claude a messy half thought, and it gives me something back. I'm really good at generating messy half thoughts. And it's something that I can react to, something I can push back on, something I can say no, not like that, more like this, and I'm I'm reacting to something. That my brain can do all day.

**Для Напарника:** Чат напарника отвечает на сырую мысль готовым первым шагом, который можно отклонить («не так, а вот так»), а не просит сформулировать задачу идеально.

## 2. Переключение контекста: выгрузи состояние перед паузой, верни себя за 30 секунд
`ИИ · СДВГ · внимание · чат` · [04:04](https://youtu.be/ZssxnYgc2wI?t=244)

**Мысль:** После перерыва теряется не работа, а ментальное состояние; запись «где я, что дальше» в диалог позволяет мгновенно вернуться.

**Слова автора:**

> Here's one that I don't hear people talk about enough, context switching. If you have ADHD, you know what I mean. You're working on something, you get interrupted by my kid, a notification, a completely unrelated thought that suddenly feels very urgent. And when you come back to what you were doing, it's just gone. Not the work. The work is still there, but the mental state you were in to do the work. I mean, it's not uncommon for me to drive all the way to a store to pick one thing up, get to the store, and completely forget the thing that I just went to the store for. That is genuinely the way my brain can dysfunction, but it also happens at work. And what I do now, and this has genuinely changed how I work, is I keep a running Claude conversation for anything I'm working on over multiple sessions. Before I stop, I dump everything into it, where I am, what I was thinking, what the next problem is, what I was about to do before I got interrupted. And when I come back, I can simply ask Claude to catch me back up to where we left off, even if it's 2 days later, even if it's a month later. Where were we? 30 seconds later, I'm back.

**Для Напарника:** Перед выходом из Фокуса напарник спрашивает «на чём остановились?» и при возвращении кратко напоминает: «ты остановилась на…».

## 3. Паралич решений: ИИ даёт черновик, человек правит
`ИИ · СДВГ · запуск-задачи · чат` · [05:20](https://youtu.be/ZssxnYgc2wI?t=320)

**Мысль:** Десять решений до старта выключают мозг; готовый вариант, который можно поменять, сокращает путь с 45 минут до 30 секунд.

**Слова автора:**

> Another thing, ADHD and decision paralysis are best friends. Too many options, brain shuts down. Or, too many options, brain goes into open-ended questions with no constraint, brain shuts down. Task that requires 10 decisions before you can even start the actual task, brain shuts down. I used to do this thing where I'd sit down to write an email, like a simple professional email that should take 30 seconds to respond to, and I'd spend 45 minutes staring at it because I couldn't figure out the right tone, the right length, correct punctuation, the right way to sign off. I don't know how to say this thing. How can I even start an email, a Slack message, a way to respond to just say thank you where thank you would have been sufficient, but then I'm going to go over 47 options about how I should actually write this really simple response. Now, I can just ask Claude to read it over if I'm worried that it's going to come across the right way, or I can ask it to draft up an email that I can change to my personal brand, and then ask if it's okay. 30 seconds, I have a draft. Change some words, change the way that it sounds a little bit, ask Claude if it's still okay for the context that I'm in. Boom, email sent.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник предлагает один вариант первого шага и тёплый выбор «так / по-другому», вместо открытых вопросов.

## 4. Ночные мысли: принимай выгрузку в любое время, без суда и объяснений
`ИИ · СДВГ · доверие · чат · стыд` · [06:35](https://youtu.be/ZssxnYgc2wI?t=395)

**Мысль:** Ясные мысли приходят в неудобное время и испаряются; безоценочный приёмник даёт место, куда вернуться.

**Слова автора:**

> You know how your brain works really well at completely inconvenient times, like 2:00 a.m. or the right before you're supposed to be somewhere, in the shower maybe, when you have no way to write anything down? For years, I would have these moments of clarity where I'd figure out all of the world's problems that I'd been stuck on for a week. And by the time I sat down at my desk the next morning, they were just gone, evaporated into the ether. And now I have a standing instruction in my Claude setup. If I come in with a brain dump at any hour, just receive it, organize it, and hold it for me. No judgment. It's just an AI, so there's no judgment. no need to explain, no need to push back, just catch what I throw.

**Для Напарника:** Быстрое «выгрузить мысль» в чате в любой час; напарник принимает, раскладывает и хранит без комментариев.

## 5. ИИ не лечит СДВГ, он снижает трение в точках, где мозг ломается
`ИИ · СДВГ · этика · доверие` · [07:19](https://youtu.be/ZssxnYgc2wI?t=439)

**Мысль:** Честная рамка: стоимость потери контекста, старта и избытка решений падает, но мозг не перепрошивается.

**Слова автора:**

> I want to be honest about something though. AI doesn't fix ADHD. I still lose things. I still hyperfocus on the wrong time. I still sometimes spend 2 hours on the part of a project that needed 20 minutes or 30 seconds, and avoid the parts that need 2 hours. I have 47 different novel ideas in Claude right now because, you know, ADHD things. What it changed is the cost of those things. When I lose context, I can get back faster. When I can't start, I have something to react to. When I have too many decisions or too much research to do, I have something to narrow them down in time. It didn't rewire my brain. It just reduced the friction at exactly the points where my brain creates the most.

**Для Напарника:** Честная формулировка в тексте продукта: «Напарник не лечит, он снижает трение»; не обещать излечения.
