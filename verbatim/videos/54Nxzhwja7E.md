## 1. Рой пчёл: собрать все возможные поведения, представив волшебную палочку
`привычки · мотивация · запуск-задачи` · [01:57](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=117)

**Мысль:** Сначала нужно собрать много вариантов действий без оценки реализуемости, а потом выбирать.

**Слова автора:**

> Step 2 explore options next collect any behavior that could help you achieve your aspiration and outcome anything that you could start stop or do once BJ Fogg calls this collecting a swarm of bees around your aspiration or outcome and he recommends to do it from a headspace of holding a magic wand assume that you can get yourself to do anything and that nothing will stand in your way.

**Для Напарника:** В режиме дробления задачи можно сначала предложить 3–5 вариантов микрошагов, потом человек выбирает один.

## 2. Focus Map: выбирай поведения с высоким влиянием и высокой выполнимостью («золотые»)
`привычки · процесс · мотивация` · [03:55](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=235)

**Мысль:** Оценка вклада и реализуемости (смогу ли в самый трудный день, хочу ли, а не должен) отсеивает идеальные, но нереальные привычки.

**Слова автора:**

> Now you want to take a closer look at the swarm of bees that you've collected and examine each one for its impact and feasibility and then you'll Place each behavior on this map this Focus map as BJ fog calls it pack means to ask how much will this Behavior actually contribute to achieving my outcome and feasibility is the question of can I get myself to do it given my resources my ability can I do it and do I want to do it is there more of a motivation and a want-to behind it than a should can you see yourself doing this even on the hardest day of the week and the habits that are most promising the ones you want to select are those in the top right corner with the highest impact and feasibility and BJ fog calls them the golden Behavior.

**Для Напарника:** При выборе первого шага предлагать «самый выполнимый и самый полезный»: вопрос «смог(ла) бы ты это сделать в самый тяжёлый день?»

## 3. Старт крошечный: уменьшить поведение или сделать стартовый шаг; последовательность важнее совершенства
`привычки · запуск-задачи · СДВГ` · [05:02](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=302)

**Мысль:** Найти, что делает привычку трудной, масштабировать (30 минут расслабления до одной минуты, дневник до одного предложения) или выбрать стартовый шаг (надеть кроссовки); не поднимать планку быстро.

**Слова автора:**

> Now you want to find a tiny version of the behavior that you're aiming for think about everything that could make this new Behavior hard and collect ideas of how you could make it easier as easy as possible one thing you can do is use that motivation wave you have at the beginning to do some research collect some know-how and information about this new habit to raise your ability level and then think about how you can scale back on that behavior so for example a tiny version of saying no to non-essential requests could be to reply with let me think about that till tomorrow whenever someone asks you to take on a new task a tiny version of making a plan and to-do list every day could be to think of the one thing that is most important for you on that day a tiny version of 30 minutes progressive muscle relaxation daily could be a one minute muscle relaxation tiny version of one hour alone time could be five minutes alone time a tiny version of journaling 10 minutes daily could be writing one sentence daily tiny version of regular mindset shifts throughout the day could be to have one mindset shift for example BJ Fogg recommends to say today is going to be a good day every morning right after waking up alternatively you can also find a starter step this is not even the behavior itself or a tiny portion of it but it's the thing you do before you start that new Behavior so for a daily meditation this starter step could be to just sit down on the cushion for a daily walk the starter step could be to put on your walking shoes to leave work on time the starter step could be to fix a time five or six pm and then to turn off all your devices at that time with the option of turning them back on but just to include that habit that starter step into your routine of turning them off at that point in time when you make a behavior easy to do by scaling it back you ensure that you'll be able to do it every day also on hard days you're aiming for consistency not perfection would be to do the complete habit every day but consistency means to do the tiny scale back version or starter step every day also don't raise the bar too quickly allow the tiny Habit to really form roots in your routine the tiny habit alone is a success.

**Для Напарника:** Основа микрошагов Напарника: «стартовый шаг» (открыть документ) как законный шаг сессии; радоваться самому крошечному варианту.

## 4. Подсказка-якорь: «после [конкретное действие] я [микрошаг]» через stacking
`привычки · уведомления · запуск-задачи` · [08:03](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=483)

**Мысль:** Для привычек без естественной подсказки нужно вставить их после того, что уже происходит; якорь должен быть конкретным.

**Слова автора:**

> Step five find a prompt another key ingredient for your change is to find a prompt that will help you remember to do the new habit some habits like saying no to non-essential to Do's come with a prompt in the sense that this habit requires a certain situation to happen which is the prompt but then other Habits Like daily alone time they don't really come with a prompt and for those we need to find prompts and what is best here is what James Clear calls habit stacking or BJ Fogg calls it Behavior sequencing to find a place in your routine where you can insert the Habit to have something that the Habit can follow some behavior that you're already doing to attach it to that and the last step of whatever you do before you do the new habit is what BJ Fogg calls an anchor prompt which needs to be pretty specific so here are some examples of what this could sound like when my feet touch the ground for the first time in the morning I'll say today is going to be a good day or after I've rinsed the cup that I used to have my first drink in the morning I will decide what the most important task project to do experience is for me on that day or after I've closed the door behind me in the morning take one abdominal breath or I'll set an alarm for 5 PM and when it goes off I turn off all my devices or after I've tucked in the kids and when I leave their room in the evening I'll open my journal and write one sentence.

**Для Напарника:** Настройка ритма в Напарнике: выбрать якорь («после завтрака», «когда закрыл дверь») и получать мягкое приглашение именно тогда.

## 5. Празднование — самый важный шаг; мы меняемся вдвое хуже, когда плохо о себе думаем
`награды · стыд · привычки` · [10:20](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=620)

**Мысль:** Короткое проявление радости (танец, «отлично!», улыбка) после мини-победы связывает привычку с гордостью; чувство вины мешает менять поведение.

**Слова автора:**

> Step 6 celebrate steps one through five were all preparation and step six is the first step that happens after you've completed the tiny habit or starter step to celebrate to open yourself up to feeling good about a Tiny Win BJ Fogg calls this the most important of all these steps and he thinks that in the future we will as a society recognize this more just like we are now aware of the importance of mindfulness and gratitude you could do a small dance say something like awesome or smile to express your celebration what's important is to feel proud of your success and happy about it we don't change nearly half as well when we feel bad about ourselves is when we feel good about ourselves.

**Для Напарника:** Реакция кота сразу после старта шага (микро-праздник) и никаких упрёков; подтверждает философию «без стыда».

## 6. Расширять только когда хочется, и разрешать откат, когда устал или болен
`привычки · СДВГ · стыд` · [11:20](https://youtu.be/54Nxzhwja7E?t=680)

**Мысль:** Рост привычки должен быть гибким; право вернуться к крошечной версии поддерживает последовательность.

**Слова автора:**

> Step 7 expand this is something you do later once your tiny habit has grown Deep Roots into your routine to expand the tiny Habit to more of the complete Behavior you're aiming for turn the one sentence of journaling into two sentences five sentences ten sentences key here is to do it when you really want to and to allow yourself the flexibility of scaling back when you're tired or sick.

**Для Напарника:** Предлагать увеличить длительность сессии только по желанию, а в плохой день — предложить сократить без чувства поражения.
