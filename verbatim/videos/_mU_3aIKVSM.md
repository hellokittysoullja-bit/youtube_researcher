**Слова автора:**

> Early Twitter would fail and there was a fail well and it was funny we would laugh Oh Twitter's down and a big deal but this startup pet net said you don't have to worry about feeding or watering your animals you could even Skype them you could see them in real time people thought great will buy the this kit and what happened was that the feeding schedule was set up to a remote server and the server one day failed and the pets were stranded people couldn't Skype them they didn't know whether they were alive or dead the new Schrodinger's cat and we had this big issue where people had no clue and yet they put the trust in the software and hardware because the application developers said don't worry about it don't worry about feeding and water in your pet we haven't taken care of this is technology you can trust technology it's the future and in the future in all of the videos and movies that you see you can always talk to a machine and they'll understand you the first time even if you have an accent no words in the future everything is perfect everything is glassy in the future nothing breaks or you have a dystopian future where everything is broken I don't want either of those futures what about a middle future we're not going to have all one future we're going to have ranges we need to have two the choice of what future we want when programmers sell a company and they get wealthy a lot of them either take a trip down to South America to take an ayahuasca ritual to maybe get the culture that they lost from programming or they buy a farm and they start to work with the original code that grows out of the ground I wouldn't looked at pet net and I said is there any responsibility and the Terms of Service said you agree not to rely on our service for the well-being of your animal we are not responsible if your animal dies yet we promised that this new technology would make your life easier.
