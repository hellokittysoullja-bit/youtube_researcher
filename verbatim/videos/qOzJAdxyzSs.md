## 2. Белая шляпа даёт долгосрочность, но без срочности; чёрная даёт срочность, но плохое послевкусие
`геймификация · мотивация · этика` · [02:41](https://youtu.be/qOzJAdxyzSs?t=161)

**Мысль:** Нужен баланс; Battle Camp сразу после чёрной шляпы (доплата за босса) вознаграждает белой шляпой, чтобы человек не чувствовал себя заложником.

**Слова автора:**

> Gamification doesn't mean it's necessarily bad. A lot of people voluntarily put themselves into black hat gamification in order to for instance go to the gym more often or eat healthily and it's you're probably going to make money for the company because it works on you. But just like gambling and other addictive behaviors, you don't feel like you're in control and therefore in the long run when you can leave the system you will leave the system. So even though white hat gamification is powerful and makes people feel good, there are limitations to it. The biggest problem with white hat gamification is that there's no sense of urgency. So if I tell you go out and change the world, you're all excited like, "Yeah, I'm going to change the world." But I'm going to first eat a nice breakfast. I'm going to brush my teeth. Right? No urgency, even though you feel powerful. But if I take out a gun and point at your head and say, "Go out and change the world. I'm going to kill you right now." You're probably going to still going to go change the world, but you're probably not going to go eat your breakfast. You're probably not going to brush your teeth. And you also don't feel good. So when you can escape my grasp of killing you if you don't change the world, you will want to. So one has urgency and one has long-term engagement. See, when you have too much black hat gamification, you always want to balance it with white hat gamification. And sometimes, you know, you have a lot of white hat and people are engaged, they're happy, but you're like, how come my numbers aren't improving as quickly? How come I'm not monetizing or whatnot? And usually in that case perhaps you should look into some black hat game techniques because it does drive results. So one example is well portrayed in the game Battle Camp that my friend Charles J made. So in battle camp you'll have a troop where 25 members are fighting this boss for about 8 hours. And so throughout 8 hours everyone's working hard putting their energy. And so at one point maybe after 7 hours 15 minutes people realize oh we can't beat the spots. We're only at 80% of damage. So then now the player has to make a choice. Do you just give up and have everyone 25 people wait waste eight hours of their time or do you spend $5 and you be the boss. So at this point it's it's really a loss and avoidance type of motivation. So people spend money. But so people will spend and afterwards as you can imagine they don't feel really good. Even though they beat the boss, they felt they were held hostage. Like they had to do it or else everyone's going to waste their time. Now, luckily in the game, immediately after the game showers them with white hat motivation, which is putting onto making them feel accomplished. Wow, you just beat this amazing boss. Hey, you just got this amazing award. But more importantly, there's social influence. Everyone's like, "Wow, you're the hero of our troop. You spent money and you saved our entire troop. You're awesome." And so the user feels, hey, I guess I guess that was great. that was that was $5 well spent. And so as a result, they're more likely to come back and spend more money because of that balance. So whenever you implement any type of black hat techniques, it's very important to add in the white hat stuff right after to a company and make sure you drive long-term behavior.

**Для Напарника:** Нам нужна лёгкая срочность без давления: мягкое приглашение «окно сегодня открыто», а завершение сессии всегда сопровождать тёплым признанием.

## 3. Выбор оттенка шляпы зависит от цели: долгосрочное вовлечение — белая, срочные продажи — чёрная
`геймификация · метрики · этика` · [06:38](https://youtu.be/qOzJAdxyzSs?t=398)

**Мысль:** Если цель — долговременное вовлечение сотрудников, избегать чёрной шляпы; для закрытия продаж сейчас нужна срочность.

**Слова автора:**

> Recognize is what is your goal when you design a human focus campaign, right? If you are looking at employee motivation, you should try not to go into the black hat zone because you know you're you're hopefully you're looking for long-term employee engagement. You don't want to feel bad about it. So, you want to go white hat. However, a lot of times if you're doing an e-commerce store and you're all about closing sales right now, right now, right now, close sales with urgency, then you're probably looking at a lot of black hat techniques. And obviously for your long-term success, you do want users to feel great so they'll come back again. But again, white hat does not create urgency. So if your goal is to close sales, you want to think a little bit more into the black hat.

**Для Напарника:** Цель Напарника — долгосрочное вовлечение, поэтому держимся белой шляпы.
