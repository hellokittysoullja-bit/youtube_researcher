## 2. Тесты в офисе дают «вау-эффект» и искажают обратную связь; перешли на удалённые тесты
`исследования · процесс · метрики` · [08:33](https://youtu.be/NmqZJEuCin4?t=513)

**Мысль:** Участники в офисе компании реагировали восторженно и не давали нужной критики, поэтому перешли на платформу с быстрыми сценариями на десятки людей.

**Слова автора:**

> So when i when i first started at headspace we would have we'd have members and kind of non-members come into the office every two weeks on it was usually on a friday afternoon and we would literally like you know show prototypes to them you know show it in front of them get their feedback get the get their reactions i think there was a bit of a this is many years ago but there was a bit of a i don't know what to like a wow factor of like you're at you're you're at the headspace office and you use the app and you like it and you know there's like there was a an aura about being there that i think was not giving us the kind of feedback we needed so we switched to using a platform you're probably familiar with which is usertesting.com and we've been using that for a few years to kind of check gut check and validate some of our ideas or features and like that's that's one part of it because you can you can kind of really quickly put together a script and a testing plan and get that out to you know 10 20 100 people very quickly and then review that later with your team.

**Для Напарника:** Тестировать Напарника с людьми с СДВГ в их обычной обстановке, с реальным использованием.

## 4. Не изобретай колесо: начни с простого решения базовой потребности, потом улучшай
`процесс · крафт · исследования` · [11:57](https://youtu.be/NmqZJEuCin4?t=717)

**Мысль:** Потребности людей зачастую простые; сначала простое решение, потом инновации.

**Слова автора:**

> Maybe it's a designer thing or a product thing but we tend to think we need to reinvent the wheel all the time and that we need to you know you need to think of the biggest best idea that's brand new never been done before but you know people's needs are you know sometimes pretty basic and you can probably start with something really simple that sort of ticks that box for them and you know do that first and then you know innovate on top of that would be my advice.

**Для Напарника:** Ядро: короткий Фокус и кот; усложнять только после проверки базы.

## 5. Помимо метрики успеха измеряй удовлетворённость: можно попасть в цифру и не порадовать человека

## 6. Новое поведение в данных ведёт к интервью: связать цифры с мотивами

**Мысль:** Замеченное необычное использование (вкладка Move) стало поводом для бесед с пользователями: когда обнаружили, что чувствовали, как сравнивают с другими; плюс опросы в приложении через неделю-две.

**Слова автора:**

> Post launch or again back to the interviews side like we sometimes do in-app surveys in headspace where we will actually excuse me prompt a user after they've engaged with something maybe a week after or two weeks after or we'll reach out to them and just kind of have a conversation or collect a survey of some type to gauge what that sentiment is yeah recently we've launched you know headspace is most known for meditation but we've also got a sleep category to kind of help you fall asleep and recently we launched the move tab which is kind of fitness content and exercises and stretches and things like that so we used to have some like you know some kind of mindful stretching and things like that and mindful like jogs and that in the app but now we have kind of our own dedicated tab so an example of that would be we saw some you know some new kind of user behaviors you know on the data side that you know oh this was interesting this is like a little bit of a different way of using the product then maybe people were using the meditation and that led us to conducting some interviews having some conversations with people after and sort of asking you know so when did you discover this you know what were your feelings on this did you how did you kind of feel about headspace offering that do you have any you know was there anything that really stood out as you know not great or great how does this compare to some of the other the other things you might use for you know for fitness and you know gathering that information and comparing that to the to the usage behaviors and hopefully leading us down the right path forward.
