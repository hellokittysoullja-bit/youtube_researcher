# A Redesign Strategy That Works

**Канал:** Manifesto · 16 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.

## 1. Редизайн: сначала структурные изменения по частям, потом визуальный рефреш разом
`процесс · дизайн-система · крафт` · [05:22](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=322)

**Мысль:** Разделили проблемы на структурные и визуальные: структуру выкатывали инкрементально в старом стиле, чтобы избежать «Франкенштейна», а визуальный рефреш одним релизом без смены рабочих процессов.

**Слова автора:**

> So when I was approaching the execution of a redesign my first instinct was to break it up I was as a product manager coming at this the same way I would come that building a new feature where we would start with an MVP we would a be test it and it Aurra iterate our way to an optimized end state in fact I could not imagine and the alternative which would be you know going heads down for a month working on new designs until this one big reveal moment where we may find that users absolutely hate it but our design team had very different instincts they recognized that the redesign is more than the sum of its parts and they couldn't imagine launching it piecemeal they did not want to put users through this Frankenstein experience where parts of the new style would exist alongside remnants of the old design they just could not stomach it so we wondered if there was a way to get the best of both worlds in order to figure that out we really took a closer look at the specific user pain points that we were trying to solve and we recognized from there that there are actually two sets of issues there are some structural issues things like broken navigation and a lack of hierarchy and then there were also visual issues which were things like outdated button styles and a drab color scheme across all of our platforms making this distinction allowed us to see that we could incrementally launch structural improvements in the old visual style without having to put users through that Frankenstein experience and then once we had made all the structural changes we could launch a visual refresh in one Big Bang but we'd be limiting the risk of user backlash because we wouldn't be forcing users to change their entire workflows overnight.

**Для Напарника:** Не менять сразу и структуру, и вид Напарника; новые ориентиры/фон выкатывать по одному.

## 2. Запускать первыми части с самыми большими вопросами
`процесс · исследования · метрики` · [08:44](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=524)

**Мысль:** Если часть сильно отличается или основана на непроверенных допущениях, её выпускают раньше ради инсайтов; ответы на главные вопросы приходят до крупных решений на ложных допущениях.

**Слова автора:**

> Knew what these independent parts were we then had to figure out which ones we wanted to launch first so we considered what we could learn from each into that in Keith if it was a huge departure from the status quo or if it was the design based on assumptions that we weren't able to test with a prototype or get user feedback on then we knew we had a lot to learn from actually launching it and we wanted to optimize for getting those insights early on so that we could course correct our way into a better end state over time so going back to all of the different parts that we launched these two were the biggest learning opportunities for us they changed the entire navigation of the web product so we tested these first two to validate our assumptions early I've said this a couple of times now but this is really the major advantage of an incremental approach you get to answer your biggest open questions before making big decisions based on what may be false assumptions and it really mitigates the risk of major catastrophe down the road.

**Для Напарника:** Рискованные идеи Напарника (новая механика) выпускать первыми малым куском и проверять.

## 3. Мелкие релизы дают импульс и доверие
`процесс · мотивация · доверие` · [09:50](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=590)

**Мысль:** Вместо месяцев в закрытой комнате частые выпуски приносят обратную связь, радость команды и доверие организации; Big Bang без видимого прогресса деморализует.

**Слова автора:**

> But this isn't the only advantage you also when you're taking an incremental approach you get to build momentum along the way so you're not holed up in a room for months with the Beautiful Mind thing happening you can actually ship something get feedback on it and generate some excitement and garner that trust with the rest of the organization so for example that top our navigation piece that you saw that was one of our first launches and that was a huge success which really excited the team and helped the company to rally around the project in general so here's this is just a stuff internal staff announcement when we when we recognize the win and we're getting comments and love from across the company which felt really good and allowed us to build momentum in the right direction had we bundled this top our change in with us several others and waited for sort of a BIGBANG launch at the end we would have gone months without seeing any signs of progress and that can be really demoralizing and erode that trust.

**Для Напарника:** Работать малыми PR с видимыми результатами (как в §7 CLAUDE.md), не копить правки.

## 4. Провал боковой панели: независимый запуск позволил откатить
`процесс · метрики · доверие` · [10:52](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=652)

**Мысль:** Несмотря на прототип, догфудинг и бету, запуск вызвал шквал недовольства, вдвое больше тикетов и провал A/B; независимость изменения позволила быстро откатить и не провалить весь проект.

**Слова автора:**

> Also hit some road bumps on the other end of the spectrum was a feature a new sidebar navigation which is something that we prototyped and we dog food it internally and we beta tested with customers but we still didn't anticipate what would happen at launch and it was pretty big bummer there was user backlash that ensued almost immediately you can see here that there was a lot of outrage on Twitter we got twice as many support tickets as we would normally get and the a/b test failed so this felt pretty terrible at the time but looking back now we're really glad that we were able to recognize this loss upfront because we had launched it as an independent piece and we were able to see exactly what the problem was and roll back the change that was causing all of this negative feedback had we bundled this in with everything else in one big redesign we wouldn't have been able to fix the problem as easily and it would have made the entire project look like a failure.

**Для Напарника:** Каждое крупное изменение Напарника выкатывать отдельно и обратимо.

## 5. Видеть убывающую отдачу и вовремя переключаться
`процесс · метрики · крафт` · [11:59](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=719)

**Мысль:** Раз видно влияние каждого куска, понятно, когда изменения становятся мельче и пора идти к более значимой работе.

**Слова автора:**

> Advantage of this approach is really knowing when to move on so because you can see the impact of each individual piece you can recognize when you're starting to hit diminishing returns so as we as the launches went further and further down the line the changes were more subtle and the impacts were more subtle so we knew that it was time to move on to higher impact work.

**Для Напарника:** Полировка Дома/Фокуса имеет предел: фиксировать момент убывающей отдачи, как «done is better than perfect».

## 6. Готово лучше идеального: после 75% списка отдача падает
`процесс · крафт · метрики` · [14:45](https://youtu.be/iIQmxRA85GQ?t=885)

**Мысль:** Настройки в глубине приложения не стоило доводить до совершенства; решили выпускать.

**Слова автора:**

> But as we got through about 75% of that list we realized that we were hitting diminishing returns we were working on settings dialogs that were hidden deep and corners of the app so we knew that we didn't really want to chase these diminishing returns into perfection and we agreed that done was better than perfect and it was time to launch.

**Для Напарника:** Не полировать глубокие экраны ценой выпуска; приоритет по влиянию.
