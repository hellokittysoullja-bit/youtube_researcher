## 2. Изобилие, но не переизбыток; защита и поддержка; радость простого
`игровой-дизайн · вкус · отступы` · [01:06](https://youtu.be/GI7kxMJnFeI?t=66)

**Мысль:** Уют лежит между пустой комнатой и захламлённой; персонажи поддерживают игрока, а радость в простых механиках (фермерство, рыбалка, коллекции).

**Слова автора:**

> One key aspect of coziness is abundance but not overabundance so when I started to think about this I pictured a spectrum where the axis is the number of items in a room for example on either end of the spectrum it's not very cozy either it's a completely empty room or a hoarders room both not really cozy so coziness I feel like would fall more to the center right of that Spectrum just enough in the room to make it feel warm and inviting but not too much to feel hectic or not too little so that it feels cold and isolating some other key themes highlighted by project horseshoe are protection and support basically throughout the game characters are there to support you and you don't have to play that Guardian type role the mundane is also a key feature in a lot of cozy games finding joy in the simple mechanics or ideas think stardew Valley's farming and fishing or Animal Crossings collecting.

**Для Напарника:** На экране Дома и острова держать «достаточно, но не слишком»: ровно столько элементов, чтобы было тепло, а не пусто и не суетливо.

## 3. Убежище, времена года, тёплые тусклые цвета и натуральные материалы
`цвет · вкус · дизайн-система` · [02:02](https://youtu.be/GI7kxMJnFeI?t=122)

**Мысль:** Уют создают ощущение убежища, осень и зима, тёплые приглушённые цвета с низким контрастом и яркостью, натуральные материалы (дерево, камень, глина) и баланс пространства.

**Слова автора:**

> Coziness is also found through refuge and Escape think about when you get home when it's really cold or rainy or sitting around a fireplace as the wind blows outside which goes back to that sense of security where your house is acting as that form of protection and support even in non- cozy games entering that Tavern can still give a cozy Vibe and to help establish that feeling of refuge and Escape Seasons play a key role in cozy games most notably autumn and winter Autumn when the leaf starts to change many people have a general associ ation with things that are cozy like sweaters warm drinks warm colors Etc similarly in winter when you walk into your house from that blizzard and hop under a warm blanket so in terms of Aesthetics cozy games focus a lot on those warm and dim colors with low contrast and low brightness where everything kind of just Fades together there's also a focus on natural materials like wood stone or clay for example what feels more cozy a wood cabin in a snowstorm or a giant glass skyscraper space is also utilize to give that sense of coziness but like I mentioned before you can't go too far on either side of the spectrum too much space feels empty and cold but too little space can give the impression of being hectic or unorganized or a feeling of being trapped.

**Для Напарника:** Подтверждает палитру AppBackdrop и очаг. Низкий контраст применять только к фону, а текст держать в измеренных контрастах и в коридоре светлоты.

## 4. Уютный звук: мягкая музыка без резких остановок и приглушённые ambient
`звук · вкус · доступность` · [03:12](https://youtu.be/GI7kxMJnFeI?t=192)

**Мысль:** Простые успокаивающие саундтреки без резких обрывов и ambient (лёгкий дождь, шелест, камин, приглушённые голоса) создают тепло через слух.

**Слова автора:**

> Now audio is obviously a huge part of game development in general but this can really make or break a cozy game many cozy games focus on simple and soothing soundtracks think low fire smooth jazz songs where it's easy to kind of blend together and they just kind of ramble on in the background there's No harsh stops or interruptions combine this with ambient sounds like light rain Le rustling a fireplace crackling even people in a Tavern chatting but everything is kind of muted or in a hush tone.

**Для Напарника:** Звук костра (ambient.ts) держать мягким и непрерывным, без резких стартов и стопов; приглушённые детали вместо ярких.

## 5. Внутренне вознаграждающие, необязательные, низкие ставки, рутина
`мотивация · игровой-дизайн · стыд` · [03:56](https://youtu.be/GI7kxMJnFeI?t=236)

**Мысль:** Рыбалка в Stardew ради процесса, а не награды, в отличие от квестов WoW; активности необязательны, без чувства вины, рутинны и без стресса.

**Слова автора:**

> But one key mechanic project horseshoe is calling intrinsically rewarding activities which is BAS basically doing something not because of the general reward you'd get for doing it but because of the process of actually doing it a good example of this is World of Warcraft questing versus stardew Valley fishing most of the time in World of Warcraft you're doing the Quest for the reward whether it's better gear experience points story progression whatever but in stard to Valley at least for me I do the fishing Min game because I like it's almost a meditative practice I'm not really doing it to increase my fishing level or get money although that isn't an option it's mostly just cuz I like it cozy game mechanics are also many times optional again avoiding that sense of responsibility where you don't feel guilty for not doing something all of the goals or activities are low stakes and non-committal and primarily many of those activities emphasize that sense of mundane we talked about earlier chopping a tree fishing planting organizing Etc the routine that brings out that sense of coziness and that ties into what a lot of cozy game narratives are like running a farm or a coffee shop organizing a store or home these sort of low impact ambient non-stressful gameplay Loops.

**Для Напарника:** Сессия Фокуса — ценность сама по себе (присутствие, ритуал), без обязательств; не вызывать вину за пропуск.
