## 1. Не обязательно «весело», чтобы быть затягивающим
`игровой-дизайн · дофамин · мотивация` · [01:27](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=87)

**Мысль:** Инкрементальные игры не очень веселы по механике, но очень притягательны: прогресс ради прогресса удерживает без эмерджентных взаимодействий.

**Слова автора:**

> Clicker games are simultaneously some of the simplest games and some of the most addicting games I've ever played I'd hesitate to call Idol games fun because their mechanical structure necessarily foros the traditional player to game interactions that allow for an emergent experience but just because something isn't insanely fun doesn't mean that it can't be insanely compelling.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник может притягивать ощущением прогресса (остров растёт), не требуя развлекательной механики.

## 2. Меньше веселья за минуту, но больше притяжения
`игровой-дизайн · дофамин · внимание` · [02:24](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=144)

**Мысль:** Кликеры быстрее ускоряют прогресс, чем игры с сюжетом, и требуют минимум навыка и внимания: чистая дистилляция игрового цикла «морковки».

**Слова автора:**

> Less fun per minute than regular titles but are much more compelling per minute but how clickers FastTrack player progression at an efficiency that a game with a story or characters can't quite match at the same time they require minimal investment in player skill or attention so in many cases they're a distillation of the basic gameplay Loop that dangling carrot on the industry's game design stick.

**Для Напарника:** Основа Напарника — минимум усилий на шаг и быстрое ощущение продвижения, особенно для истощённого внимания.

## 4. Flow не описывает кликеры: притягивающий опыт, не игра
`игровой-дизайн · внимание · мотивация` · [03:32](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=212)

**Мысль:** Нет навыка и сложности, чтобы строить график потока; автор считает кликеры скорее притягательным опытом, чем игрой.

**Слова автора:**

> We've already talked at length about flow Theory and video games on this channel in the past the general idea being that as long as you're balancing player skill and game difficulty to stay within this special range here your game is probably pretty enjoyable but if we try to plot out what a game like clicker Heroes might look like on this chart we run into a few major problems there's no specific skill that a player needs or will learn and there's no level of challenge that can restrict your progress so we actually can't assess our flow through either axis so do skill and challenge really have nothing to do with it I mean you can certainly watch a film or listen to an album without any prerequisite skill or challenge but what about a video game this is why I tend to believe that clickers are more accurately defined as compelling experiences rather than compelling games.

**Для Напарника:** Не пытаться обязательно держать пользователя в потоке; приемлемо создавать лёгкое притягательное присутствие.

## 5. Без навыка и без провала: глубина без ловкости
`игровой-дизайн · доступность · СДВГ` · [04:19](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=259)

**Мысль:** Кликеры отказываются от правил-провалов, а глубина (древние, реликвии) открывается медленно и не требует ловкости или анализа; единственный вызов — выбрать играть.

**Слова автора:**

> By definition games have rules failure States and challenge as well as varied input from players but many Idol games do away with all of these instead opting for a certain dichotomy you're either actively clicking or you're waiting to click this doesn't mean that clickers can't be deep or mechanically complex or that they can't surprise you Cookie Clicker starts off innocuous enough but before you know it you're in an eldrich hellscape clicker Heroes initially only lets you hire and level up Heroes before introducing ancients relics Outsiders and countless other slowburn mechanics that don't show themselves until your days or even weeks in but none of that requires manual dexterity or an analytical mind although both of those things do help to speed up your progress while playing and this really goes against the grain of what we come to understand games as today if any amount of skill is sufficient and the only challenge is whether you choose to play the game or not.

**Для Напарника:** Для Напарника: прогресс не требует навыка и не наказывается, а единственный вызов — прийти и начать.

## 6. Цена ресурсов растёт быстрее богатства
`игровой-дизайн · награды · дофамин` · [05:16](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=316)

**Мысль:** Каждая покупка чуть дороже предыдущей; рост производства захватывающе взлетает, но действия становятся менее эффективными по времени. Это формула «клика» из Cookie Clicker.

**Слова автора:**

> At first glance most clicker games revolve around pumping up a single Resource as big as it can possibly be which you can then spend to increase the rate at which you gain that resource for example if you're buying buildings to produce cookies in cookie clicker each building will produce a static ount of cookies per second a cookie Farm gives eight cookies per second a factory will give 260 per second and a prism gives 2.9 billion cookies per second each of these buildings can be upgraded eight times and each upgrade doubles your production so the higher the base production rate the more effective the upgrade and it's extremely satisfying to watch your production rate Skyrocket after clicking your fingers off and it pulls you right back into the groove of things even if you're not necessarily within flow range and unlike a typical game where the mechanics get more and more challenging as you progress clickers make your actions less and less efficient instead with each new upgrade taking longer than the last so let's try to flesh out our previously one-dimensional summary of clickers and instead say this the cost of new resources gradually but inevitably outpaces your wealth accumulation that's clickers in a nutshell.

**Для Напарника:** Для острова: каждая следующая постройка требует чуть больше сессий, но видимый рост остаётся впечатляющим.

## 7. Качественный успех: никто не говорит тебе, что ты провалился
`стыд · игровой-дизайн · мотивация` · [07:40](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=460)

**Мысль:** В Dark Souls и Hearthstone игра считает ваши победы и поражения; в idle-играх нет условий победы или провала, и успех определяется субъективным восприятием прогресса.

**Слова автора:**

> Important advantage to idle games that many modern AAA titles lack a subjective or qualitative success to give you an idea of what I mean by qualitative success let's look at the counterpart quantitative success if you die to an enemy ambush in Dark Souls you invoke a fail state which involves the game explicitly telling you that you died and you get sent back to the last bonfire at the end of your journey you can count up all the times you died and have a quantitative measure of all the times the game called your failures along the same lines when you defeat an opponent in Hearthstone this is an unqualified success a quantitative measure of your Victory the other guy of course gets a quantitative failure State when he loses but Idol games by contrast have no Victory or failure conditions you can't reach a point where your reflexes or logic aren't sufficient enough to continue likewise there's no amount of reflexes or logic that will get you to the end credits because they don't exist either so the barrier for entry is effectively zero but with all the Allure and satisfaction of sweet arbitrary progression and since games do lack Victory or failure conditions then whether you're succeeding or failing is entirely up to your own mind regardless of your actual efficiency your perception of progress in an idol game is unique to you and completely uninfluenced by those pesky quantitative measures that come from the game itself.

**Для Напарника:** Прямая поддержка принципа «без стыда»: не показывать счётчик провалов и невыполненного; прогресс определяется ощущением человека.

## 8. Любой темп без ощущения провала
`СДВГ · стыд · привычки · удержание` · [09:00](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=540)

**Мысль:** Каждое улучшение ощущается как успех без контрастного укола неудачи; по умолчанию темп свой: 5 минут в день или 5 часов. Контент заперт на недели вперёд, поэтому всегда есть цель.

**Слова автора:**

> More often than not an idol game becomes more and more rewarding the more attention you're actively putting into it's just as easy to play clicker Heroes for an hour in a full screen window as it is to leave it running in a browser Tab and just check on it every few minutes and even after tens or hundreds of hours idle games can still be compelling every upgrade feels like a success but you never feel that contrasting sting of failure you're encouraged by default to take things at your own pace whether it's 5 minutes a day or 5 hours there are no complex controls that require your dexterity no complex puzzles that require your full attention and in general idle games will just keep on trucking until you've got some time to come back and manage them unlocks and new content can be mathematically locked behind days weeks or even months of play so there's always a long-term goal for the player to shoot for.

**Для Напарника:** Не наказывать за короткие сессии и пропуски; держать долгосрочные цели острова (ориентиры), которые ждут столько, сколько потребуется.

## 9. Каждая зона обещает новое, чтобы обновлять интерес
`удержание · игровой-дизайн · награды` · [09:50](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=590)

**Мысль:** В Trimps зона 230 открывает новую механику спустя недели; каждая зона несёт обещание чего-то нового (снаряжение, профессии, механика), что возрождает угасающий интерес.

**Слова автора:**

> I always look to a game called trimps here that exemplifies just how skillfully this can be done trims revolves around sending animals called trimps into battle ATT you start in zone one and every Zone has 100 Battles but every battle is just a little bit more difficult than the last it's not uncommon to reach Zone 25 after a day or two of play but it can be weeks or even months before you reach Zone 230 which by the way unlocks a brand new game mechanic called magma this drastically changes the core gameplay altering the numerical Power Balance that you've become so familiar with up to this point and giving you something new to contend with and even the most efficient autoc clicking program won't be able to reach Zone 230 in any meaningful amount of time to test this out I've had a game going for about a week now using an auto progression program called Auto trims which makes the most efficient choices possible to progress I'm just now pushing into the lower 30 zones and it's only becoming more and more difficult from here but luckily every single zone carries the promise of something new like stronger equipment new jobs for your trimps or a brand new mechanic that revitalizes your interest when it's begun to wne.

**Для Напарника:** Планировать для острова редкие качественные сдвиги (новый тип постройки, новое существо), а не только больше того же.

## 10. Короткое замыкание цикла: быстрая награда без риска провала
`игровой-дизайн · награды · стыд` · [10:54](https://youtu.be/2lEDS6Of1i4?t=654)

**Мысль:** Кликеры сокращают игровой цикл до мгновенной награды без неудачи, дают числовой рост и намёки на будущие награды, и работают по твоему расписанию без чувства «делаю не так».

**Слова автора:**

> So in a nutshell clickers are surprisingly satisfying because they Short Circuit the conventional gameplay Loop into something more immediately rewarding without the risk of a fail State then once you're hooked they provide numerical progression at a ridiculous rate while leaving breadcrumbs about what kind of rewards you'll get next and while they can certainly be played with 100% of your attention they operate mostly on your schedule so you can play them as frequently or as rarely as you want without feeling like you're doing it wrong.

**Для Напарника:** Резюме принципа для Напарника: быстрый вознаградительный цикл, крошки о будущих наградах и полная свобода расписания без чувства ошибки.
