## 1. На мобильном меньше слов — выше шанс, что прочтут; одно слово на кнопке
`микрокопирайтинг · мобильный · тексты` · [04:37](https://youtu.be/lfZCI-SOS2g?t=277)

**Мысль:** Люди сканируют; на экране отмены «Cancel/OK» двусмысленны, и пользователь вынужден решать головоломку.

**Слова автора:**

> The clearest language possible it's about less is more especially on mobile because all the research study after study after study tells us that people don't read on mobile they scan and so removing words actually makes it more likely that people will see what you've written and then once just those essential words remain it's your job to make each one of those work for you here's an example this is a message that might pop up if you're in the middle of trying to cancel one of your subscriptions it's pretty straightforward right so which button honey cancels the subscription who thinks it's canceled a few people thinks it's canceled who thinks it's okay who's not really sure yeah so the point here is that it's just not clear right it could be canceled it could be okay but if your user has to think about it you've added friction you've given them a puzzle to solve and all because of one word on one button and that's what microcopy is about it's about taking the time it's about agonizing over that one word so that your users don't.

**Для Напарника:** Проверить модальные окна выхода из сессии: кнопки «Закончить сейчас» / «Вернуться» вместо «Отмена/Ок».

## 2. «Request»→«Next» (+33% кликов), «Book»→«Check availability» (+17%)
`микрокопирайтинг · запуск-задачи · метрики` · [05:59](https://youtu.be/lfZCI-SOS2g?t=359)

**Мысль:** Слово «запросить» было обязательством до готовности; язык должен соответствовать цели пользователя на этом этапе, и это привело к добавлению промежуточного шага.

**Слова автора:**

> Google Maps they were working on rideshare integration so being able to get an uber or a lyft right when you're searching for directions in Google Maps originally the team went with requests for the button on the bottom right naturally because you're using this to request an uber well it turns out that requests didn't perform so well the team changed the text on that button to next and ended up seeing 33% increase in clicks which in turn drove a double-digit increase in revenue and keep in mind right millions of people use Google Maps every day so this is microcopy having a huge impact on the bottom line so you might be asking why did next work better to answer that we have to zoom out and we have to look at the rest of the story microcopy is about the words that we use but the bigger opportunity happens whenever we take a step back and look at the whole flow or the user journey it's never just about one word or one button in isolation this is about a story this there's a storyline here so looking back at our Google Maps example through research it turns out that we learned request is a commitment before the user is ready to make one in other words people at this stage in the flow were just kind of checking things out just wanted to poke around and see how this ride booking thing worked within Google Maps and they were hesitant to hit a button that instant excuse me instantly sent a car to them that insight led the team to add a second step where users could adjust their pickup location or review their payment option before requesting a ride and this is another example from hotel search on Google so originally the header was booked a room and again it turned out that book a room was overly committal at this stage in the users journey the UX writer suggested changing that to check availability which matches with the user's intent is at this stage which is to browse write rather than to book that simple change led to a 17% increase in engagement so that's 17% more users who are taking the first step in booking as a direct result of tailoring language to match users goals not the products goals.

**Для Напарника:** Не требовать обязательств в первом шаге: «Посмотреть шаги» / «Попробовать 5 минут» вместо «Начать задачу»; снижает порог старта.

## 3. Важная информация живёт рядом с ценой, а не внизу простыни текста
`иерархия · микрокопирайтинг · доверие` · [09:05](https://youtu.be/lfZCI-SOS2g?t=545)

**Мысль:** Условие удвоения цены было утоплено внизу; перенос рядом с ценой сделал его ясным; писатели тоже проектируют макет.

**Слова автора:**

> The UX writer was asked just add a line of text under the payment option that said that the price of the phone would increase to $349 if you didn't activate it within 30 days so I thought about what we were really trying to say here and how the hierarchy on the screen works what I wrote it was getting lost in this wall of text literally getting buried remember this is at the very bottom of the screen when you scroll down all the way don't you think it's pretty important for the customer to know that the price that they pay would almost double if they didn't activate the phone so I thought a little bit more about where this information really belongs and that was at the top of the screen right where the price already is I basically designed a new proposal that made the $199 price conditional now says with activation and then right by right by that right below by design it says $349 and the conditions with that potential price it's clear it's up front and most importantly in the right place the takeaway is that writers are designers and we work outside the box given to us in this example it meant adjusting the layout on the screen but if you just edit words here and there your job isn't really done your users are going to trust you more if they receive information and they feel like they were there receiving the information in the right place at the right time.

**Для Напарника:** Условия и важные пояснения размещать там, где принимается решение, а не в сноске.
