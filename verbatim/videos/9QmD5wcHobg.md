# Comment RÉUSSIR l'onboarding de vos applications :  ASTUCES et TUTORIEL complet

**Канал:** Lucas & Nathan Simula · 17 мин · субтитры: en-US (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/9QmD5wcHobg

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.

## 1. Онбординг подстраивается под ситуацию человека (Uber: минимум данных, сразу результат)
`онбординг · запуск-задачи · СДВГ` · [02:11](https://youtu.be/9QmD5wcHobg?t=131)

**Мысль:** Человек, которому срочно нужно такси, терпит один вопрос и сразу хочет результат; поэтому спрашивают только необходимое.

**Слова автора:**

> Let's take an example, let's take Uber. When you download the Uber app and sign up, well, I wouldn't say " always," but I'd say most of the time —I don't have the stats. It's because you're leaving a party, you're cold, there's no public transport, it's raining, you're far away, and your girlfriend tells you: "Yeah, let's go home please, don't take a bike," and I don't want to walk home, you know. So, you download Uber. You're kind of in a rush, you're in "I'm downloading Uber because I want an Uber right now in 30 seconds" mode. So, what is Uber's objective here? They think: "Okay, if I make the onboarding super annoying, ask for his grandmother's name, where he lives, his motto, or whatever, it’s annoying. What he wants is to get home. " So I’ll just ask for one piece of info, just to verify he’s not a robot . And I’ll ask for his phone number, not his email, because on a mobile app —we’ll see later—it’s better to ask for a phone number. And then, I’ll let him enter an address right away. 1.1.b, B, I’ll geolocate him and, boom, I’ll tell him: "There’s an Uber near you, he’ll be here in 5 minutes, it costs you 10 bucks." See, that’s the info he wants. Okay. So your onboarding flow will depend on the situation your user is in;.

**Для Напарника:** Пришедший в состоянии «не могу начать» не должен проходить анкету; один вход и сразу первая сессия.

## 2. Самооценка уровня ненадёжна: измеряй поведением и подстраивай сложность
`онбординг · мотивация · СДВГ` · [05:04](https://youtu.be/9QmD5wcHobg?t=304)

**Мысль:** Оценка «от 1 до 5» субъективна; лучше дать пробные задания и по скорости и точности подобрать уровень, это ощущается прогрессом.

**Слова автора:**

> A mistake to avoid is asking for their level just like that. Example of who is, who is, sorry, self-assessing. If I tell you, what’s your level of Japanese between one and five stars, well, that’s useless, you see. Because you have one guy, he learned Japanese 8 years ago, he’s going to say, "Well, I’ve got two stars." The other one, he went to Japan for a week and he’s going to say, "Oh, it’s good, I managed to ask for a dish from a waitress, I’ve got three stars," you see? And in the end, the experience will be terrible later because you’re going to push content that isn't adapted at all. The best solution, since they have time, once again, they have time, so you can add slightly longer onboarding screens, you can push content to them. For example, test them ; actually, you send them a simple question, a translation to do. In Japanese with three choices. If, for example, they get it right, you adjust. Well, they got it right, so you give them a slightly more difficult question . They got it right, but they still took 3 minutes to answer, right? That means they might have had some difficulties. I'll push content that's maybe a little simpler, or a little harder than the first question, but less so than if they had answered quickly. If they get it wrong, I'll offer simpler content and I'll do that for 4, 5, 6, 7 questions. I have no idea how many questions are needed for my algorithm to have a sort of assessment to then push the right content. And doing that will allow them to get that addictive quality and feel like they're progressing, that they have something at their level.

**Для Напарника:** Не спрашивать человека, сколько он может фокусироваться; предлагать длительность по реальным прошлым сессиям и мягко подстраивать (не наказывая за недотянутое).

## 3. Убери всё, не нужное для главного действия; запрашивай остальное позже
`онбординг · конверсия · иерархия` · [07:37](https://youtu.be/9QmD5wcHobg?t=457)

**Мысль:** Трение (платёж, данные) выноси как можно дальше по пути, пока у человека нет интереса и ценности (Uber, TikTok).

**Слова автора:**

> Point: you get rid of the rest. It's as simple as that. In fact, everything that isn't useful for carrying out the action, you remove it. It will come later. I'll go back to my examples. Uber, the credit card. Well, I don't remember off the top of my head. If it asks, but if it does ask, you can skip it and then you'll find it again when you pay, actually. So, anything that's a friction point, because payment is actually a friction point. Entering your credit card—everyone has filled an e-commerce cart and then you get to checkout, and you're like "actually no, forget it, it's a no-go." Everyone has done that. Abandoned shopping carts. I don’t have the stats, but it’s huge . Same thing for Uber; you’re hyped, then it’s time to pay. Oh man, that’s annoying. And so, the friction , you want to push it as far back as possible. You see, when they’re desperate, their Uber tells them, " Arriving in 2 minutes." Hurry up, someone else will grab it, just like Booking does so well. And, well, put in your card info, quickly, quickly, quickly! But if you make the onboarding annoying? Oh, forget it. Wait, I don't even know if I’m going to use your app yet. You’re already asking for my money. No, no, that’s not how it works. So add friction, but at the right time, and any info you can ask for later, ask for later. Example, another example, social media apps now. Right? Social media, you go on TikTok, but you can use TikTok without signing up. Actually, after a while, you’ll get a pop-up because they want your data. But anyway, TikTok knows perfectly well: "I’ll get your data; you’ll be so addicted to scrolling that, don't worry, I’ll get everything later." So, add friction— all that useful info for your marketing , for gathering data, or pushing the right content—but do it as late as possible if it’s not mandatory, and remove as much friction as you can.

**Для Напарника:** Не просить ничего, кроме шага задачи; имя и чек-ин предлагать по одному вопросу за визит и после первой пользы.
