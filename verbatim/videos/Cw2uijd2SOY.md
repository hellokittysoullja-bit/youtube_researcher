## 1. Исполнительные функции — жрущие топливо дирижёры мозга

**Слова автора:**

> Your executive functions are those higher-order mental processes that are involved with things like memory and prioritization and decision-making and regulation and lots of other things. I always think of them as like the human-only functions that your brain does. Your executive functions also coordinate different mental processes together, sort of like the orchestra conductor of your brain or the CEO of your brain functions. And your executive functions are also energy-intensive. I always call them brain fuel hogs, right? So, your executive functions burn a lot of brain energy, they burn a lot of actually glucose. And that's the way I explain it to clients.

## 2. Активация и ментальная организация тянут из одного бака
`СДВГ · нейро · запуск-задачи` · [03:34](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=214)

**Мысль:** Старт задачи и приоритизация/последовательность оба энергоёмки; когда на решение уходит топливо, на запуск его не остаётся.

**Слова автора:**

> The two executive functions that are most impactful on our productivity when you have ADHD are the one that we call activation and the one that I call mental organization of information. And again, there are lots of different models. Russell Barkley calls the one that I refer to as mental organization of information. I do believe he calls that mental play. And there are different, you know, different ways to look at this, but activation for starters is simply getting started on something. It's it's all of the mental processes and all of the things that your brain needs to do to actually activate or sometimes this is called initiate on a task, right? It's simply just getting started. The mental organization of information is all of the different ways that in your mind you make sense of information while you're using it. So, for example, prioritization, putting things in order, putting things in sequence, thinking about the steps in something. When you're doing all of that in your mind, that's all of that mental manipulation of information or mental organization of information. That's another executive function. So, both activation and that mental organization of information are executive function energy intensive activities, right? So, they're they're literally burning the same fuel from the same fuel tank. Or metaphorically, they're burning the same fuel from the same fuel tank. They're literally both burning glucose at higher rates than other kinds of mental processes do.

**Для Напарника:** Разводить «что делать» (решает напарник) и «делать» (человек), чтобы старт был дешёвым.

## 3. Экстернализация: часы, списки и таск-система снимают нагрузку с мозга
`СДВГ · время · процесс` · [05:14](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=314)

**Мысль:** Всё, что можно вынести наружу (время, память, организацию), освобождает исполнительные функции.

**Слова автора:**

> To externalize those executive functions, to have those the things that those executive functions are doing for us, find ways outside of our bodies to compensate for those executive functions. Simple example for that is wearing a watch or always having a clock in view to help support your executive function challenge with time sense, right? ADDers tend to be time blind. So, always having a clock in view, always having a watch is one way to externalize time. Lists are ways to externalize our, you know, our executive function struggles with memory. A good task management system is another way to externalize your executive function struggles with mental organization of information and memory and all of that,.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник как внешняя память: очередь шагов, видимые часы, «что дальше».

## 4. Разделяй решение и действие по времени или активности
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · время` · [06:09](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=369)

**Мысль:** Деньги топлива уходят на планирование; если разнести планирование и старт, остаётся энергия активироваться.

**Слова автора:**

> So, when you are trying to decide what to do next, you're decide you're trying to plan, you're trying to figure out what action to take next, and then you're trying to get started on it, sometimes that mental organization that's required in the deciding piece takes enough processing power, takes enough of that, you know, executive function fuel in the executive function fuel tank, so to speak, that you don't have a lot left over to activate. So, one of the very simple things that I encourage my clients to do is to find ways to separate the deciding from the doing. So, separating the deciding, separating the planning, separating the prioritization from actually getting started on the task. So, if you can separate with some span of time or some other kinds of activities, your process for figuring out what you're going to do when in some way from actually getting started on whatever it is that you intend to do, you may have more energy and you may have more focus and you may have more ability to activate and get started on that thing.

**Для Напарника:** Вечерний или утренний «план-на-завтра» отдельно от Фокуса; в Фокус человек приходит с готовым шагом.

## 5. Без плана день диктует то, что попало в поле внимания
`СДВГ · процесс · мотивация` · [07:14](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=434)

**Мысль:** Планирование нелюбимо: одни перепланируют, другие избегают; но без него остаётся реактивность и тушение пожаров. Назвать можно как угодно.

**Слова автора:**

> Most of my clients don't love the word planning. Most of us with ADD have sort of a love-hate relationship with planning. Some of us are over-planners, so we burn ourselves up, we use, you know, sort of use all of our energy up with making the perfect plan. Others of us find it really hard to sit down and force ourselves to plan. But, when you're determining what action to take when, that's a form of planning. So, if you can call it whatever you want, I actually call it setting myself up for the next day. I have clients that just call it the deciding, and that's okay, right? But, that form of planning is an important thing for us to do. If we're not planning in some way, again, no matter what you call it, if you're not planning in some way, you're letting whatever comes into your attention direct your action. So, you're reactive, you're your whole day is dictated by email, your whole day is dictated by whatever comes into your attention, or whatever sort of fires need to be put out in the moment. And that's not proactive, that's not moving anything forward, that's just basically treading water and keeping up. And most of us do a lot of that.

**Для Напарника:** Назвать планирование мягко («настроить завтра», «набросок на день»), не «план»; не требовать идеального.

## 6. План не работает без паузы перед стартом: мозгу нужно дозаправиться
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · время` · [09:02](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=542)

**Мысль:** Планирование и действие берут из одного источника; нужен разрыв по времени или активности, чтобы восстановиться.

**Слова автора:**

> But, that's not enough. It's not enough just to have a plan. We can still have trouble executing the plan or activating or getting started when we need to if we're not careful about when we plan with respect to when we actually intend to get started. So, anytime that you have to make a plan, decide what comes next, it can really be helpful to separate that from the actual getting started by some chunk of time or some other kind of activity that isn't quite so executive function intensive. I hope that makes sense, but got to give your brain a chance to recharge. You've got to give your brain a chance to sort of fuel back up, so to speak. And remember, the planning or the deciding and the getting started or the doing are both taking from the same energy source. So, again, conserve that energy, make it easier for yourself to actually get stuff done,.

**Для Напарника:** После составления шага предлагать маленькую паузу (вдох, вода) перед стартом таймера.

## 7. Не планируй в час упадка и прерываний; подгоняй систему под себя
`СДВГ · время · процесс` · [10:09](https://youtu.be/Cw2uijd2SOY?t=609)

**Мысль:** Планирование стоит ставить в момент энергии и без помех; чужой способ редко подходит.

**Слова автора:**

> You don't want to slate your planning, you don't want to slate your deciding part for a point in your day when your energy's really low or a point in your day where your focus is really low. You don't want to slate your planning for a time in the day when you're prone to interruptions, right? You want to You want to look at your life, look at how you do things most naturally, how you do things most effectively, what you're already doing now that's working well, and figure out how to make these things work for yourself. You can't try to plug yourself into someone else's way of doing things. It very rarely works.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-ин: «когда у тебя больше сил?» и подгонка напоминаний и Фокуса под ответ.
