## 5. Сократи трение: одно поле (email) вместо длинной формы
`запуск-задачи · онбординг · активация` · [06:39](https://youtu.be/YkxGTwbRQ9w?t=399)

**Мысль:** Каждый клик, скролл или переход — трение, и «сегодня неохота» побеждает; чем проще и раздробленнее процесс, тем выше конверсия.

**Слова автора:**

> Reduce friction and what does friction mean anytime somebody needs to do something they need to click a button they need to scroll down they need to move to another page that creates friction and the more friction there is the more likely they are to say ah you know what i don't feel like doing this today it takes too much time it's too difficult i'm just giving up and they're not going to purchase so one way to really reduce the friction is in this example of egghead which is courses for developers you can see that they the only thing you need to do here is put your email and click start learning this looks very easy there isn't like a very big sign up form with like 10 fields in it you don't need to click a button of get started and then be taken to a page where you need to open up an account and whatever connect my google and all of these different things that might scare me might be time consuming i might whatever i don't have that data right now and so the more that you can simplify and break down the process to reduce friction the more high conversion your website is going to be.

**Для Напарника:** Первый старт Фокуса: без регистрации и длинных форм; напечатал шаг, нажал, пошло.

## 6. Скорость загрузки — решение дизайнера: меньше и легче картинки, больше векторов
`мобильный · конверсия · доступность` · [09:15](https://youtu.be/YkxGTwbRQ9w?t=555)

**Мысль:** Долгая загрузка заставляет уходить; тяжёлые изображения выбираются дизайнером, векторы грузятся быстрее.

**Слова автора:**

> Speed load not a lot of designers think about this because they think well this has to do with developers right they need to build this on a fast server and so what so the website loads fast right and by the way you can go into google page speed insights to check out your website to know how fast it loads and if things take too long to load right people are just and google has the data on this people are just screw this i'm not going to i'm not going to buy here right this takes too long and they're dropping so the faster the website loads the more the faster the more conversions you have and by the way our flux academy website is not optimized for speed load and i'll tell you why because we're using very heavy imagery so again this is your choice as a designer you can design for assets that are not going to be heavy for example if you're using either less images or smaller images not like big images or if you're using vectors which are loading faster and smaller file size versus images you can make your website load faster and then you'll have higher conversions.

**Для Напарника:** SVG-спрайты острова и MascotSvg — правильный путь; на лендинге избегать тяжёлого растра.
