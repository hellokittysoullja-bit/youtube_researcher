## 2. Форма вверху страницы повышает и количество лидов, и долю продаж
`лендинг · конверсия · метрики` · [02:56](https://youtu.be/vtajfv7_QFo?t=176)

**Мысль:** Тест: когда форма вверху, ясно, что делать, и убирается лишний шаг прокрутки.

**Слова автора:**

> So number one with the positioning we always put our landing page forms at the top of the page. The reason being is when someone gets the landing page we want them to know what they need to do which will be fill in the form and what they need to fill in. So many landing pages that I've seen force people to go through a lot of content to go to the bottom of the page to fill it in there. Now, even if you have a dynamic button that will take you to the bottom of the page, it's adding another friction point that someone has to go through, the goal is for someone to submit a lead. We know our leads are qualified because of the quality of the traffic and the quality of our ads. So, we're trying to remove any friction point for the user to fill in the form. So, the reason why we put in here is we've tested that we get higher conversion rates when the form is at the top of the page. And we've also noticed that we actually get a higher sales rate from those as well. Number one, because we're actually getting more leads because it's at the top of the page. And number two, it's very clear what they do. The sales rate is actually higher as well.

**Для Напарника:** Сценарий старта задачи на экране Фокус: поле шага сразу на виду, без прокрутки.

## 3. Каждое поле формы снижает конверсию; баланс между объёмом и качеством лидов
`конверсия · онбординг · метрики` · [04:47](https://youtu.be/vtajfv7_QFo?t=287)

**Мысль:** Выбирай только критичные поля; вопрос — компромисс между конверсией и обогащением данных.

**Слова автора:**

> Now with the fields on the landing page, this is a part where I think it's really important to be really considerate about what fields you add or what form fields you add. So the ones that are very obvious from the start would be name and email. But as we move on and for most businesses, phone number is then appropriate. But a lot of landing pages don't ask for them. I think it's really appropriate for B2B and a high ticket to be asking for them. Now for the nature of this client and the business, we've have multiple questions that we'd want to ask people. But for the distributors and the business owner of this business, we just really needed to know the crucial information, which would be their location, which in this place we're actually looking for state. Sometimes we use postcode, but we've decided to use state in this instant. And the next part would be industry because this product can be used across many industries. We're wanting to load up the algorithm in Facebook to actually know what users and conversions are coming from what industry. So if we look at this here we have mining, construction, agriculture, self- storage and container storage facilities, councils and governments and other. Now there's a significant longer list that the client gave us but we wanted just to hone in on those at the start as they're our ideal prospect. Now with other landing pages I tend to ask things of time frame to purchase, budget and other intentbased questions. But for this client, as we're just rolling out this landing page over the last few weeks, and we're still getting 20 to 30 conversions a day at a 30% conversion rate, we're noticing that the quality is actually quite great from this. As we go in time, and I've told the client this, when we start to ask those questions, we will expect the conversion rate to drop because every time you add another question to the form, it will naturally drop the conversion rate. It's just statistically shown. So, I'm always wary of ensuring that the highest conversion rates achieved whilst also getting the highest quality lead possible, especially for a salesperson or a distributor or a business owner who needs to contact the lead. You don't want to be getting 100 leads of just name and email. You want to be getting them enriched with data.

**Для Напарника:** Онбординг: спрашивать минимум (имя), остальное — позже и по одному вопросу за визит.

## 4. Тест: «Submit» на кнопке дал +2–5% в B2B
`конверсия · микрокопирайтинг · метрики` · [07:49](https://youtu.be/vtajfv7_QFo?t=469)

**Мысль:** Традиционная скучная кнопка выглядит профессионально и вызывает доверие в B2B high-ticket.

**Слова автора:**

> And the other thing I just want to mention was the call to action button. Now, we've been testing this out recently and in previous times with our landing page, we've been using access brochure, request pricing, but we've actually noticed recently going back to the traditional submit form actually has more of a professional look and feel. And we're noticing we're getting higher conversion rates just from using the button submit because as we're using so many direct response elements on the landing page, this comes back to a traditional business model which is almost a bit boring which I kind of love because it senses more trust. I spoke to our team about this and they just recommended and I thought it was a great idea. We've noticed that we're getting higher conversion rates and we started rolling it out on other landing pages and we've noticed upticks from 2 to 5% on many landing pages. If you're going to take one hack from this is test out a submit word on your submit button and see how that goes.

**Для Напарника:** Не применимо: для Напарника кнопки должны описывать исход и быть тёплыми; результат для B2B-лидов.

## 5. Закреплённая кнопка CTA, чтобы действие было доступно сразу
`лендинг · конверсия` · [08:56](https://youtu.be/vtajfv7_QFo?t=536)

**Мысль:** Вместо кнопок по всей странице одна всплывающая кнопка: когда человек готов, она уже рядом.

**Слова автора:**

> And as you would have noticed, we had a pop-up button up here because we noticed that when we started using buttons throughout the page, it clanked up the page. Whilst as the user is going through the page, we ultimately want them to do one thing, access the brochure and request pricing. So whenever they've had enough on the page, the button is there immediately. They don't need to scroll up. It's there immediately.

**Для Напарника:** На длинном лендинге липкая CTA «Попробовать» на мобильном.
