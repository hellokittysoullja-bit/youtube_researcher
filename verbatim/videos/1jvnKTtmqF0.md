## 1. Внутреннюю сложность нельзя убирать, её нужно дозировать
`мотивация · СДВГ` · [02:21](https://youtu.be/1jvnKTtmqF0?t=141)

**Мысль:** Внутренняя нагрузка это сам процесс обучения, преодоление которого даёт чувство достижения; управляется нарезкой (chunking).

**Слова автора:**

> This intrinsic load is the process of learning ultimately it's the complexity of what you're trying to get your head around and overcoming that gives the user a real sense of achievement so we don't want to take it away even if we could what we do want to do though is manage it the main way we do that is again probably one of the most foundational things instructional designers learn chunking we break down large complex Concepts or pieces of information into smaller more manageable pieces this might be all delivered in the same modality but in different sections this might be split up further this might be a syllabus rather than a single module or it might be multiple syllabuses across multiple courses the important thing is that we recognize that we cannot remove intrinsic cognitive load but we can manage it by breaking content up.

**Для Напарника:** Дробление задач на микрошаги сохраняет чувство достижения, а не лишает его.

## 2. Эстетика снижает нагрузку до переломной точки, потом увеличивает
`вкус · крафт · иерархия` · [03:52](https://youtu.be/1jvnKTtmqF0?t=232)

**Мысль:** Красота помогает, пока не превращается в «эффектно и грандиозно» с кучей элементов на экране; каждый элемент должен помогать человеку, иначе «ты делаешь обратное».

**Слова автора:**

> This is why when people talk about Aesthetics and learning design it's not just I want to make it look pretty because that's what I like to do or because I want to show off Aesthetics matter in that they can help reduce extraneous cognitive load now there is a limit to that and a Tipping Point where we can very easily Focus too much on making things look impressive and Grand and actually go from reducing to increasing extraneous load by putting so much stuff on the screen it starts to get confusing remember every decision we make in terms of modality in terms of navigation in terms of how content is delivered whether it's digital or human or some combination of the two is all about helping the learner succeed if that is not the reason you're adding something you need to really stop and question yourself hard why is this thing being added if it's just for you if it's to make yourself look clever or to show off or to make it pretty simply for the sake of that please reconsider you are doing the exact opposite of what you think you are doing you are hindering the learner by increasing extraneous loads.

**Для Напарника:** Прямое подтверждение правила CLAUDE.md §10: анимации и глоу только с привязкой к принципу.

## 3. Баланс нагрузки, а не только её снижение
`внимание · исследования` · [06:12](https://youtu.be/1jvnKTtmqF0?t=372)

**Мысль:** Задача не просто уменьшать нагрузку, а балансировать внутреннюю и внешнюю, чтобы осталась продуктивная (germane).

**Слова автора:**

> New behaviors or come up with new systems synthesize new information based on what we're giving them and their existing understanding it's all about balance not just about reduction which so often is what we hear about cognitive load we so often hear it reduces cognitive load and that's a massive oversimplification and quite often simply not accurate you might say I'm looking to reduce the extraneous load in order to balance a higher intrinsic load of this complex topic but you're not just reducing cognitive loads be specific in how you talk about these kinds of topics.

**Для Напарника:** Для Фокуса: убрать лишнее вокруг, чтобы осталась энергия на сам шаг.

## 4. Перерывы и чередование интенсивного и спокойного не неэффективность
`время · СДВГ · внимание` · [10:18](https://youtu.be/1jvnKTtmqF0?t=618)

**Мысль:** Человек интенсивно учится около 20 минут; после интенсивного блока нужен более лёгкий период или настоящий перерыв, паузы нужно закладывать намеренно.

**Слова автора:**

> Sessions there always was the accusation of their not very time efficient they're full of breaks and cool down moments and just grabbing a coffee or having some snacks on the table so people can get them when they need them but actually those aren't inefficiencies those are deliberately placed or should be deliberately placed to help manage load across the session think about it if you're reading an all-day training session for eight hours and all you do is go oh yeah we're gonna stop half an hour in the middle doesn't work think back to school and hour-long lessons maybe two of them back to back before a 15-minute break how much did you learn in that second lesson how much did you learn in the second half of the first lesson realistically we as human beings have quite a short span though it's not perfectly defined of time where we can work intensely and learn so we need to bear that in mind and offer breaks cooldowns slow and fast parts of a face-to-face training session and by that I mean yes we might do 20 minutes of really intense kind of reflection and learning or delivery of fresh content but then we need to make sure that we go from that very fast very intense delivery to a period of more relaxed subtle delivery perhaps that is the difference between a very high challenge activity and then a period of reflection and presentation maybe it's an actual break maybe not.

**Для Напарника:** Сессия Фокуса: после 25 минут предлагать тёплую паузу с котом, а не сразу «ещё один раунд».
