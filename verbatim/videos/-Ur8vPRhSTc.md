## 1. Бесконечный скролл убирает точку «продолжать?», уведомление — условный рефлекс
`тёмные-паттерны · внимание · этика` · [02:15](https://youtu.be/-Ur8vPRhSTc?t=135)

**Мысль:** Отсутствие естественной точки остановки лишает мозг момента решения; уведомление вызывает лёгкий стресс, проверка его снимает — это и есть награда.

**Слова автора:**

> Mechanism number one, infinite scroll. Before 2006, web pages had pagination. You'd reach the bottom, click next page, and that small friction gave you a natural stopping point. Aza Raskin, who designed infinite scroll, later said he regrets inventing it. By removing the bottom of the page, apps eliminate the moment where your brain can ask, "Should I keep going?" The decision never has to be made. Mechanism number two, notifications. Your phone buzzes and you feel a pull to look. That pull is not random. It's the product of classical conditioning. Your brain has learned that a notification might mean something socially important. A reply, a like, a message from someone you care about. So, the alert triggers a mild stress response and checking the phone relieves it. That relief is the reward.

**Для Напарника:** В Напарнике делать явные мягкие точки остановки (конец сессии, «на сегодня хватит»), а не бесконечную ленту; уведомления — только редкие и тёплые.

## 2. Рычаги: выключить уведомления, добавить трение, менять контекст, а не волю
`привычки · внимание · процесс` · [07:55](https://youtu.be/-Ur8vPRhSTc?t=475)

**Мысль:** Решение о среде принимается один раз и не требует воли в моменте; трение работает, потому что мозг ленив к усилию.

**Слова автора:**

> First lever, change the defaults. Turn off all non-essential notifications, not reduce them, turn them off. Notifications are interruptions by design. When you check the app on your schedule instead of responding to a buzz, you've already reclaimed a significant amount of autonomy. Studies show that notification-free phone use produces the same content consumption with dramatically lower stress and distraction. Second lever, introduce friction. The platforms remove friction to keep you in. You can add it back. Move social apps off your home screen, so opening them requires deliberate steps. Use a separate browser tab instead of the app. It is clunkier, and that is exactly the point. Some people put a rubber band around their phone as a physical reminder to pause. These are not perfect solutions, but friction works because our brains are genuinely lazy about effort. Third lever, design your environment, not just your willpower. Willpower is a limited resource that depletes throughout the day. Environmental design is permanent. Keeping your phone in another room while you work does not require any discipline in the moment. The decision was made once. This is a core insight from behavioral economics. The best way to change behavior is to change the context in which the behavior happens, not to try harder.

**Для Напарника:** Подсказка в настройке сессии: решение «телефон подальше» принимается один раз перед стартом, а не посреди работы.
