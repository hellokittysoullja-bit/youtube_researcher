# The Psychology of High Converting Landing Pages

## 1. Эффект ореола: первые 50 мс решают, как воспринимается всё остальное
`лендинг · конверсия · иерархия` · [00:33](https://youtu.be/qGJG7nLPd8w?t=33)

**Мысль:** За 50 мс формируется мнение, за 5 секунд решение остаться; захламлённый первый экран создаёт негативный ореол на всю страницу.

**Слова автора:**

> Study show that it takes about 50 milliseconds for someone to form an initial opinion about your landing page, and then it takes them another 5 seconds to determine whether they're going to stay on your page or leave altogether. This is driven by a cognitive bias known as the halo effect, which is the idea that somebody's first impression of something entirely influences how they perceive everything else that follows. So, if the first thing a visitor sees is high-quality or premium, and it speaks directly to what people care about, their brain will automatically assume that your products or services and your entire company as a whole are equally just as valuable. On the flip side, if the first thing that people see is cluttered, it doesn't have a good layout, and it just doesn't have good copywriting and creates intrigue, then that is going to create a negative halo and sabotage the rest of the entire page, no matter how good it is.

**Для Напарника:** Первый экран лендинга: чистый, красивый, с одним понятным обещанием; кот и остров сразу в кадре.

## 2. Правдоподобие соцдоказательства: фото, полное имя, источник
`доверие · лендинг · конверсия` · [02:32](https://youtu.be/qGJG7nLPd8w?t=152)

**Мысль:** Текстовые безличные отзывы не верят; результат/видео, имя+фото и источник дали рост конверсии на 60–80%; на мобильном показывать всё сразу, не прятать в карусель.

**Слова автора:**

> There's one big difference between social proof that is poor and high-converting social proof, and that is believability. The more people believe your social proof and subconsciously interpret it to be legit, the higher the propensity for them to then trust your brand and subsequently convert. This is extremely important in today's day and age where trust is at an all-time low. Low-converting landing pages often just have boring text-based reviews, which people really don't trust anymore, and they lack any verifiable details. In comparison, the highest-converting landing pages that I've ever seen typically show number one, either an image of the actual results or a video testimonial. Number two, the full name and photo of the person leaving the review. And number three, the actual source of the review. Psychologically, this makes a massive difference, and we've seen conversion increases upwards of 60 to 80% just by implementing this one strategy. The other thing is that the highest-converting pages show all of this up front, right? So, on desktop, they're going to show a wall of love format where everything is shown up front. And on mobile, they're not going to hide the social in a carousel, right? They stack everything vertically and show it all up front. Like people having to subconsciously absorb the sheer amount of social proof, they have absolutely no choice but to trust your brand.

**Для Напарника:** Отзывы на лендинге с реальным именем, фото и ссылкой на источник; на мобильном вертикальным стеком.

## 3. Когнитивная лёгкость: приоритет главного, иконки/буллеты, простой язык
`внимание · иерархия · СДВГ` · [04:11](https://youtu.be/qGJG7nLPd8w?t=251)

**Мысль:** Порог когнитивной нагрузки: меньше выбора и текста, чёткая иерархия размером, весом и цветом, язык 5–8 класса.

**Слова автора:**

> The third principle is about making things easy, aka cognitive load. The human brain has something called a cognitive threshold, which just means that there's a limit to how much the human brain can process at one given time. A low-converting landing page has a high cognitive load, which means that people have to exert a lot of mental power just to decipher the information on the page, right? Because maybe the page has a confusing layout, it has way too much information, or just way too many choices. As a result, people end up getting frustrated and overwhelmed and just end up exiting the page altogether without ever converting. What you want to aim for instead is called cognitive ease. This is a landing page where a human can process the information with little to no effort. High-converting landing pages achieve this by prioritizing the most important conversion elements and completely getting rid of anything else. They avoid having large blocks of text by using things like scannable icons and headings or bullet points that are way easier to digest. They have crystal-clear messaging that speaks on a fifth or eighth-grade reading level, whereas poor landing pages use vague headlines or generic jargon. And finally, high-converting pages have clear visual hierarchy, which is basically the art of guiding a user's information from the most important elements to the least important through things like font size, weight, color, etc.

**Для Напарника:** Особенно важно для СДВГ-аудитории: на каждом экране один главный элемент и минимум текста.

## 4. Три шага: первый экран, правдоподобные отзывы, убрать лишнее
`лендинг · конверсия · процесс` · [06:37](https://youtu.be/qGJG7nLPd8w?t=397)

**Мысль:** Убрать навигацию и оставить одну CTA, объединить поля формы, заменить текст иконками и задать иерархию.

**Слова автора:**

> Step one, create a killer above the fold. Think of that one hard-hitting benefit or outcome that your target audience dreams of wanting to achieve, and communicate that in just your headline and your image. This is going to create a powerful halo effect like no other. Step two is creating ultra-believable social proof. All of the social proof you have, I want you to think of how can I make the social proof way more believable and trustworthy? Can you add images or video? Can you add a story of what the person has experienced? The more specific you can be, the better the results. Step number three is to make things simple. Go through your entire landing page and simply just remove anything that is not absolutely critical for conversions. On your navigation, right? Remove all of your items and keep your focus on just one CTA button. On your forms, combine first name and last name into just full name so you can get rid of an extra field. Instead of text, utilize images and icons and things that are more scannable wherever possible. Finally, have a clear visual hierarchy using things like font size, weight, boldness, and color to prioritize the most important elements and deprioritize the less important elements.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-лист для лендинга Напарника: один CTA, без лишнего меню, минимальная форма.
