# UX Writing 101: A Simple Guide for UX Designers (With Real Examples)

**Канал:** Joana_UX Designer · 9 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/rSxiUayPWQ4

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.


---

## 1. Ошибка Slack по-человечески; пустое состояние Duolingo про возможное, а не про отсутствие
`микрокопирайтинг · маскот · онбординг · тексты` · [02:24](https://youtu.be/rSxiUayPWQ4?t=144)

**Мысль:** «Пароль не совпадает, сбросить?» даёт причину и следующий шаг; пустой экран превращается в мотивацию («готов начать серию»), а не в «нет прогресса».

**Слова автора:**

> You know that frustrating moment when you type the wrong password? Most apps hit you with something like error, authentication failed or invalid credentials, something very negative and sometimes is not helpful at all. As a designer, I used to just design an error box and call it a day. But the words inside that box, that's where the magic happens. Look at what Slack does. H, that password doesn't match the one we have on file. Want to reset it? H makes it feel human, not robotic. And it tells you exactly what's wrong. Your password doesn't match. And it immediately offers a solution with a clear next step. Reset it. Same red error box, same design pattern, but completely different user experience. All because of the words. Another example is empty states. This one changed how I approach empty states forever. Empty states are those screens when someone first opens your app and nothing is there yet. No messages, no files, no activity. I used to design empty states with a nice illustration and some text like nothing here yet or get started. Yes, it's functional. It works but is not very inspiring. Then I studied Dualingo. When you first open the app before starting any lessons, it doesn't say no progress or start learning. It shows their really cute mascot and something saying ready to start your strike. So they didn't focus on what's missing. They focus on what's possible. This taught me that empty states aren't just functional placeholders. They are opportunities to motivate, to encourage, and set the tone for the entire user experience.

**Для Напарника:** Прямо в духе проекта: пустой остров и день без плана — «ноль, а не минус»; на пустых состояниях говорить о возможном.

## 2. Сначала ясно, потом остроумно
`микрокопирайтинг · тексты` · [06:19](https://youtu.be/rSxiUayPWQ4?t=379)

**Мысль:** Если остроумный текст создаёт даже миг колебания, он не работает.

**Слова автора:**

> Tip number two is be clear first, clever second. Clarity always come first. Users should never have to guess where a button does or decode what an error message means. If your clever copy creates even a moment of hesitation is not working, use a simple and clear language to communicate with users so they can make the right decisions.

**Для Напарника:** Шутки и характер напарника в ответах — после ясного действия, не вместо него.

## 3. Предугадывай тревогу и снимай её прямо в микротексте
`микрокопирайтинг · доверие · стыд` · [07:02](https://youtu.be/rSxiUayPWQ4?t=422)

**Мысль:** Рядом с рискованным действием короткая фраза («платёж зашифрован»; «удаление нельзя отменить») успокаивает и помогает решить.

**Слова автора:**

> Tip number four is anticipate anxiety and address it proactively. This change my approach to designing forms, checkouts, and any complex interactions. Users are often anxious when they are entering personal information or making purchases or doing anything that feels risky. Your UX writing should acknowledge and calm those nerves. Instead of a form field that just says credit card number, you can add a microcopy underneath something like your payment info is encrypted and secure. This is just to make them feel confident and secure while doing that. And instead of a delete button that just says delete account, you can show exactly what happens. For example, deleting your account will permanently remove all your data. This can't be undone. So the user knows exactly what will happen and this will help them to make the right decision without feeling anxious. Think about what users might be worried about and address it directly in your copy.

**Для Напарника:** Рядом с «Начать» или «Завершить раньше» — тёплая фраза: «Можно остановиться в любой момент, это не провал».
