## 1. Идея должна быть краткой, запоминающейся и передаваемой, как мем

**Мысль:** Вторая встреча решает судьбу идеи; комикс из трёх панелей про граф контрибуций GitHub дал историю, которую пересказывали и вставляли в презентации.

**Слова автора:**

> That's why the key to effective storytelling is making sure your ideas are easily shared kind of like a meme now I know when you hear the word meme you probably think of distracted boyfriend or Futurama Fry or maybe the Drake grid but if we abstract what's really happening a meme is simply a method of distilling an idea into a format that is one concise two memorable and three sharable that's why I think we can learn a lot from this story from Kathy zang's time at GitHub I remember when I was like creating designs around the GitHub user profile it was just like me trying to explain the problems around the profile that I was observing first comic I made was this story that one of my peers who worked at getup told me where she wears her get up hoodie and she was on an airplane had her laptop open and she was like looking at her get a profile and somebody passed her on the plane and she said that they asked her oh do you work at GitHub and she was like yes and then the person was like oh but your graph it's like not very green like there's not enough commits on your contribution graph and then she just felt so gutted so my comic just like told that story in three panels I just anchored the whole like okay here's like the problem with the contribution graph and the problem it creates in the community and why people are like so polarized on it's an example of how to like condense that story into a format that maybe has the chance to meme itself people will screenshot it and include it in other presentations when they're talking about a thing that is anchoring to the same problem or same user story and then that thing just like proliferates.

**Для Напарника:** Формулировки микрокопирайта и сообщений Напарника должны быть короткими и запоминающимися: фраза вроде «это не минус, это ноль» шарится и запоминается.

## 2. Давать названия концептам: «Less but better», «Sea Peek»
`тексты · процесс · микрокопирайтинг` · [03:16](https://youtu.be/LZEh-QTim2Q?t=196)

**Мысль:** Короткое имя превращает прототип в общий словарь команды: идея разошлась и была выпущена. Названные сущности упрощают асинхронное обсуждение.

**Слова автора:**

> Memes don't have to be images either I mean deer Ram's design philosophy can be summed up in three words as a meme less but better and that's why the language that we use to present our Design Concepts is so important I think sometimes it's helpful to name your Explorations so that it's easy for people to grasp like oh this is the drawer concept and that is the sheet that's y wng who was a design L at Twitter Airbnb and now is the head of design at Maven and I remember specifically when Yen was leading a project to redesign the maven marketplace now she used data to shine a light on some potential opportunities one of the problems she wanted to solve was making it easier for students to get the gist of a course without having to navigate to what we would call the CLP how we refer to course landing pages on Maven so at our weekly demos she showed off a pretty nifty popup like this now people liked the Prototype but she did something really genius while presenting it she gave the concept A name she called it the C Peak it was perfect because her prototype was a way for students to get a quick look or dare I say a peak into a course then it's easy for them to also talk about them sure enough the idea behind seal Peak caught like wildfire because now in only three syllables anyone on the team could reference her prototype and all of the surrounding conversation data points everything from that meeting and as a result the concept of C Peak quickly gained popularity and the feature was shipped shortly after but it's not just y I talked with Alex Cornell about his time designing the linear mobile app and he shared the same tactic you're now referring to these things as named entities and you maybe sometimes it's even worth giving them a name you know this is the no menu option this is the gestural option or whatever then it gives people a vocabulary that they can use in their asynchronous message back.

**Для Напарника:** Давать имена механикам Напарника (Фокус, Дом, Мир, ориентир), чтобы единообразно говорить о них в продукте и в команде.

## 3. Побуждающий инцидент: сначала мир, потом конфликт, потом решение
`тексты · процесс` · [05:19](https://youtu.be/LZEh-QTim2Q?t=319)

**Мысль:** Запоминается история с конфликтом в первых 20%, а не список фактов и фич.

**Слова автора:**

> To stick with people then you need to nail the inciting incident the inciting incident is the single most crucial part of any story that serves one function which is to arrest your attention and the inciting incident is the moment that happens almost always within the first 20% of a film or a story that turns the Hero's World on its head it's this major opposing Force the first major piece of conflict that the hero faces and then has to take action to try and overcome to address this major upheaval now if you think about that and you care about the design and presence of the inciting incident everything else you need comes out of it so the sighting instant only matters if you create the world beforehand so if you establish the world of the story and then you need to articulate your way to a resolution stories are memorable facts and features not so much.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг: начинать с узнаваемой боли (не могу начать) до показа механик.

## 4. Микро-конфликты: эмоциональный пик, где пользователю тревожно
`исследования · процесс · СДВГ` · [07:09](https://youtu.be/LZEh-QTim2Q?t=429)

**Мысль:** Момент тревоги или перегруза в потоке можно сделать завязкой истории: сначала мир пользователя, затем конфликт, затем решение.

**Слова автора:**

> Inciting incidents exist in the micro too maybe there's an unexpected change in the data or maybe you identify an emotional high point where users feel anxious or overwhelmed in a given flow now you can use that as a way to tell a story that makes your designs more memorable first you create the user World beforehand then you really highlight that moment of conflict and then you can propose Your solution so going back to our Q3 planning meeting internalizing this moment of conflict is what empowers your product lead to effectively tell your story if you arm your person with the major piece of conflict and the way to overcome it they are more able to rearticulate that idea onwards.

**Для Напарника:** Использовать момент, где человеку с СДВГ тревожно перед стартом, как отправную точку для онбординга и текстов.

## 7. Кубик Рубика: у каждого своя сторона идеи
`процесс · исследования` · [11:32](https://youtu.be/LZEh-QTim2Q?t=692)

**Мысль:** Разные люди видят разные стороны; нужно выбирать, какую сторону показать, чтобы все поняли, что речь об одной идее.

**Слова автора:**

> Some people really cared about metrics and qualitative anecdotes from users some people really cared about the perfect pixel view of that potential product and the most helpful insight that I received from my head of design Max showning was this analogy he gave me which is people are very different they all see different sides of a Rubik's Cube you have to learn which side to show them so that they realize that when you're talking about an idea you're actually talking about the same thing.
