## 1. Найти свои продуктивные часы и не требовать работы в провал
`СДВГ · время · привычки · стыд` · [02:00](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=120)

**Мысль:** Стандартный график 8-5 не подходит всем; попытки работать в личный спад порождают стыд. Нужен эксперимент, чтобы найти окно.

**Слова автора:**

> The next tip if you do have some flexibility or some choices in how you break up your tasks find the time of day that you are most productive we're so different so some people are really productive in the morning some people are more productive in the afternoon but we all have this idea in our minds that we need to wake up first thing in the morning and get work done and just do the eight to five thing and although that's just how the system set it up it doesn't mean it's going to work for you for me I find that I am more productive between 10 a.m to 1pm but I have a major crash between two and three around that time if I do anything nothing's going to get done and I'm going to end up shaming myself for even trying to do something and feeling horrible for not being able to do it so knowing that I have to take a break between two and three is really important and this might take some trial and error so you might have to play a round of it to see when your best Focus time is you may not be aware of it.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-ин напарника может спрашивать, когда тебе легче всего, и предлагать Фокус в это окно; в провал — мягкий отдых без вины.

## 2. Body doubling: присутствие человека с делом рядом мотивирует
`body-doubling · СДВГ · мотивация` · [02:54](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=174)

**Мысль:** Просто сидеть рядом с тем, кто тоже работает, без разговоров, даёт мотивацию делать свои задачи.

**Слова автора:**

> Body doubling is simply getting somebody that also has a lot of work to do to work next to you as a warm body but you're not allowed to like engage and talk with them and like waste time you're really just there as they're working and you're working because something about having somebody next to us while they're doing their tasks feels more motivating for us to do ours.

**Для Напарника:** Кот у костра в Фокусе — именно эта метафора присутствия; подчёркивать «молча рядом, не надзирает».

## 3. Склеить неприятное дело с любимым подкастом: дело становится условием удовольствия
`привычки · дофамин · СДВГ` · [04:12](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=252)

**Мысль:** Подкаст можно слушать только за уборкой, поэтому дело превращается в способ получить интересное; нелюбимая задача идёт 4 часа.

**Слова автора:**

> Habit stacking has literally saved my life I hate cleaning like I hate organizing not because I don't like a clean space because I function best with a clean space but it also feels like there's too much to do and I get really overwhelmed so I love listening to podcasts specifically like True Crime podcasts or something really interesting about psychology I'll find a podcast ahead of time that I know has a lot of episodes that I can like fly through or want to binge on and I put that in my ears and then I start cleaning and all of a sudden it's like four hours later and I've completed everything I needed to do and it's not because it makes me like to clean but it just makes it so I don't want to stop listening to the podcast and so I want to clean because that's the only way I can also focus on the podcast because if I were to sit down and put the earbuds in I wouldn't be able to pay attention to the podcast or whatever it is I'm listening to.

**Для Напарника:** В запуске задачи напарник может предлагать «заряд» — музыку/подкаст на время шага; звук костра как фон.

## 4. Собрать все разбросанное в одну кучу: мозгу нужен один объект
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи` · [05:28](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=328)

**Мысль:** Консолидация хаоса в одно место превращает много маленьких стрессов в одну понятную задачу.

**Слова автора:**

> Another thing that's really helped me specifically when it comes to like laundry and like organization is to grab everything that you have around in your room and on the floor in the little piles that you put the organizational mess that you know and you understand but you know also stresses you out because it's everywhere pick it all up and put it in one place say on the bed now your brain Consolidated it and you just have to clean up one pile.

**Для Напарника:** В разбиении: «собери всё в одно место» как первый микрошаг для уборки.

## 5. Убрать трение: инвентарь в каждой комнате, чтобы не было отговорок
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · привычки` · [06:00](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=360)

**Мысль:** Если нужный предмет далеко, по дороге отвлечёшься на другое; реальный пример с двумя этажами и дублированием швабры.

**Слова автора:**

> Have cleaning products in each room so one thing that would like really suck for me is I have a two-story home so I would want to clean upstairs but I'm like oh the mop is downstairs the vacuum's downstairs the Clorox wipes are downstairs I have to walk down and come up and by the time I went down to do something I already found something else that interested me so things never got done so I went and I bought a separate mop for upstairs a separate box of Clorox wipes for upstairs I'm in the process of finding a new vacuum for upstairs and that way there's no excuses if it's there if it's in front of your face and you don't have to work extra hard to get the cleaning product it's going to be so much easier to just do it.

**Для Напарника:** В «Подготовке» к Фокусу: шаг «положи всё нужное рядом» до старта таймера.

## 8. Делать «на отвали» и ограничить список тремя делами
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · стыд · мотивация` · [09:57](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=597)

**Мысль:** Прокрастинация — про чувства неадекватности и перегруза в начале; ожидание плохого результата снижает барьер. Список из 15 пунктов парализует, три — нет.

**Слова автора:**

> This tip actually helped me a lot my psychiatrist gave me this tip half ass everything procrastination is not about anything other than like what you feel when you're about to start a task it's a lot about feeling inadequate feeling overwhelmed trying to avoid those feelings like when we sit down to do something and we get that Rush of oh my God this is so much that's what causes us to procrastinate so I want you to go into the mindset of I'm going to half-ass it this is going to suck I'm not expecting it to be good and just start with the expectation that it will be horrible that's how you get yourself going another thing that really helps is limit your to-do list I bet that if you looked at your to-do list right now you have at least 15 things on there and then you look at it and you're like of course I can't do anything it's so overwhelming I don't want to do any of it sit down take a breath cut it down to three things three things you want to do today.

**Для Напарника:** Фокус: старт с «сделаю плохо, и это ок»; Дом: на сегодня максимум три шага, не больше.

## 9. Выговорить все «не могу, потому что» в голосовую заметку и стартовать заново
`СДВГ · запуск-задачи · чат` · [11:14](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=674)

**Мысль:** Список отговорок крутится в голове; вынос наружу голосом быстрее дневника и освобождает голову для старта.

**Слова автора:**

> When I'm about to start something I have all these complaints about it like I can't do this because of a b c d e f g and how am I even supposed to get started because I need to do this before I can do this and that's where my mind goes I start thinking of all the reasons why I can't do that thing right now and what's really helpful is to open up your voice notes on your phone and just record all your complaints all the reasons why you can't do it all the reasons that are getting in your way get it out of your system I would tell you to journal but let's be honest as an ADHD or you probably can't keep up with journaling but voice memos are so much easier because they're just faster so get the phone out voice record you don't have to listen to it just get it out of your system and then try to start again and see if that helps you.

**Для Напарника:** В чате напарника: кнопка «выплесни все причины, почему не хочется» перед созданием первого шага.

## 10. Заранее договориться, чтобы критика не читалась как «я плохой»
`СДВГ · стыд · доверие` · [13:34](https://youtu.be/Yi1szTIJy4U?t=814)

**Мысль:** Из-за отвержения-чувствительности замечание воспринимается как приговор; договорённость заранее переводит это в рабочий сигнал.

**Слова автора:**

> Rejection sensitivity so if somebody says you interrupted me that might come to you like I'm a bad friend and a lot of Shame and you might feel really horrible about it so a way to avoid that is to have the conversation and come to an agreement beforehand so that you know hey they're not doing this because they think I'm a horrible person but we did come to the agreement that if I interrupt them or if this happens that they're going to help me kind of work through it.

**Для Напарника:** Тон напарника: любая ошибка/пропуск подаётся как нейтральный факт, а не оценка человека.
