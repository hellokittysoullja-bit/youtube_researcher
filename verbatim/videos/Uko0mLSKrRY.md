# Fastest Way to Fix a Landing Page That's Not Converting

**Канал:** Ansub Khan | Supafast · 6 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/Uko0mLSKrRY

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.


---

## 1. Пиши заголовок так, как говорит клиент, а не как говоришь ты
`тексты · лендинг · конверсия` · [00:22](https://youtu.be/Uko0mLSKrRY?t=22)

**Мысль:** Слоган читается как лозунг; фраза покупателя из второго экрана («изменения за часы вместо месяцев») должна быть в заголовке.

**Слова автора:**

> So, their headline says, "Unlock the system that powers your business." But, it's a very generic headline. Their buyers are IT people who own SAP. But, if when they're coming to their landing page, they're not getting that this is basically for them. For them, this reads like a slogan, and 5 seconds later, they will leave, because they still don't know if this thing is for them or not, if it is solving their problem or not. Now, if we scroll one section down, you will see here it says, "System changes that took 1 month, now take hours." This is the buyer statement, but it is not there. It is in the second section. I did a similar thing. I tried to redesign it, a very quick redesign on their website. And what I did here is, I just add that same line here. It says clearly, "System changes that took months, now take hours." On your page, do the same thing. Write the headline the way your customer says it, not the way you say it.

**Для Напарника:** Заголовок лендинга словами пользователя: «Не могу начать» вместо «раскрой продуктивность».

## 2. Hero из четырёх элементов по порядку: заголовок, для кого и что получит, доказательство, кнопка
`лендинг · иерархия · процесс` · [01:19](https://youtu.be/Uko0mLSKrRY?t=79)

**Мысль:** Без явного «для кого и что получит» покупатель не скроллит; проверять текст без дизайна.

**Слова автора:**

> If you strip away the design of the hero section, that leaves us with just three lines, a small label, a slogan, and a button. So, there's no line saying who's it for, what it does. It's not there, and buyers now have to scroll down to learn that. And most of them, they don't do that. A hero section that works have four things in order: a headline, who's it for and what they get, and proof, and the button. And if you see our redesign, it basically has all four of them right away. A clear heading, a clear indicator of what's happening. We're also telling them who's it for, and what they get. And now, we're also telling them who uses it, which is our proof. And a clear button which says what exactly is going to happen. So, write your hero section as a plain text before you design it. Don't worry about how it will look. Just see the text.

**Для Напарника:** Сначала написать текст героя лендинга без дизайна и проверить по этим четырём пунктам.

## 3. Показывай продукт в идеальном состоянии — момент, когда он сделал работу
`лендинг · награды · конверсия` · [02:46](https://youtu.be/Uko0mLSKrRY?t=166)

**Мысль:** Полузаконченный запрос непонятен, а результат даёт покупателю оценить, чего ожидать.

**Слова автора:**

> So, the general rule of thumb I like to follow is that we show our product in its ideal state. The moment it has done its job is its ideal state because now buyers can judge what exactly are they hoping for, and is your product is delivering that or not. That's as simple as that. Now, Cognac is literally doing that. They are showing it, but it is buried down. I now have to go to the solutions page, and now I can see how the product works, what exactly is happening, and on. This should be in the hero section itself. So, now buyers get the idea of how the product works. In the hero redesign, I didn't actually spend much time on this. I just gave a basic idea of product in its ideal state where I can ask questions. I can see the results and everything. But, it's still better than this because I now I'm getting clarity on how the product might feel like. So, your hero should show the result because that's the picture that makes them book, not some sort of prompt which is half finished and I'm not even seeing the work later on.

**Для Напарника:** Hero лендинга: готовый остров с кот и результатом сессии, а не пустой таймер.

## 4. Доказательство выше кнопки; кнопка называет, что произойдёт после клика
`микрокопирайтинг · доверие · конверсия` · [04:06](https://youtu.be/Uko0mLSKrRY?t=246)

**Мысль:** Клик требует причины, следующего шага и мягкого толчка, и порядок важен: сначала доказательство, затем просьба.

**Слова автора:**

> Button says go live with Conductor in 48 hours. And if you click on it, let me click here, and you will get redirected to their book a demo page, right? And it sits above the customer logos, so it asks before it gives you a reason to book it. Now, a click needs three reason at once. It needs a reason, a next step, and a slight nudge. And Conductor has all three of them. They have the logos, they have a demo page, they have this claim that, you know, you just need 48 hours, so it's very easy. But it's all in wrong order. In a rebuild, what exactly are we going to be doing is we are going to be flipping this order. So, in the rebuild, you can see logos are now sitting above the button. And button is saying book a live walk-through. Or it can be as simple as book a demo or something. We just need to tell people exactly where they are going to be landing. And you can also see one more thing. We also have a nudge which says set up in 48 hours, right over here. Now, the button says what you get and proof sits right above it. For your own page, make sure the proof is above the button and button names what happens after the click. You don't want buyers to now guess what exactly will happen and create that level of uncertainty among them.

**Для Напарника:** CTA лендинга: над кнопкой отзывы/цифры, на кнопке «Начать первую сессию».
