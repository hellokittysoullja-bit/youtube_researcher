## 1. Полезность, юзабилити и крафт: крафт даёт положительную эмоцию
`крафт · вкус · дизайн-система` · [01:46](https://youtu.be/tNGDgK0aMjA?t=106)

**Мысль:** Утилита и юзабилити считаются обязательными, а крафт это то, как продукт ощущается; хорошо сделанный продукт вызывает положительную эмоцию, плохой нейтральную или отрицательную. Все три вместе дают великий дизайн.

**Слова автора:**

> So let's take a big step back and talk about the qualities that go into software design we oftentimes land in three big areas utility usability and craft so utility is about whether the software actually serves a purpose is some to people usability is whether the software can actually like work and whether people can make use of the software and then there's craft is about how the product feels to use in a sign of well-crafted software is a product that elicits a positive emotional response in the person who uses it and that's in opposition to poorly crafted products which often elicit negative or at best neutral responses now what's interesting here is the VIN diagram created when you combine these three product qualities together and we can see that when all three are combined we have the chance to create a product that has great design but when we only have two factors we risk negative experiences.

**Для Напарника:** Обосновывать полировку (хаптика, анимации, голос кота) как отдельное измерение ценности, не как украшение.

## 2. Анимация должна имитировать лучший реальный опыт (дверца с доводчиком)
`анимация · крафт` · [04:01](https://youtu.be/tNGDgK0aMjA?t=241)

**Мысль:** Плавное замедление к концу и щелчок воспринимаются приятнее, чем резкое схлопывание; движение объектов софта должно повторять лучшие физические переживания.

**Слова автора:**

> Animation or the way the software objects in the software move do they snap immediately from point A to B or do they transition smoothly know when you think about animation it's it's about really mimicking an experience that exists in the real world right and ideally it's mimicking exper the best types of real world experience it like when you have a soft closed cabinet dorm you know when you close it kind of goes fast and then at the end it eases to close and then it clicks right at the end that feels amazing feels much better than a door that you just slam shut.

**Для Напарника:** Кривые easing для появления реплик, роста ориентиров и завершения таймера делать с мягким торможением и точкой «щелчка» (в паре с хаптикой на событие).

## 3. Тактильность из индустриального дизайна: будильник Apple Watch
`хаптика · крафт · дофамин` · [04:58](https://youtu.be/tNGDgK0aMjA?t=298)

**Мысль:** Вибро-отклик на тиках колёсика делает установку будильника фиджет-приятной саму по себе, даже без цели; эргономика плюс материальность переносятся в софт.

**Слова автора:**

> Now craft also brings forward the best in industrial design industrial design is about marrying ergonomics the way something feels to be used with materiality or the combinations of materials that go into the product when we think about bringing ideas from industrial design into software the result is an experience that actually feels tactile right it feels more like the real world in tactile one of my favorite examples of this is setting the alarm on the Apple watch who here has tried doing that it's a pretty awesome experience when you use the jog dial you get these like bumpy feedback vibrations as you go over the hands or go over the five minute ticks and it feels fidget you just want to play with it for its own sake even if you don't want to set the alarm.

**Для Напарника:** Хаптика на выборе длительности в пикере (hapticThreshold) уже следует этой логике; держать ощущение 'щелчков' по шагам значения.

## 4. Самый удовлетворяющий чекбокс: крафт на главном действии
`дофамин · награды · привычки · крафт · геймификация` · [06:16](https://youtu.be/tNGDgK0aMjA?t=376)

**Мысль:** Приложение привычек вложило графику, 3D, анимацию, звук и интерактивность в единственное главное действие (отметить привычку), чтобы дать дофаминовую награду и закрепить цикл. Крафт служит цели продукта.

**Слова автора:**

> Let's talk about an example of a software company that dives deep into craft now I love this story I'm not sure if anybody you saw Andy's talk here config it's he's incredible he's is just a master of crap so Andy Ellen of not boring software wrote about how they labored over and over again to design and build the world's most satisfying checkbox they did this for their habits app this app is basically meant to help people build habits right so you state what you want to do in the app and then you get to check it off but instead of just a boring checkbox they wanted to create the world's most satisfying checkbox to complete that habit because that was the entire point of the app complete a habit click the check box that was it so here's the point of that most satisfying check box they wanted to feel like a reward because when you get a reward you're releases a little bit of dopamine and once you get that hit of dopamine your mind Associates the action you took with the dopamine hit and that's the Neuroscience of habits they're simp they're simply dopamine reward Loops so if you haven't read Andy's article yet or his articles he's written a number of these they're amazing he describes using all of the dimensions of craft we discussed in building this checkbox they use graphic design 3d design motion animation sound design and of course interactivity all to make that most important checkbox feel just incredible so you get the dopamine hit the reward from clean completing the habit and you do it again and again so it's craft with a purpose and a goal in the product.

**Для Напарника:** Финал сессии Фокус и RevealIsland и есть такой «чекбокс»: все измерения крафта (анимация, звук, хаптика) собирать вокруг момента завершения, а не размазывать по экранам.

**Слова автора:**

> Hierarchy of needs right well this is the hierarchy of design qualities at the Baseline the software is functional it works great then it's reliable performant it's free of bugs then it's usable and then finally it's a pleasure to use and the way to think about this Apex T level is by inserting craft into the experience.

## 8. Планировать крафт заранее: минимальный любимый продукт вместо минимального жизнеспособного
`процесс · крафт` · [15:41](https://youtu.be/tNGDgK0aMjA?t=941)

**Мысль:** Крафт требует времени и итераций, поэтому закладывается в расписание; цель minimal lovable product, а не MVP.

**Слова автора:**

> To make a plan for it from the start craft takes time we bake it into our schedule now this is a bigger conversation but I think there's a real opportunity for us to make sure that we scope projects to attain something that is a minimal level of Love minimal lovable product versus a minimal viable product right that we're hope will Fast follow and make better and another way that we attain craft at slack is through the active prototyping now it is impossible to attain a high level of craft in one try it is the nature of craft that it requires iteration why because craft is all about refinement honing something never settling tweaking it until it's just right always having this desire to make it better.

**Для Напарника:** Делать MVP-экраны Напарника сразу с тёплым тоном и одним продуманным моментом радости.
