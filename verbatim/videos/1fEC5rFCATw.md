## 1. Сегментирование: подача кусками в темпе пользователя
`внимание · СДВГ · время · запуск-задачи` · [10:56](https://youtu.be/1fEC5rFCATw?t=656)

**Мысль:** В долговременную память пускает узкий вход (5-7 блоков); нельзя подавать слишком быстро и пачкой, нужно порциями, которыми человек управляет сам, и давать мозгу передышку.

**Слова автора:**

> This next best practice is about presenting multimedia messages in user paced segments rather than a continuous unit remember at the beginning of this presentation we said the gateway into long-term memory is limited we talked about studies in which people were asked to remember strings of numbers and repeat them back the limit being about 5 to 7 this can be improved by chunking or making sure that content is segmented what we do not want to do is present information too quickly or pack too much information together instead we want to present content in user paced segments we want to ensure we are giving learners time to process information and essentially give their brains a break organizing content into nuggets of material supports the brains ability to do the work that is required of learning or allows germane cognitive load to take effect.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики кота и шаги подавать по одному, «Дальше» по тапу; не вываливать весь разбор задачи сразу.

## 2. Сигналы экономно + когерентность: иллюстрация, а не декорация
`иерархия · внимание · крафт` · [12:27](https://youtu.be/1fEC5rFCATw?t=747)

**Мысль:** Выделение (жирный, подчёркивание) снижает нагрузку, только если применяется редко; всё, что не служит цели (фоновые звуки, лишние картинки), увеличивает нагрузку.

**Слова автора:**

> One of the easiest ways to reduce cognitive load is by signaling provides cues that highlight the organization of essential material thereby reducing extraneous cognitive load throughout the content examples of signaling include bolding underlining highlighting and organizing content into bullets one thing to note is that signaling should be used sparingly to be most defective and yet another best practice in designing to reduce extraneous cognitive load includes the coherence principle designing with the coherence principle in mind includes excluding extraneous words sounds and video everything that you include in your content should align to the established learning objectives remember illustration not decoration this includes background sound non-essential images and any additional treatment that does not somehow aligned to the learning goals.

**Для Напарника:** Согласуется с правилом CLAUDE.md: не добавлять glow/анимации без цели; один акцент на экран.
