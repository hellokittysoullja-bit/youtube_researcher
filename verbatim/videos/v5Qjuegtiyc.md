## 1. Геймификация — не очки/бейджи/лидерборды, а мотивация через базовые драйвы
`геймификация · игровой-дизайн · мотивация` · [01:38](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=98)

**Мысль:** Элементы игры сами по себе не делают игру весёлой (большинство игр скучны); сначала нужно понять, какие драйвы мотивируют человека.

**Слова автора:**

> So what is gamification? Gamification is the craft to take all those fun and exciting elements of games and pouring them into boring non-game context. Things you have to do, but you don't necessarily want to do. So when I say gamification, some of you might start thinking about what we call the PBLs, the points, badges, and leaderboards. And this is where a lot of people think, "Oh, if I just take points and put into a product or badges, it'll make it fun and exciting." And that's a big misconception. When I started in gamification in 2003, it was a very lonely passion for me. Very few people cared about it and very few people understood it. Now about 3-4 years ago, gamification became a buzzword and a lot more people and companies, organizations, schools started to care about it. But still very few people truly understand it. So if you think about what makes a game fun, if you ask gamers that, they're not going to say, "Oh, because the game has points." They'll say, "Oh, because it challenges me, it makes me use my creativity, it allows me to hang with friends, it makes me be more than who I am today." And if you think about it, every single game out there has game elements and game mechanics in them. But most games are still boring. Most games are still not engaging. So it's very naive to think that, "Oh, if I just take these game elements and mechanics from that you can find in games that are even boring and pour them into products and experiences, it'll automatically make it super fun and exciting." So what we know here is that good gamification does not start with game elements, but really starts with how it motivates our core drives.

**Для Напарника:** Остров — это не набор очков, а ощущение роста и принадлежности; избегать PBL-шаблона.

## 2. Epic Meaning: Pain Squad — ты часть команды, а не пациент с дневником
`геймификация · маскот · мотивация` · [03:52](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=232)

**Мысль:** Рамка «секретная полиция против боли» и реплики-подбадривания превращают скучную обязанность в вовлечённую роль в команде.

**Слова автора:**

> The first core drive is epic meaning and calling. This is a core drive that says you're motivated because you feel like you're part of something bigger than yourself. So then in a game, it does that often. The world's about to end and for some reason you're the only person qualified to save the world. You feel big. But there's a lot of other examples to make people feel bigger than they are. It could be used to get children with cancer in children's hospital to update their pain journals. And that's what Pain Squad does. So Pain Squad is an app that gets kids to record their pain journal so doctors can diagnose them better. And you can imagine if you're a child and you have cancer, you feel lonely. You feel like no one cares about you. You're in pain. You're not very motivated to keep up keep track of your pain journal. So the Pain Squad is an app that pretends that you're in the secret police force dedicated to hunt down this thing called pain. And the motto is keep pain in its place. Whenever there's pain two times a day, you got to record it down. But to reinforce on that epic meaning and calling, look what they do in the app. Hey rookie, welcome to Pain Squad. It's really great you're here. We need all the help we can get to help put pain in its place. You are now officially a full-fledged detective in Pain Squad. Well done. At this rate, you might even be the next chief. They just don't make them like you anymore. You truly are one of Pain Squad's best and brightest. Keep it up. But this case isn't closed yet. Your squad is still fighting. We couldn't have done it without you. It's a way to go. So now, when the child updates her pain journal, she's not just doing something because she has to. She feels like she's part of something important. A team depends on her. She's fighting with the team against this force called pain. And that motivates her. That's epic meaning and calling.

**Для Напарника:** Кот-напарник как рамка «мы вместе»: его реплики подтверждают, что человек часть общего дела (остров как общий дом).

## 3. Development & Accomplishment: короткие достижения вместо долгосрочной выгоды
`геймификация · награды · мотивация` · [05:56](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=356)

**Мысль:** Мозг плохо ценит долгосрочные выгоды, поэтому нужно показывать краткосрочный прогресс и добавлять персонажа, который празднует вехи.

**Слова автора:**

> So we all know that health and exercise is very important. But it's a health is a long-term thing and our brains are terrible at processing long-term benefits. We like short-term gratifications. So what the Nike+ does really well is it shows you short-term accomplishments. It shows you how, "Hey, you have run an extra mile compared to last week. You're improving. Your Nike+ score is getting higher." And so you feel accomplished.

**Для Напарника:** На финале сессии показывать конкретный прирост («ещё один шаг к вчерашнему») и пусть кот празднует с характером.

## 4. DragonBox: скучная математика замаскирована под кормление дракона, ребёнок «не знает», что учится

**Мысль:** Правила вводятся ступенями, сложность растёт, обратная связь вкус/«фу» и одна-три звезды мотивируют улучшать решение; цель игры — питомец.

**Слова автора:**

> DragonBox is a learning game that gets little kids between 5 to 9-year-olds to be obsessed with solving hundreds of middle school algebra questions. Now, I don't know about you, but when I was a kid, math was the ultimate school grind. Like everything else could be fun, you know, physics can be fun, science, history can be fun, but math, no. Math is not fun. Math you just sit there and you have to do it so your parents don't get mad at you. So check out how DragonBox gets this to be fun for little kids. So the premise of the game is that you have a box and inside the box there's a baby dragon. This baby dragon only wants to come out when nothing's around it so it can eat. So this is the onboarding stage, teaching the rules. These green circles you see are zeros in disguise. Zero means nothing, so you tap on it, disappears. Zero means nothing. It scans, the dragon wants to come out and eat happy. Now on stage three, it introduces some basic math principles. Positive robot and negative robot cancel each other out, become zero. Negative two and positive two cancel each other out, become zero. And now the dragon scans and the dragon wants to come out eat again. And you can see the little dragon turns from a egg to a little baby. Nice. At one point it gets pretty complex. The DragonBox becomes a X and you're trying to isolate that X. So you have see you have to do things like balance both sides of the equation, multiply everything by two. You also need to make sure the numbers are optimally solved. So no two over four. So you can see in this process, the child is trying to make sure something this is optimally solved and the dragon will eat it because it's yummy. But the other two, they're not, so dragon refuses to eat it. That those will be yuck. As you can see, it'll be yum, yuck, yuck. You know, bad numbers. Dragons don't like bad numbers. So the child only gets one star out of three. So now the child doesn't even know he's doing math. He's just trying to feed his dragon. Grow his dragon. He's like, "Oh, why don't I get three stars? I got to figure out a better way to isolate this so the dragon wants to eat everything.".

**Для Напарника:** Питомец/кот как смысл действия: микрошаги становятся «кормлением» острова; обучение правилам через игру, а не инструкции.

## 5. Opower: сравнение с соседями сэкономило $250 млн
`мотивация · исследования · метрики` · [10:18](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=618)

**Мысль:** Социальное сравнение меняет поведение, потому что все считают себя чуть выше среднего.

**Слова автора:**

> Opower is a utility SaaS company that tries to get people to lower their utility bills. And they saw that the best way to change people's behavior is to show them how their neighbors are doing. You show them their utility, how much you're using, your best neighbor, and your average neighbor. And everyone who always thinks they're somewhat a little above average, which is impossible. You can't have everyone above average. When they see that, they're like, "Oh, I need to change my behavior." So, with Opower, within a year, they saved over $250 million of utility bills. And that's a lot of electricity, over 1 terawatt.

**Для Напарника:** Не применимо: сравнение с другими даёт стыд при СДВГ; можно сравнивать только с собой прошлым («на 5 минут дольше, чем на прошлой неделе»).

## 6. Scarcity: Kickstarter — приманка, таймер, последняя миля, групповая миссия
`геймификация · тёмные-паттерны · игровой-дизайн` · [11:01](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=661)

**Мысль:** Дефицит и срочность сильны, потому что запретное желаннее; Kickstarter сочетает приз, обратный отсчёт, «осталось немного» и общий квест.

**Слова автора:**

> Want something just because you can't have it. Like if grapes are on the table, you really don't care about the grapes. But if they're on a shelf just beyond your reach, you're always thinking about those grapes, you know, can I have them? When can I have them? Are they even sweet? And Kickstarter is a great example that utilizes a lot of great game design techniques. They first dangle this great reward, this great prize in front of you, cool technology. Then they show you what we call a countdown timer, 21 days to go, you have to act now. If you don't act now, you won't get this prize. So, the sense of urgency. Then it shows you what we call the last mile drive. Oh, there's only $3,000 left, only 2,000 more and we'll get it, you'll get it. And finally, to all together, this what we call a group quest. You can only get You can only accomplish the quest when a entire team will move forward together on it.

**Для Напарника:** Осторожно: срочность и дефицит — чёрная шляпа; использовать только «последнюю милю» (ещё один шаг до рассвета на острове) без давления.

## 8. White hat vs black hat: чёрная шляпа мотивирует сильно, но оставляет плохое послевкусие
`геймификация · этика · мотивация` · [13:58](https://youtu.be/v5Qjuegtiyc?t=838)

**Мысль:** Драйвы смысла, роста и творчества дают ощущение контроля; страх потери, неопределённость и дефицит мотивируют, но несостоятельны на длинной дистанции.

**Слова автора:**

> Every single thing you do is based on one or more of these core drives. But it's also put together on octagon shape for a reason. The left side core drives are more extrinsic motivation core drives, which means you're doing these things for a goal, for a purpose, for a reward. The ones on the right are the intrinsic motivation core drives, which means you're doing it because it's just enjoyable. You don't need a reward to use your creativity. You don't need a reward to hang out with friends. And you don't necessarily need to gain anything by being in the suspense of unpredictability. In fact, most people have fun losing money in casinos. And if you notice, the core drives on the top are more positive. I call them white hat gamification techniques. So, if you're always doing something because you feel like you're being something part of being a part of something bigger than yourself, and you're growing, you're improving, and you're using your creativity, it feels very good. It feels like you're in control, you're powerful. The ones on the bottom, I call them black hat game techniques. And if you're always doing something just because you want to avoid a loss, just because you don't know what's going to happen next, or you can't have something, it's going to be very powerful in motivation, but sometimes in the long run leaves a bad taste in your mouth. And it's not a very sustainable long-term design.

**Для Напарника:** Ядро Напарника — белая шляпа (смысл, рост острова, творчество); без FOMO и страха потери стрика (согласуется с CLAUDE.md §3 про стыд).
