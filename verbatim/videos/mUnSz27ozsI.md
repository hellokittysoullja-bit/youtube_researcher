## 1. Скука это недостаток стимуляции; интерфейс может её восполнить
`маскот · вкус · мотивация · внимание` · [01:19](https://youtu.be/mUnSz27ozsI?t=79)

**Мысль:** Продукты для скучных рутинных задач забрасывают, но скука просто нехватка стимуляции, поэтому геймификация, юмор, анимация, иллюстрации и маскоты её закрывают.

**Слова автора:**

> Humans can't endure boredom for a long time which is why products that are built for non exciting repetitive tasks so often get abandoned and gathered dust on computers and phones but boredom according to psychologists is merely a lack of stimulation the unfilled desire for satisfying activity so what if we use the interface to give them that stimulation i sat with product designers here at Mack Paul who spend their waking hours designing not-so-sexy things like duplicate finders and encryption apps and they shared five seekers to more emotional you is gamification humor animation illustration and mascots.

**Для Напарника:** Кот и тёплый тон дают стимуляцию рутинным шагам, не отвлекая от задачи.

## 2. Ожидание приятного даёт дофамин заранее; Gemini 2 сделал скучную проверку файлов игрой
`дофамин · геймификация · награды · микрокопирайтинг` · [02:46](https://youtu.be/mUnSz27ozsI?t=166)

**Мысль:** Предсказуемое приятное событие запускает дофамин ещё до него; ачивки усиливают приятное чувство зачёркнутого пункта. Космическая тема совпала с аудиторией, и пасхалки заводили людей.

**Слова автора:**

> Full of cells that control the levels of dopamine one of the major neural chemicals of happiness when something enjoyable happens those neurons light up and trigger a release of dopamine into the blood but what's even better if this Pleasant event is regular and can be predicted they'll light up and release dopamine before it even happens what does that mean for your interface that expecting and enjoyable things such as the next achievement will give the users little shots of happiness throughout their experience with the product gamification and UI Gemini 2 and duolingo when designing Gemini 2 the new version of our duplicate finder from Mac we had a serious problem at hand reviewing gigabytes of files was so crushingly boring and some users complain they quit before they even were done so what we try to achieve with the achievement system is intensified the feeling of a crossed out item on a to-do list which is the only upside of tedious tasks the space theme unwittingly set with the apps name and Exploited in the interface was perfect for gamification our audience grew up on Star Wars and Star Trek so sci-fi inspired ranks would hit home with them within days of the release we started getting tweets from users asking for clues on the easter-egg that would unlock the final achievement a year after our release Gemini 2 got the Red Dot award for a design that exhibits clarity and emotion so while it's hard to measure how motivating our achievement system has been it sure didn't leave people cold.

**Для Напарника:** Тема и ориентиры острова должны совпадать с аудиторией; пасхалка-находка вызывает ожидание и ритуал возвращения.

## 3. Юмор активирует систему вознаграждения, но зависит от контекста и аудитории
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · дофамин · маскот` · [05:06](https://youtu.be/mUnSz27ozsI?t=306)

**Мысль:** Смешные картинки задействуют ту же лимбическую сеть, что еда и музыка. Но юмор субъективен: шутка на запуске и в ошибке разные вещи.

**Слова автора:**

> But the biggest power of jokes is that they're emotional while we still don't fully understand the nature of humor one thing is clear it makes humans happy according to brain imaging research funny cartoons activate the reward network in the limbic system the same network that responds to eating music sex and mood altering drugs in other words a good joke gives people a kind of emotional hi would you want that kind of reaction to your interface of course but the tricky part is not only is humor subjective but the way we respond to it depends a lot on the context one thing is throwing in a pun on the launch screen a completely different is goofing around in an error message and while all humans enjoy humor and this or that format it's vital to know your audience what they find hilarious and what might seem inappropriate crude or poorly timed not that different from cracking jokes in real life.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики напарника: мягкий юмор по поводу, без сарказма и без шуток в моменты ошибок и усталости.

## 4. Authentic Weather и Slack: рутинное действие превращается в ожидаемое
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · маскот · примеры` · [06:02](https://youtu.be/mUnSz27ozsI?t=362)

**Мысль:** Саркастичные комментарии погоды делают проверку прогноза событием, которого ждёшь. Slack выбирает безопасные добродушные каламбуры, потому что аудитория разная.

**Слова автора:**

> Authentic weather apps our prime example of utilitarian products there is something people use to get information period but with authentic weather you get a lot more than that no matter the weather it's going to crack you up with a snarky comment like it's freaking freezing go home winter and my personal favorite it's just okay look outside for more information what happens when you use authentic weather is you don't just open it for the forecast you want to see what it comes up with next and a routine tasks like checking the weather becomes a thing to look forward to in the morning now the apps moody commentary packed full of f-words and scorn what probably seem less entertaining to my mom but being the grumpy millennial that I am I find it hilarious which proves humour works if you know your audience another interface that puts fun to good use is slacks for an app people associate with work emergencies select as a solid job creating a more humane experience not least because of its one-liners from loading screens to the moments you're finally caught up with all your chats it cracks a joke when you don't see it coming with such a diverse demographic humor is a hidden miss so slack plays safe with goofy puns and good-natured banter the kind of jokes that don't exactly send you rolling on the floor but don't annoy or offend either in the best case scenario the user will chuckle and share the screen shot in one of their channels in the worst case scenario they'll just roll their eyes.

**Для Напарника:** Ежедневное приветствие кота как маленькая приятная неожиданность; тон добрый и безопасный, не язвительный.
