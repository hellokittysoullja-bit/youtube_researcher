## 1. Понимание не значит низкую нагрузку: усталость не видна в ошибках
`исследования · внимание · юзабилити` · [00:46](https://youtu.be/TaM8urQyZaM?t=46)

**Мысль:** Пользователь может успешно завершить задачу и всё равно нести скрытую нагрузку, которая истощает внимание; нужно измерять нагрузку напрямую (think-aloud), а не только по успеху.

**Слова автора:**

> So as ux designers it's easy to assume that if users appear to understand what we've created then we succeeded however we need to remember that cognitive load still applies even when comprehension seems high users May indicate that they grasp the workflow they may have succeeded in completing a task but still The Experience they may be experiencing significant cognitive burden The Strain may not always be manifest in obvious errors according to cognitive load Theory their mental resources are still taxed in inefficient ways that deplete attention and engagement the design choices we view a straightforward could be imposing this unseen load that's gradually fatiguing your user this speaks to the importance of measuring cognitive load directly through techniques like thinkaloud protocols rather than just relying on feedback so we should validate that our designs optimize total mental effort and not just the apparent understanding by digging deeper into cognitive responses we can create experiences that truly minimize every form of load.

**Для Напарника:** При тестировании Напарника смотреть не только на завершение, но и на колебания и усталость у людей с СДВГ.
