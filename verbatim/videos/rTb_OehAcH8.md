## 2. Лёгкие «да» сначала, аккаунт потом; прогресс-бар; «Продолжить с» вместо «Регистрация»
`онбординг · активация · запуск-задачи · микрокопирайтинг` · [07:19](https://youtu.be/rTb_OehAcH8?t=439)

**Мысль:** Поток без навигации с индикатором прогресса и вопросами про человека создаёт ощущение персонализации; «sign up» звучит как обязанность, «continue with» как естественное продолжение.

**Слова автора:**

> You don't want any navigation you want to make sure the user is primarily focused on completing this flow and you can see things like progress bars work really well I've seen down here having some social proof here you're really engaging the users and making them feel like you're going to personalize the product to their needs so it's a win-win situation it's a win for you because you get data on your users it's a win for the users because they feel like you are customizing that experience for them and lastly it's a win for you once again because you're going to convert more of your users all right let's get started which one of these best describes you I'm just starting or I'm already selling online or in person next where would you like to sell next and I love how the progress bar is moving like oh I'm almost done perfect what do you plan on selling first so you're getting some Intel on your users and your users are feeling like whoa Shopify is going to probably customize this experience for me awesome and then the US region create an account I like how they ask for everything before they asked you to create the Shopify account I've seen that work really well on the app site and the website as well and then boom I sign up now I'm more inclined to sign up right because I've already given you some info so ask for some easy wins easy yeses first and then ask them for something that is more interesting now what I've also seen I'm going to stop here Shopify create a Shopify account I love this but I would say continue with rather than sign up we have seen that work really well on the app side because sign up feels like a burden or it's more work whereas continue with just seems like just rolling along you know passing along and signing up.

**Для Напарника:** Онбординг: сначала простые вопросы про человека, мини-прогресс; аккаунт (если появится) формулировать как «продолжить».

## 3. Текст на кнопке решает: «Начать» вместо «Подписаться» (+30%), «Открой» вместо общего (+12,5%)
`микрокопирайтинг · конверсия · тексты` · [09:28](https://youtu.be/rTb_OehAcH8?t=568)

**Мысль:** Никто не хочет «подписываться», все хотят «начать»; результат-ориентированная формулировка кнопки поднимает клики.

**Слова автора:**

> Test your call to actions these are the buttons on your website these are the buttons on your homepage these are the buttons for you to sign up right think about where all the places users can drop off on and then really start testing that particular page here's a website this is a figma file that I found online so thank you to the figma community but what we did was we AB tested for one of our clients that had a web platform as well his pricing page and we AB tested the call to actions so he originally had subscribe and then we're like hey let's test get started as an example ex Le now I'll give you a second again guess which one won A or B get started or subscribe it was a get started 30% increase in sales when we change that button from subscribe because nobody wants to subscribe for crry out loud to get started that simple change LED to 30% increase in sales that's what I'm talking about when it comes to call to action furthermore you can do that on the app side of things friend of mine from Blue throne. tested this as well they had get spot to Stats plus that's a hard thing to say or they also said get all features unlock spotted stats plus unlock everything unlock lifetime insights let's explore stats which I pretty much hate but guess which one won for this one 1 two 3 four five it was number four unlock lifetime insights that led to a 12.5 increase in click-throughs and conversion right that's what I mean these simple textual changes on your buttons can make a huge impact and they're so easy to implement.

**Для Напарника:** Кнопки Напарника — глагол начала («Сядем рядом», «Начать шаг»), а не «Подтвердить/Отправить».

## 4. Точное «нечётное» число правдоподобнее круглого (4 173 вместо 4 200)
`доверие · тексты · лендинг` · [16:42](https://youtu.be/rTb_OehAcH8?t=1002)

**Мысль:** Конкретная цифра воспринимается как реальная, круглая как оценка.

**Слова автора:**

> You have social proof here with some odd numbers this isn't 10,000 apps or 10,000 people foreigners right it is 4,173 when you have a weird number like that or an exact number rather than 4,200 people it's more believable.

**Для Напарника:** На лендинге использовать реальное точное число (например, «412 сессий за эту неделю»), только если оно подлинное.
