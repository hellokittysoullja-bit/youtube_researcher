## 2. Рабочий прототип плюс видео-разбор убеждают в десять раз сильнее сообщения в Slack
`процесс · тексты` · [02:21](https://youtu.be/gZvsoS4XGuc?t=141)

**Мысль:** Видео, где автор объясняет проблему и показывает убеждённость, делает идею убедительнее; опрос показал видео почти вдвое чаще ссылки Figma.

**Слова автора:**

> In this case sending someone a working prototype is 10 times more compelling than presenting the idea as a slack message but code isn't the only thing at your disposal the defining moment in Vincent's Journey for getting bu in came when he filmed a video to walk through the Prototype and get people excited about the idea I figured that the prototype by itself wouldn't be enough what are you going to do are you going to send people a link so you have do trusted people like understand the Prototype your narration so one thing I did in addition to the Prototype was create this video around it where I'm basically going through the Prototype and I'm basically pitching the idea of hey we should focus on search instead and here are all the three ways that would truly enrich like you know fig Mas product I'll say it again design is sales and it's a big reason why video has quickly become the go-to way that I share my work internally when people can hear you talk about the problem and why you have so much conviction in this specific idea it's so much more compelling.

## 3. Крючок: первые фразы описывают знакомую боль в конкретном моменте
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · внимание` · [05:09](https://youtu.be/gZvsoS4XGuc?t=309)

**Мысль:** Видео стало вирусным внутри компании, потому что начиналось с узнаваемой ситуации («почти 5 вечера, дедлайн, не можешь найти нужный экран»).

**Слова автора:**

> I want you to pay attention to the first couple of sentences in the video that he shared internally in figma it's almost 5:00 p.m. and you're on a design deadline and you're scrambling P make cut but you just can't find the freaking design SCE you're looking for he asked your co-workers on Flack where the design spiles are but they don't know and now you just make sure that lie your entire career is gone that my friends is a hook.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг и онбординг Напарника: открывать конкретной сценой («сел за дело, а голова не включается»), а не абстрактным обещанием.
