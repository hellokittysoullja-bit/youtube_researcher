## 1. Кнопка «Отправить» — формальная; попросить ИИ 10 вариантов с меньшим усилием
`микрокопирайтинг · ИИ · тексты` · [07:06](https://youtu.be/54vpeQBiP4w?t=426)

**Мысль:** Подпись «Submit» звучит как налоговая форма; варианты вроде «показать план» меняют восприятие усилия и риска.

**Слова автора:**

> Let me make this concrete with the smallest possible example. Let's say you have a button that says submit. That's a very common button label. It might be the default label in your own template. It's also not very motivating. Submit sounds like something you do on a tax form. So you ask AI give me 10 alternatives that reduce perceived effort and increase motivation without sounding hypey. And you might get things like, get my estimate, see my options, start my free analysis, show me the plan. Some of those will be wrong for your situation. Some will be too promotional, some won't fit your brand. But suddenly you're thinking about that button. From the customer's perspective, what does the customer get? How much effort does this imply? Does it feel risky? Or does it feel like the next easy step? That's behavioral science applied to a tiny bit of copy.

**Для Напарника:** Подписи кнопок Напарника — через результат («Начать на 25 минут»), не «Отправить/Сохранить».

## 2. Сначала критика, потом создание: ИИ как машина перспективы
`ИИ · тексты · процесс` · [08:01](https://youtu.be/54vpeQBiP4w?t=481)

**Мысль:** Вместо «напиши письмо» просить разобрать черновик глазами клиента: где запутается, будет давление, скука или раздражение.

**Слова автора:**

> What we need is psychologically smarter communication. And AI can help with that, especially when you ask it to critique before you create. That's a key distinction. So let me say it again. Critique before you create. Most people use AI by saying, write me an email. Sometimes that works. More often it produces something grammatical and bland. A better approach is to say, here's my draft. Analyze it from the customer's point of view. Where will they feel confused, skeptical, pressured, bored, or annoyed? That question changes everything.

**Для Напарника:** Прогонять реплики кота через критику «где здесь стыд или давление?» перед выпуском.

## 3. Корпоративный тон: «приносим извинения» слышится как «не наша вина»
`тексты · стыд · доверие` · [09:53](https://youtu.be/54vpeQBiP4w?t=593)

**Мысль:** Юридически верные фразы звучат бездушно; сообщение может быть фактически правильным и эмоционально провальным.

**Слова автора:**

> The problem is that most customer communication gets written from the company's point of view. Operations weighs in. Legal weighs in. Finance weighs in. By the time the message goes out, it's accurate, it's approved, it's compliant. And it's completely devoid of human warmth. You've seen this kind of message a thousand times. "We regret any inconvenience." The customer hears. We know you're upset, but we're not really taking responsibility due to circumstances beyond our control. The customer hears, not our fault. "We appreciate your understanding." The customer hears, you don't actually have a choice, but we'd like you to be pleasant about it. A message can be factually correct and emotionally disastrous.

**Для Напарника:** Реплики кота должны быть человеческими, без казённого «Ошибка/неверно»; тон ошибок — в его характере.

## 4. Убеждение — это снижение сопротивления, не давление сильнее
`запуск-задачи · СДВГ · мотивация` · [13:01](https://youtu.be/54vpeQBiP4w?t=781)

**Мысль:** Лучший ход часто — убрать причину колебаний: путаницу, неопределённость, недоверие, усилие, страх ошибиться.

**Слова автора:**

> It ties everything together. A lot of persuasion isn't about pushing harder, it's about reducing resistance. This was a central idea in my book Friction, and it carries straight into this AI conversation. Marketers tend to think persuasion means adding more benefits, more urgency, more claims, more influence triggers. Sometimes that's right. But often the better move is to remove the thing that's causing hesitation in the first place. Confusion. Uncertainty. Distrust. Effort. The fear of making a mistake. The feeling that the company doesn't understand me. AI is really good at helping you find these resistance points. You can ask it what objections a skeptical customer might have. You can ask what's unclear, what feels risky? What would make someone stop before clicking? Those questions are useful for sales copy, sure, but they apply to almost everything. A donation page, a renewal notice, an onboarding flow, a pitch deck, a customer apology. The same human brain is on the other end, and that brain has limited attention, avoids effort, scans before it reads, is sensitive to fairness, and really hates uncertainty. This is where behavioral science and empathy come together. Persuasion isn't just about getting someone to say yes. It's about understanding why they might say no, or do nothing, or get angry, or lose trust, and then designing the experience so the right action feels easier, clearer, safer, and more aligned with what they already care about.

**Для Напарника:** Старт сессии: убирать трение (меньше полей, предзаполненные значения), а не добавлять мотивационные слова.

## 5. Три вопроса к сообщению: эмоция, трение, честная переработка
`тексты · ИИ · этика` · [15:26](https://youtu.be/54vpeQBiP4w?t=926)

**Мысль:** Какую эмоцию создаёт текст, где трение или недоверие, как переписать яснее и теплее без преувеличений и сокрытия правды.

**Слова автора:**

> Three questions. First, what emotion is this likely to create in the reader? Second, where does it create friction, confusion, distrust, or resistance? Third, how can it be rewritten to be clearer, more persuasive, and more empathetic without exaggerating or hiding the truth? That last phrase matters. Without exaggerating or hiding the truth. Good persuasion is not deception. Good persuasion helps people understand value, reduce uncertainty, make better decisions with less effort.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-лист для всех текстов кота: без обмана и пафоса, с одним ясным следующим шагом.
