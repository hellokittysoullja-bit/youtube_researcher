## 3. Дизайн-система это продукт: знание не равно использованию
`дизайн-система · процесс · исследования` · [14:53](https://youtu.be/yPLY-FFf7OY?t=893)

**Мысль:** Нужно знать пользователей системы, оставлять место для итераций, учитывать разную зрелость пользователей; система полезна, только если реально используется.

**Слова автора:**

> We've learned that we need to treat scapa as a living and breathing product and never make the mistake of thinking that one solution will fit everyone more or less like all products right there's no difference between this and a regular product so to make your design system a success you need to know your users and you need to understand what impacts their work the most their needs they will inform your roadmap and be the base for understanding the efforts needed to execute on it and you're not going to get it right the first time trust us you're not so make sure that you leave room for iteration and encourage your users to give feedback that's very important because without your users you don't get the right system and the second thought is that a d design system needs to be relevant to your users to make the most of this you need to understand not all users have the same level of maturity this is something that we learned the hard way as we thought that our designers would just pick this up and be happy with it turns out that it's not as simple some will need more support than others and planning out how to best support the rollout of your design system is equally as important as advocating for it and making people understand that it exists so knowledge is not the same as usage and the design system is only as useful if it as it's as it is and it's only useful if it's actually being used across your whole organization.
