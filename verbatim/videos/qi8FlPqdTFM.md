## 1. EAST: Easy — дефолт и снижение трения
`привычки · онбординг · конверсия` · [00:33](https://youtu.be/qi8FlPqdTFM?t=33)

**Мысль:** Предвыбранный вариант требует нулевых усилий, а люди склонны к инерции; любое неудобство, даже небольшое, непропорционально снижает долю выполнивших.

**Слова автора:**

> So the E of East stands for easy and that's because if you want your behavioral intervention to work it should be easy for people to use it and easy for people to do I don't know about you but when the company or organization tries to get me to do something there's no way that I'm going to do it unless it only takes me a few clicks or a few seconds so what are some Behavioral Science principles that you can use to make your intervention easy what I'm going to give you two and I'm going to be giving you two for each of the four different elements so the first way you can make a behavioral intervention easy is by setting a default so a default is a pre-selected option that if people don't do anything they end up going with for example pre-selecting accepting terms and conditions or pre-selecting which type of plan they want to subscribe to on your service defaults are really good ways of making an intervention easy because they literally require zero effort from the customer or the person that you're trying to nudge and as a result of that since most people go through inertia or most people tend to just go with the status quo whatever's been preset for them having a default is a very powerful way to make your nudge effective easy peasy living squeezy the second Behavioral Science principle you can use is reducing friction this is basically a behavioral science term for hassle we want to make our intervention as little hassle as possible so ask yourself these questions when designing it do customers have to call up somebody in order to do this action do they have to visit a branch in real life is it very difficult for them to find the specific web page that they need in order to do the action that you want them to all of these things add extra Hassle and inconvenience to the people you're trying to nurture and as a result of that any inconvenience even a small amount will actually have a disproportionate effect on how many people do that behavior so try and reduce as much hassle or friction as possible.

**Для Напарника:** Дефолт 25 минут «рекомендуем» подтверждён принципом; убирать лишние поля и шаги из запуска сессии.

## 2. EAST: Timely — нудж в нужный момент и мгновенные награды против present bias
`уведомления · награды · удержание` · [04:34](https://youtu.be/qi8FlPqdTFM?t=274)

**Мысль:** Нудж перед оплатой в McDonald's срабатывает, потому что человек уже готов; present bias требует немедленной награды в первые использования, как в Headspace с оттоком в первые недели.

**Слова автора:**

> Is important because an intervention that comes at just the right time is going to be far more effective than an intervention that comes when people aren't even considering that decision one example that I've talked about on this channel before but I think is worth mentioning again is this nudge from McDonald's so when you go to the checkout at McDonald's right before you pay it nudges you to round up your order and donate that rounded up amount to the Ronald McDonald charity this is a very well times note it's right there when you're about to pay you've already got your payment device out and as a result of that it makes it very easy the first part of East in order to perform the action so by making it timely you tend to inherently make it easier for people as well and you also make them more receptive to your message I might not be calm I might not be present I might not be good but I will be on time and another way to make something timely is to consider what are the immediate benefits and rewards for people because human beings have something called president a bias or in more layman's terms just short-term gratification we tend to Value things that we can get right now or in the very near future compared to things that only pay off in the long run so if I am designing an app for example and I want users to use my app for a long time in order to reap the benefits of using it then I need to program some kind of immediate reward for them I need to consider what will users get when they first use the app and for the first few times they use it how can I reward them for using the app those first few times even if the biggest benefits only come in the long run one example of this that I've encountered in the wild is headspace is the meditation app and Claire Purvis who's the head of Behavioral Science over at headspace talked about how she was struggling with this issue where users of headspace only get the real benefits of meditation after they've been meditating for quite a while however they saw enormous drop-off in users during those first few weeks of them using the app because while it wasn't very rewarding for them to meditate those first few times so if you want your behavioral intervention or just your service to be more effective you need to consider the immediate rewards for using your service.

**Для Напарника:** Первая сессия должна давать немедленную награду (кот, росток на острове), не дожидаясь долгосрочной пользы; напоминания только в подходящий момент.
