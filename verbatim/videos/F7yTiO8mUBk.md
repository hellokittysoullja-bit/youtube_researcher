## 1. Дизайн-система нужнее всего, когда не знаешь, каким будет продукт
`дизайн-система · процесс · крафт` · [02:16](https://youtu.be/F7yTiO8mUBk?t=136)

**Мысль:** Ценность не только в стандартизации, но и в скорости: небольшой набор базовых компонентов (кнопки, сетка, типографика, цвет) даёт гибкий фундамент, а не жёсткий.

**Слова автора:**

> We often fixate on standardization as the main value prop of Design Systems and through that lens it's easy to view it as the thing that you do once you've figured out the UI and you're ready to codify it but don't forget about speed I'll suggest that Design Systems are most valuable when you don't know what the product will be that's why they're the perfect first steps for startups which at first might seem a little bit counterintuitive but that's why I want to shine a light on perplexity and how their founding designer and now head of design Henry modet thought about it any startup that I've built over the last couple years like that's always the first thing I built because that lets me go fast investing early in a set of core components minimizes the number of knobs that you need to turn in order to itate you're laying a Nimble foundation not a more rigid one the key is laser focusing on the atomic level that way you're never over architecting the system You're simply giving yourself an open-ended box of Legos that you can use to assemble uis more quickly.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник живёт в быстрых итерациях: держать компактный набор базовых токенов и компонентов, не раздувая систему.

## 2. Базовые элементы почти одинаковы везде: две-три высоты кнопок, 40 и 48 пикселей
`дизайн-система · процесс · крафт · отступы` · [05:49](https://youtu.be/F7yTiO8mUBk?t=349)

**Мысль:** Ядро системы повторяется от продукта к продукту, поэтому нет причин откладывать: не нужен product-market fit.

**Слова автора:**

> You don't need product Market fit to confirm that you'll probably have two to three button sizes and they'll almost certainly include 40 and 48 pixels if you're designing for web I even double checked A system that I made over 5 years ago and sure enough like the core elements are almost identical to the system that I most recently designed from Maven so don't overthink it I'm working on a new product right now and there's just no reason to delay building out these core components and colors and typography it's an easy way to explore more ideas more quickly.

**Для Напарника:** Опорные размеры кнопок (40/48 px) и тап-таргеты фиксировать в токенах с самого начала.

## 3. Toolkit как ящик конструктора, а не связанная система
`дизайн-система · процесс · крафт` · [06:45](https://youtu.be/F7yTiO8mUBk?t=405)

**Мысль:** Пока определяешь сложные организмы и связи между компонентами, начинаешь строить систему и скатываешься по скользкому склону. Пусть компоненты будут отдельными кубиками.

**Слова автора:**

> What do we call it then well I'm orbiting around a term that rapael used to refer to his system for KRON yeah definitely the design system like pretty much from the GetGo maybe more like a design tool kit like design tool kit feels like a little lighter weight kind of components I can kind of reuse design toolkit somehow it kind of feels fresh compared to UI kit although the extra syllable isn't exactly ideal but I like how it feels more like an open-ended box of Legos than a connected system and I think that's the most important line to draw when thinking about the right level of system to create for your company startups can't afford to Main contain anything other than the core product and as soon as you start defining more complex organisms and worrying about the relationship between components you start building a system and that's where the slope gets pretty slippery so instead of focusing on the connectivity of your system think of components as a box of one-off Legos that you can use to more quickly assemble interfaces I wanted to just give myself a toolbox so that not knowing exactly what the product would be or how it would work and make it very easy to assemble that product experience in my opinion the pendulum has swung way too far with Design Systems I believe every product can benefit from a set of really simple components and that investing in that toolkit upfront is one of the more High Roi decisions.

**Для Напарника:** Не усложнять компонентную библиотеку Напарника связностью раньше времени; компонентов хватает для трёх экранов.
