## 2. Тени в тёмной теме: чёрные с настроенной прозрачностью, серые дают свечение
`цвет · тёмная-тема · крафт` · [02:33](https://youtu.be/JNhG2T6dGOY?t=153)

**Мысль:** Серая тень на тёмном фоне выглядит как свечение; близкая к чёрной с прозрачностью около 25% читается как настоящая тень и добавляет объёмность.

**Слова автора:**

> Chances are if you're working in lighter designs you're used to working with either the shade of the background color itself or using gray and that's not going to cut it when we're working with dark mode let me demonstrate if we select the actual background itself we'll go to our effects and we'll set a shadow inside there and we'll set this to be a mid-gray like we're pretty much used to doing and we'll adjust the parameters for this as well to give a more natural looking shadow you can see straight away that looks kind of strange we now get this sort of glow effect around it as opposed to a shadow effect so we need to go ahead and adjust that so let's go back in let's select this one more time choose our background come into our drop shadow and let's set this to be totally black or pretty close to it we're gonna go right the way down we're dealing with 25 opacity in this example but you could adjust that to taste to get a stronger or lighter effect whatever you think works but you'll find as soon as you set that to black now we get a much more natural looking shadow the whole design starts to look a little bit more 3d and we just get a more realistic looking layout so when you're working with dark mode and you're working with shadows go for black or close to black and adjust the opacity to taste to give you a more realistic looking shadow in your designs.

**Для Напарника:** Для карточек над AppBackdrop использовать почти чёрные тени низкой непрозрачности, а свечения оставлять только для событийных наград.

## 4. Яркий акцент на тёмном «дрожит»: осветли его, пока эффект не уйдёт
`цвет · тёмная-тема · доступность · нейро` · [06:00](https://youtu.be/JNhG2T6dGOY?t=360)

**Мысль:** Сильные вибрирующие цвета на тёмном фоне создают эффект дрожания; чуть более светлая версия акцента решает проблему.

**Слова автора:**

> Using those in darker designs can create a kind of strange shaking effect it's kind of difficult to emulate on screen right now for this youtube video but you've probably noticed it yourself or you will notice it and if you wonder how to deal with it's actually relatively simple and straightforward you can see i've got this color strip on the right hand side that i've created the first color is my initial accent color the rest are lighter shades of the same color and also you could go the opposite way darker shades depend upon your use case to create something like this very easily i use a free tool called coolers all i do is insert my base accent color inside any of these and then what i can do is i can go up to the view shades option and this will then show me all the shades from white through to black from my initial color you can see in the middle there's my starting point and then what i'll tend to do is either jump up or down two shades a time copy those over and create a palette of gradient based colors this gives me a nice color palette that i can work with and to avoid that shaky effect the easiest thing to do is often take a look at your accent color and then make it slightly lighter until that effect goes away.

**Для Напарника:** Проверить насыщенный тёплый акцент на ночном фоне на вибрацию и при необходимости осветлить или десатурировать.
