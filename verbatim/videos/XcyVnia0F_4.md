## 1. «Зона большого пальца» — миф: 120 млн касаний показали смену хватов
`мобильный · исследования · доступность` · [03:41](https://youtu.be/XcyVnia0F_4?t=221)

**Мысль:** Анализ более 120 млн реальных касаний показал, что меньше половины пользуются телефоном одной рукой, а хват постоянно меняется; единой фиксированной зоны достижимости нет.

**Слова автора:**

> Hoover and his fellow researchers didn't just ask a few people in a lab. They analyzed over 120 million actual touch events from thousands of people using their phones in the real world walking down the street, sitting in a cafe, riding the bus. This is some serious data. So, what did all that data show? Well, first off, that central idea behind the thumb zone that we're all using our phones one-handed is just not true. Less than half of us are actually holding our phone that way at any given time. And just think about how you use your phone throughout the day. You cradle it, holding with one hand and pointing with the other. You type with both thumbs. You prop it up. The reality is we are constantly and without even thinking about it shifting between all these different grips to fit whatever we're doing. So what does that mean? It means the whole idea of one single fixed easyto-reach zone is a total fantasy. Our reach and what's easy is changing every single time we shift our grip.

**Для Напарника:** Не прятать важное в нижнем углу по расчёту «зоны пальца»; опираться на реальные данные использования Напарника.

## 2. Главное в центре экрана, вторичное по краям, редкое в углах
`мобильный · иерархия · крафт` · [04:47](https://youtu.be/XcyVnia0F_4?t=287)

**Мысль:** Чаще и точнее всего нажимают в центр; основная кнопка и контент идут в центр, табы и поиск на края, редко нужное (настройки) в углы.

**Слова автора:**

> This brings us to the biggest bombshell from all the research. When you look at where we actually tap most often and with the most accuracy, it isn't the bottom of the screen. It's the center, right in the middle. I mean, think about it. You instinctively scroll a link or a button to the middle of your screen before you tap it. That is the real prime real estate on your phone. Okay. So, if the thumb zone is dead, what are the new rules? What does all this mean for designing apps that actually feel good to use? apps that are designed for human hands and not for a little computer mouse. The new framework is actually beautifully simple and it completely flips the old model upside down. Your most important stuff, the main content, the primary buttons, that goes right in the center where we're already looking and where we tap the most accurately. Secondary things like tab bars or search, they go on the edges. And the corners, which are actually the hardest places for us to tap, are saved for things you rarely need, like a settings menu. This table just sums up the whole mindbending shift perfectly. With a mouse, the edges were magic. With our fingers, the center is king and the edges are awkward.

**Для Напарника:** Фокус: главная кнопка (Старт, Готово) ближе к центру; AppNav остаётся вторичным краем, настройки не выносить на видное место.
