## 1. Focusmate: чужой человек лучше друга, потому что нет соблазна болтать
`body-doubling · СДВГ · внимание` · [00:43](https://youtu.be/RWNopBhKjks?t=43)

**Мысль:** Видео включено, звук выключен, заявили цель и сразу работают; присутствие удерживает, а с незнакомцем нет света для болтовни.

**Слова автора:**

> To book a virtual co-working session with a complete stranger now i'll give it to you it sounds a little bit weird at first but it's incredibly effective it takes advantage of a popular concept in the adhd world these days called body doubling so the system automatically pairs you with another human being at your scheduled time so you log on at your scheduled time and you both have your video on and then you say what you're going to work on and you go on mute but because you've left your video on it's sort of like a study session or even just being in a coffee shop with a friend where you're both working independently but just the presence of the other person there has you stay focused and i'll go even further to say that it's actually a little bit better than working with a friend a lot of the time because with a friend the temptation to talk and to chat is a lot higher whereas with a stranger on focus meet you're on basically a one-to-one video call with this person and you don't really have much to chat about and they probably want to get to work so you just say what you're both gonna work on you say hi and then you just dive right in but you leave your camera on and if you're gonna step away to go the bathroom you type brb in the chat.

**Для Напарника:** Кот как «тихий напарник»: никаких разговоров, но видимое присутствие; сказать цель в начале сессии.

## 2. Забронировать сессию на потом — и можно расслабиться до неё
`body-doubling · запуск-задачи · время` · [02:13](https://youtu.be/RWNopBhKjks?t=133)

**Мысль:** Запланированный слот с обязательством превращает «не хочу» в «ок, через час»; снимает давление до старта.

**Слова автора:**

> The first situation is when i want to do something but i also kind of don't want to do it a classic time for me is sundays on sundays i have a standing task to do week planning for the upcoming week but what happens is i drag myself out of bed sunday morning and i of just want to do nothing however i've discovered that my weeks tend to be a lot worse when i don't have a plan going in and planning monday morning doesn't really give me the same benefit what to do what to do focusmate to the rescue do i have sweaty pits so i hop on and pretend today is sunday maybe i booked myself a focusmate session at 11 so then i tell myself okay great it's a 50 minute session coming up at 11 i can relax until then and i know that'll be a sufficient amount of time to get my butt in a chair and get me to plan out my week.

**Для Напарника:** В Доме: «запланировать Фокус на 11:00» как мягкое обязательство; кот будет рядом.

## 3. Длинный свободный блок: бронируй слот в его начале, чтобы запустить
`body-doubling · запуск-задачи · время` · [03:10](https://youtu.be/RWNopBhKjks?t=190)

**Мысль:** Четыре свободных часа без структуры уходят в прокрастинацию; один ранний слот запускает работу.

**Слова автора:**

> The other situation in which i religiously rely on focus me is when i am lucky enough to have a big block of open time to do desk and creative work unfortunately with my adhd brain if i have four hours open on my calendar to do desk work i am going to procrastinate the crap out of it this is again where focus mate comes to the rescue i book a slot early in that long block of things to do and that gets me to at least get started on the work if desired i can also book additional sessions in that window.

**Для Напарника:** Предлагать короткий первый Фокус в начале длинного окна, дальше сессии по желанию.

## 4. Body doubling работает и для недеск-дел: медитация, готовка, уборка
`body-doubling · привычки · СДВГ` · [04:03](https://youtu.be/RWNopBhKjks?t=243)

**Слова автора:**

## 5. Ритуал сессии: приветствие, заявка цели, мьют, колокол, разбор
`body-doubling · онбординг · награды` · [07:08](https://youtu.be/RWNopBhKjks?t=428)

**Мысль:** Структура из приветствия, цели, работы, звонка и короткого итога делает сессию предсказуемой и закрепляет достижение.

**Слова автора:**

> They give you some very simple guidelines on how to use the tool for instance when you start a session you greet the other person in a friendly way and then you both share what you're going to work on during the session and then you ask if you're both good going on mute which i always like to do and then you just get to work then when the session ends there'll be a very pretty bell that was a terrible bell and you both take a minute to debrief what you got done how the session was and thank the other person for working with you.

**Для Напарника:** Фокус: начало с фразы цели, конец со звуком и тёплым разбором «что сделали»; Peak-End.
