## 1. Мост ценности: от любопытства к убеждению до просьбы о коммитменте
`онбординг · конверсия · доверие · активация` · [00:10](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=10)

**Мысль:** Цепочка: совпадение с проблемой, ясность результата, личная релевантность, доверие плюс момент (социальное доказательство: никто не хочет быть первым); если звено сломано, платёж не конвертирует.

**Слова автора:**

> So, the big idea of how we get there is to turn your onboarding from a tutorial to an actual value bridge. So, you really want to move the user from curiosity to belief before you ask for your commitment. So, the mistakes I've found those will make is when they're first building their iOS app, they're going to shoot up a payroll. That's a mistake because although they've downloaded your app, they don't really know if it's going to be valuable to them. As you really want to see them go through the flow of what is this? Is this app for me? Is it going to provide any benefits? Am I going to get any use out of it? And if we can guide them in a way that gets them to say, "Hey, I want this result." They're going to be much more inclined to actually pay for that product. And so, if we were to look at the overall conversion chain, we can see there are some steps that we want to be paying attention to. The first one is the problem match. This is match what either they searched for in the App Store or what they saw in your organic post, we first need to establish that connection. Let's say they're a 65-year-old person and all of a sudden the first screens of the app are targeting someone who's Gen Z, there's going to be that disconnect. So, you want to make sure we're reinforcing that there. The next one is outcome clarity. What are they actually going to get when they subscribe to their app? Next up is personal relevance. Is it something that the user is actually going to use? They're going to be deciding this throughout the onboarding process. So, if we can personalize the onboarding in any way to make it extremely relevant for their specific use case or pain point, we want to be trying to do that. Next up is trust plus momentum. Here, we'll have a lot of apps including reviews and social proof. So, people can say that they're not the first. Nobody really wants to be the first one to pay and use something that's not very good. But if there are thousands, millions of people who have done it before them, they're going to feel so much more safety when subscribing and paying for your product. The perfect example is just when you're walking around person. Would you rather go to the restaurant that has the really big line or one where nobody is in it? And if we break any of these steps, your paywall is going to get colder and convert less.

**Для Напарника:** Просьбу о чём-то серьёзном (платёж, регулярность) задавать только после того, как человек увидел пользу на деле: сначала первая сессия, потом всё остальное.

## 2. Не тур по функциям: «тысяча песен в кармане» вместо характеристик iPod
`тексты · онбординг · лендинг` · [02:10](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=130)

**Мысль:** Людей не интересуют кнопки и экраны, они спрашивают, решит ли это мою проблему достаточно быстро; iPod говорил о выгоде, а не о гигабайтах.

**Слова автора:**

> First one is that treat onboarding like a future tour. Users don't care about your product. They don't care what you offer, but we need to use onboarding to convince them. As they're not asking what buttons exist or how does the app look? They're asking, "Can this solve my problem fast enough to be worth it?" And it really goes back to that whole setting and advice of sell the benefits, not the features. The classic example is the original iPod. They didn't talk about how much storage it has. They didn't talk about how fast it was, what the menu elements look like. They said, "It's a thousand songs that fit in your pocket." It's very easy to understand the benefit as a user and it gets the point across in a much clearer and more beneficial way. We need to be making the users feel understood first.

**Для Напарника:** Лендинг и первый экран: формулировать выгоду («начать, когда не получается») простым языком, не объяснять механику острова и кота.

## 3. Одна идея на экран, ясность важнее минимального числа экранов
`онбординг · иерархия · дизайн-система` · [02:55](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=175)

**Мысль:** Нужно гнаться не за наименьшим числом экранов, а за кратчайшим путём к вере; 10–20 экранов часто золотая середина, одна мысль и одна кнопка на экране.

**Слова автора:**

> Just want to make sure the design reduces friction. And the way we get there is with simplicity. Users are used to having a lot of screens during the onboarding sequence and we want to follow that rule of thumb by having one idea per screen. Typically, we see a button at the bottom here that makes it super easy for the user to understand what's next. They should be understanding the screen just a glance and we don't want it to be cluttered as we're just going to be piling friction on for the user. And the less friction, the better. One thing to note about the number of screens is that speed matters, but clarity matters more. As during our onboarding sequence, we don't want to chase the fewest screens possible. If we just look at fewer screens, it would be first screen and then the payment screen is the next one. Rather, we want to chase the fewest path to belief. We can't have it too short or too long. We want to find a compressed clear value middle. It's going to depend on the app, but I've seen many really successful apps have around 10 to 20 onboarding screens. This tends to be a happy medium, where it's not taking 45 minutes to onboard a user, they're never getting to the paywall, but it's not too short, where there's not enough time to convey any value and bring our user through the value chain.

**Для Напарника:** Если онбординг будет, то по одному вопросу на экране с одной крупной кнопкой; число шагов выбирать по ясности, а не по минимуму.

## 4. Копирайтинг результата: «знай, что есть, набери мышцы» вместо «умный трекинг»
`тексты · микрокопирайтинг · лендинг` · [04:02](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=242)

**Мысль:** Каждую функцию переводи в результат для человека: вместо «умная оптимизация резюме» писать «больше приглашений на собеседования».

**Слова автора:**

> Again, I want to reinforce that we want to be selling benefits, not features. As the way to do this is to use outcome copy. If we take a look at a feature copy example, we could say AI-powered means meal scanner, advanced resume optimization, or maybe your app has smart fitness tracking. What would work better is we could say something more along the lines of know what to eat, put on muscle. Know what to eat to lose weight. Instead of saying advanced resume optimization, we could say get more interviews, get more likely to get your job. And for smart fitness tracking, it could be stick to your plan with reminders, stick to your plan with intelligent advice. We need to be translating features into outcomes at all times.

**Для Напарника:** В текстах Напарника: не «таймер с AI-дроблением», а «разберём задачу на крошечные шаги, чтобы начать».

## 5. 2–4 простых вопроса, на которые приложение потом отвечает отражением
`онбординг · чат · мотивация` · [04:46](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=286)

**Мысль:** Вопросы о целях, трудностях, уровне, сроке и стиле заставляют человека чувствовать себя увиденным, а повторение его ответов позже показывает, что его услышали.

**Слова автора:**

> Something we also want to be doing during our onboarding sequence is having heavy personalization when possible, as we can turn the app into a specific version for that user. If we were to take this a next step, we can actually use their onboarding answers to influence settings in the app, so we can customize it for that specific user. A popular and effective way to do this is to use two to four simple questions that make the user feel seen, whether it's their goals, their struggles, their levels, their timeline, or their preferred style. Then we can reflect back to it during the onboarding sequence, so we can really show that we've listened to them there.

**Для Напарника:** Имя и чек-ин: кот потом упоминает сказанное («ты говорил, утром проще»), используя ответы для настроек, при этом один вопрос за визит.

## 6. Разрешения только когда ценность понятна и привязаны к цели
`уведомления · доверие · онбординг` · [05:52](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=352)

**Мысль:** Спам-запрос уведомлений сразу хуже, чем напоминание, связанное с целью человека, где уведомление само приносит ценность.

**Слова автора:**

> Something else we need to be watching out for is making sure we ask permissions only after the value is clear. One of the big ones would be for asking for notifications or for tracking permission. The wrong way to do it would be just to ask for notifications right away, basically spamming them with pop-ups. A better way to do it would be to remind them to not miss their workout daily if that's your niche for your product. This way we're actually providing value when we notify them to come back. And permission requests should feel connected to the user goal.

**Для Напарника:** Запрос уведомлений формулировать как «напомню тебе в час, который ты выбрал для старта», после первой сессии.

## 7. Закончи онбординг первым полезным действием и не оставляй пустой дом
`онбординг · активация · иерархия` · [06:46](https://youtu.be/e4JUFoidPvM?t=406)

**Мысль:** Поток: боль и обещание, цель, вопрос о трудности, план, превью результата, доверие, затем первое действие; пустой дашборд недопустим.

**Слова автора:**

> So we want to start with reiterating their pain and the promise our app is going to solve. Then want to move to a goal. So what are they looking to get out of the app? We want to add a friction question and then start building them a plan. We can have a results preview of what they're going to get, add a trust builder with some reviews or social proof, and then have their paywall. And then once they're we can bring them into the first action of the app. So if you're looking at a calorie tracker example, they'd be taking a photo of their food to get the results right away. And to note, we never want to have an empty dash board or home page with our application. We want to end with the first useful action, get them actually using the product, get the aha moment, and to really understand why they should be staying subscribed to this app.

**Для Напарника:** Платёжные части не применимы. Но принцип «без пустого Дома, завершить первым полезным действием»: после чек-ина сразу предложить одну микро-сессию.
