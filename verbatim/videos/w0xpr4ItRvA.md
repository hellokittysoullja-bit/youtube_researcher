## 1. Малое обязательство делает попытку дешёвой
`запуск-задачи · привычки · время` · [03:48](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=228)

**Мысль:** 30-секундное видео почти не требует решения, поэтому пробовать и бросать легко; низкая цена входа поощряет эксперименты.

**Слова автора:**

> The hook. There's another reason short videos work so well. They have very little commitment. A two-hour movie requires a decision. A 20-minute documentary requires a decision. A 30-second video? Barely a decision. Your brain can think, "Sure, whatever." And if the video is boring, you leave immediately. That makes experimentation extremely cheap. Watch, reject. Watch, reject. Watch, interesting. The cost of testing each piece of content is tiny.

## 2. Нет естественного конца: продолжать автоматически, остановка — решение
`внимание · этика · время` · [07:07](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=427)

**Мысль:** В ленте нет финала, поэтому по умолчанию вы продолжаете, а стопить нужно действием; дефолт сильнее воли.

**Слова автора:**

> Here's another huge difference. There is no natural ending. A book has a final page. A movie has credits. Pizza has a crust. The feed just keeps going. There is no obvious moment where the experience says, "Congratulations, you've completed TikTok for today." Imagine playing Minecraft with no world border, no objectives, and an infinite supply of blocks. You could technically keep going forever. And if every few minutes something interesting happened, stopping would require a deliberate decision. That's the important part. Stopping isn't automatic. Continuing is automatic. That changes the default. Default behavior. Default's so powerful. If your phone automatically plays the next video, continuing requires almost no action. Stopping requires an action.

**Для Напарника:** Сессия Фокуса имеет явный конец и тёплую точку финала; после неё по умолчанию мягкая пауза, а не следующая сессия.

## 3. Скука как сигнал: сигнал, привычка, награда
`привычки · внимание · СДВГ` · [08:34](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=514)

**Мысль:** Привычка — выученная связь между ситуацией и поведением: скука запускает телефон, награда — стимуляция.

**Слова автора:**

> Boredom. Now, here's something people underestimate. You don't always open TikTok because you desperately want TikTok. Sometimes you're just bored. Waiting for your food, standing in line, sitting on the bus, waiting for a friend, lying in bed. 5 seconds of empty time appears. Your hand reaches for your phone. This can become a habit. A habit is a learned pattern where a repeated behavior becomes associated with a particular cue or situation. The cue might be boredom. The response is checking your phone. The reward is stimulation. And eventually, you don't need to consciously think, "I am now going to consume short-form video." Your hand just does it. Your brain has already learned the routine.

**Для Напарника:** Подменить рутину: скука/застревание → открыть Напарника, и получить маленькое приятное действие.

## 4. Среда побеждает дисциплину: трение работает без монаха в пещере
`привычки · СДВГ · доверие` · [09:39](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=579)

**Мысль:** Печенье на столе заставляет решать каждый раз, печенье в магазине — самоконтроль за тебя; трение снижает автоматику.

**Слова автора:**

> The problem with just have discipline. This is where people often give terrible advice. Just use more discipline. Okay, very inspiring. But discipline isn't the only variable. Your environment matters. If your phone is sitting next to your keyboard with notifications constantly appearing, resisting it requires repeated decisions. If the phone is in another room, the behavior requires more effort. That's a completely different situation. Think about food. If there is a box of cookies sitting directly on your desk, you have to repeatedly decide not to eat them. If the cookies are in a store 20 minutes away, congratulations. Your environment just developed self-control for you. The same principle applies to attention. Friction. Friction means the amount of effort required to perform an action. Make something easier and people generally do it more. Make something harder and people generally do it less. Not always, but often enough to matter. Want to reduce random phone checking? You can increase friction. Turn off unnecessary notifications. Move distracting apps away from your home screen. Log out. Put your phone somewhere less convenient. Use app limits if they actually help you. None of this requires becoming a monk who lives in a cave. You are simply changing the environment.

**Для Напарника:** Подсказка «убери телефон подальше на время сессии» кота; блок отвлечений как мягкое предложение.

## 5. Выбирать развлечение и дрейфовать в него — разный опыт
`внимание · привычки · мотивация` · [14:07](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=847)

**Мысль:** Ощущение хуже, когда не выбирал, а продолжал; осознанный выбор развлечения не оставляет чувства потерянного времени.

**Слова автора:**

> There is also a difference between choosing entertainment and drifting into entertainment. Suppose you finish your work and think, "I'll watch videos for 30 minutes." You intentionally choose it. That's different from opening the app without thinking, scrolling for 90 minutes, and later wondering what happened. The second experience often feels worse because you didn't feel like you were actually choosing. You were continuing. That distinction is useful.

**Для Напарника:** Старт сессии и завершение должны ощущаться как выбор человека («ты решил начать»), а не как автоматическое продолжение.

## 6. Менять среду, а не винить себя
`стыд · этика · привычки` · [17:09](https://youtu.be/w0xpr4ItRvA?t=1029)

**Мысль:** Телефон — не магия, а удобная среда; можно менять её и не корить себя.

**Слова автора:**

> The useful takeaway is simple. Don't think of your phone as a magical device that controls your brain. Think of it as an extremely convenient environment designed to keep giving you things worth looking at. Once you understand that, you can change the environment instead of constantly blaming yourself.

**Для Напарника:** Тексты кота при срывах: меняем условия, а не обвиняем человека.
