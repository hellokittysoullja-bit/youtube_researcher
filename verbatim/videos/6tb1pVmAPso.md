## 1. Узнаваемый силуэт даёт бесконечные вариации персонажей
`персонаж · маскот · крафт` · [01:25](https://youtu.be/6tb1pVmAPso?t=85)

**Мысль:** Дизайн начинают с силуэта, который узнаётся везде, и добавляют черты через личность и увлечения, а не внешние признаки. Всё разнообразие кошек выведено из одного силуэта.

**Слова автора:**

> Now when starting out the design process or the upgrade from their previous looks the developers begin with a silhouette that could be recognized no matter whereabouts they are as you can see this is clearly a cat silhouette so there's no surprise here and sets up the designer with a blank canvas so many cats can be imagined during the design process it was said that it's easier to design a unique cat character by suggesting personality traits and hobbies rather than being limited to actual features hence the basic silhouette now one example given was if fluffy cheeks were an attribute from the start it would be hard to imagine multiple cat characteristics but sticking with the identifiable silhouette removes any limitations and allows for endless designs it's pretty clever thinking now using this technique animal crossing characters can be created with different textures based on a common recognizable appearance the cat silhouette which of course makes it easier to see what species they are whilst also giving enough scope to widen their individuality this screenshot shows a variety of different types of real world cats such as calico himalayan and siamese as well as other familiar appearances such as kabuki makeups and of course an orange for some reason as you can see here they're all clearly cats but all derived from a single silhouette which is really awesome.

**Для Напарника:** Закрепить силуэт кота Напарника как нерушимую основу, а варианты (позы, предметы, настроение) строить поверх него.

## 3. Лишняя детализация убирает индивидуальность
`персонаж · маскот · вкус` · [03:55](https://youtu.be/6tb1pVmAPso?t=235)

**Мысль:** Блики в глазах у одних животных усиливают, у других убирают характер; детали выбирают исходя из личности, а не по шаблону.

**Слова автора:**

> As i understand it this was more of a brainstorm and part of the design process to filter out the types of details that should be included in the new horizons characters so not to make them too detailed or take away from the traditional cute animal crossing appeal we all know and love for example here is the screenshot showing the basic level of detail just take note of the eyes next is this screenshot that as you can see shows something as small as i highlights is said maybe too much and may take away from their personality this is of course different from each animal and shows how every single villager is also designed with their personality in mind rather than limitations with features as you can see here some cubs have eye highlights and some don't the designer's message here is that if you add unnecessary detail like for example adding highlights to the ones on the right you could remove individuality.

**Для Напарника:** Не усложнять MascotSvg: добавлять детали только если они служат характеру кота.

## 4. Поведение, а не роботы: действия в одиночку и «находки»
`персонаж · маскот · body-doubling` · [04:58](https://youtu.be/6tb1pVmAPso?t=298)

**Мысль:** У каждого жителя два вида действий: дело в одиночестве (читает, чистит, ловит рыбу) и находка (заметил бабочку, пользуется предметом). Паузы-раздумья и смена одежды делают жителей живыми.

**Слова автора:**

> Essentially the designers questioned how animals would actually live alongside you so not to make them like robots who are just performing tasks randomly now they define the behaviors in three groups including typical animal-like behavior being able to make decisions on the spot and of course behavior that doesn't appear random based on this definition every single animal has two actions which include doing something alone and finding something just like human behavior the doing something alone action includes things like reading cleaning or fishing whereas the finding something action includes things such as spotting a butterfly flying past or walking past an interactive item and using it this together with the in-between moments where animals have stood their thinking as well as changing their clothes and accessories according to their current action makes the villagers come across more lively and like their actual residents of the island rather than robot npcs who just perform random tasks one after another in a strange way.

**Для Напарника:** Кот-напарник «сидит рядом» с мелкими собственными делами (дремлет, наблюдает, замечает бабочку), как body-doubling без надзора.

## 5. Грубые жители заменены более приветливыми и близкими
`персонаж · тексты · стыд` · [06:05](https://youtu.be/6tb1pVmAPso?t=365)

**Мысль:** Для связи и памятных отношений жители стали доступнее и понятнее, а резкие грубые характеры убрали.

**Слова автора:**

> Suggest that from how the characters new horizons look from afar to how they interact when you're just walking by helps players want to interact with villagers which will assist in making memories and creating a relationship where the animals become important to the player so in short the developers were showing this creative road map from old new characters helps keep us playing and of course explains why the rude grumpy and somewhat offensive villagers we used to know no longer exist in the animal crossing universe instead they act like more playable characters and they're more approachable and relatable.

**Для Напарника:** Подтверждает выбор тёплого тона кота без грубости; привязанность строится на приветливости.
