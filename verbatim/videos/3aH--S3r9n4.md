## 1. Нейроразнообразие как несовпадение ожиданий общества и работы мозга
`доступность · СДВГ · этика` · [03:23](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=203)

**Мысль:** Проблема не в человеке, а в зазоре между стандартом общества и тем, как работает мозг; ровно поэтому продукт не должен мерить человека по норме.

**Слова автора:**

> I really think the easiest explanation for this is that neurodiversity is sort of the mismatch between society's expectations of how your brain is supposed to work and the reality of how your brain actually works right so for me society really dictates that we need to be excellent spellers right we need to have sort of a hundred percent grammar and spelling at all times but in reality that's just not how my brain works and i'm not able to meet that standard and so that's why i consider myself to be neurodiverse i think you can apply that same logic in you know any situation that you're in any situation or ability how you define yourself as neurodiverse it's really just the mismatch between expectations and reality.

**Для Напарника:** Философская основа тона Напарника: не «ты не выполнил», а «система не подстроена под тебя»; ноль, а не минус.

## 2. Две стороны: доступ (access) и успех (success)
`доступность · дизайн-система · процесс` · [05:28](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=328)

**Мысль:** Access — человек может войти и пользоваться; success — получает реальную ценность. Команды застревают на первом и забывают второе.

**Слова автора:**

> Two sides of the same coin basically the first one the first thing that i think we need is what i'm calling access which is i think boiled down really sort of digital accessibility so this is providing users with sort of in essential engagement in your product right so they can navigate they can use your features they can log in you know they can do the sort of table stakes things that every website and every person using a website needs to do right and they can do those things without burden the other side of the coin is something i like to call success and i think if you boil this down you can sort of think of it as inclusive design or more on the inclusive design spectrum right and so this is that users can have really valuable engagement with your product right so they can not only navigate around but they can understand and achieve the value you're trying to bring them right they can have a meaningful experience and build strong proficiency with these products.

**Для Напарника:** Проверять Напарника не только на доступность (контраст, клавиатура), но и на то, достигает ли человек результата (запущенный шаг).

## 3. Изобилие путей: разные представления для разных мозгов (Asana)
`доступность · иерархия · дизайн-система` · [07:05](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=425)

**Мысль:** Доска, список, календарь, таймлайн — не избыточность, а способы визуализировать работу под свой тип мышления.

**Слова автора:**

> Is that your experience should be abundant right and so i like to my little sub head here is additional workflows increase usability right so the more the merrier here the first example that i have is from asana if you're not familiar saunas task management project management software and they have really built into their core product multiple views of ways to manage your work your tasks your goals whatever you're putting in there right part of their core experience is that you can see those things as a board as a list as a timeline on a calendar right some people might think that all of these different views are maybe a little redundant but i really like to think of them as abundant so the whatever type of person you are whatever way you're thinking whatever makes the most sense for you and your team you can visualize your work that way with asana which i think is so impressive and i'm so excited to see all the new views that they'll develop in the future.

**Для Напарника:** Дом/Мир могут давать разные способы увидеть прогресс (остров vs список шагов), но без перегруза выбором.

## 4. Две кнопки закрытия в модалке Pinterest: избыточность помогает
`доступность · иконки · иерархия` · [08:10](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=490)

**Мысль:** Дублирование выхода в двух привычных местах снимает когнитивный поиск; дизайнеры зря считают это излишеством.

**Слова автора:**

> I wanted to like really zoom in on a very small way that you can bring abundance to your interface right so this is a modal from pinterest when you're creating a new board and the thing i absolutely love about this modal is it has a close button in the bottom left and in the top right now these buttons essentially do the same thing right there's a cancel button it'll stop this workflow close the modal the x at the top will also do the same thing this is probably my favorite example in my talk today because it's so simple but yet it actually will help anyone who's using this modal who needs to get out of it they don't have to think oh where's the close button right it's in two spots that these buttons are commonly placed so people will easily be able to find them and achieve their goal i can't tell you how many times i've had this discussion with fellow designers where they think again this might this these two buttons are a little redundant but i think that a certain amount of redundancy can really just help people right i don't think any user will look at this modal notice both close buttons and be offended or disappointed right i think it will only help them achieve their goals faster and with less frustration.

**Для Напарника:** Любой модальный экран Напарника — выход в привычных местах; «Не сегодня» всегда рядом и без вины.

## 5. Прощающий интерфейс: отмена отправки и алерт про забытое вложение
`доверие · стыд · микрокопирайтинг` · [09:42](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=582)

**Мысль:** Лишнее трение оправдано, если оно даёт ощущение безопасности: можно ошибиться и исправить.

**Слова автора:**

> The next principle i want to introduce is that your interfaces should be forgiving right we should be forgiving users the first example i have comes from gmail i absolutely love both of these things i can't talk about them enough the first one is that you can simply undo an email that is sent right i don't know how many of us have sent emails that we regret but gmail file you know has a feature that we can change our minds very quickly super useful easy and helpful on eve in even more detail they're helping us before we send as well with the forgot attachment alert i don't know who else benefits from this i hope everybody watching but i constantly forget to attach things to emails before i send them and this alert gives me some feedback before i send to make sure that i you know wrote attachment and didn't mean to attach something or it helps remind me if i forgot to go back and attach it i think both of these examples you could say perhaps are unnecessary or maybe add more friction to the user experience but at the same time they forgive users and make them feel safe and comfort them that if they do happen to do something wrong there are ways to fix and remedy those things.

**Для Напарника:** Отмена/возврат для случайного завершения сессии или закрытия шага; фраза напарника «ничего страшного, вернём».

## 6. Dropbox: корзина и история версий как конкурентное преимущество
`доверие · стыд · этика` · [11:05](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=665)

**Мысль:** Прощение ошибок превращено в продуктовое позиционирование; сервис, где ничего не потеряно навсегда, снимает тревогу.

**Слова автора:**

> My next example comes from dropbox this is my absolute favorite feature of dropbox i use it constantly at work and it is the deleted files and version history feature all right so on dropbox you can easily navigate to this tab in the main navigation and look back see all your deleted files and version history of those files i think this really puts this really creates like a competitive advantage for dropbox because they are positioning themselves as a really forgiving storage platform right they're not the trash can on your desktop that if you empty everything's gone forever when you store things with dropbox you just don't have to worry about that right they have really taken a bold approach to forgiving their users and allowing them to have access to their deleted files right again i think this is a feature that dropbox could easily have never built right they don't they don't have to have this but because they want to optimize workflows because they're thinking about their users and their feelings they want to forgive them they want to make sure that everyone storing files there can make a mistake and come back and i think these this example is just the best one of the best that i've seen for forgiveness.

**Для Напарника:** Сохранять очередь шагов и «пропущенное» (Zeigarnik): ничего не пропадает насовсем, всегда можно вернуться без штрафа.

## 7. Питающий продукт: Grammarly учит, объясняет, просит обратную связь
`доверие · дизайн-система · ИИ` · [12:34](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=754)

**Мысль:** Инструмент, который не просто исправляет, а объясняет, повышает качество работы пользователя и становится основной ценностью.

**Слова автора:**

> Your product should be nurturing right and for this i like to think of it as that a lot of us make tools that other people make stuff in right you might work on a document editor or a video editor and the better we make those tools the better things people will create in them so my first example is my personal favorite product in the world grammarly whom i mean their keyboard has truly just saved my life and everyone that i text benefits so much for me having access to this but grammarly really took like one feature from traditional word processing right it's that little red zigzag line under the words that you misspell and they made it like the disneyland of spellcheck they are so thoughtful they are very concise consistent and rigorous in presenting you helpful information for word processing and editing right they really thought of this idea of nurturing right they give you suggestions they not only correct you but they help you learn about why they're correcting you there's options to give them feedback on their corrections there is just so many more features than a traditional word processor right and because of this any writing that's done in grammarly is obviously just going to be of a higher quality or there's going to be more thought put into it because users have this very comprehensive experience in front of them again i think other word processing products could have taken this approach right they could have had these features built into them but grammarly really said let's let's be nurturing let's nurture our users and make that our core value proposition.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник объясняет, почему предлагает именно этот шаг/время, и учится на обратной связи человека.

## 8. TikTok: автосубтитры плюс возможность править их
`доступность · ИИ · крафт` · [14:41](https://youtu.be/3aH--S3r9n4?t=881)

**Мысль:** Автоматизация + контроль: человек получает результат одним тапом, но может донастроить, и итог точнее и выразительнее.

**Слова автора:**

> And it is their auto captions and then the ability to edit those auto captions so i don't know how much how much time y'all spend on tick tock for me it's probably like an hour a day and you might have noticed in the past couple months that there are closed captions that you can turn on and off while you're watching your videos well tiktok actually introduced a feature where creators people making videos on their platform can just tap a button and captions are automatically generated to those to those videos now i think we would all say like that's impressive enough right that's really cool technology it's a super simple and easy user experience just to be able to tap a button and bam there's captions but they took it one step further which is really the thing i want to call out about the genius of this feature which is that they allow you to then once the captions are generated to then go into what they've auto generated for you and edit them right so if they've made a mistake if you want to rephrase or re-clarify something they give you that power right and so this feature really nurtures accurate but also very expressive closed captioning for anyone who's making a tick tock video right they are actively allowing you to say what you mean and they are actively nurturing better videos better closed captioning through this product experience.

**Для Напарника:** AI-разбиение задачи (api/breakdown) выдаёт шаги, которые человек может быстро отредактировать, а не принимать как есть.
