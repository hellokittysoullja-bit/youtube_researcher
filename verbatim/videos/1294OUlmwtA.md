## 2. Дофамин — химия ожидания, переменное подкрепление
`дофамин · нейро · тёмные-паттерны` · [02:21](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=141)

**Мысль:** Дофамин пикует до награды; неопределённость исхода держит цикл проверок как у игрового автомата.

**Слова автора:**

> Dopamine is not the pleasure chemical. That's the first myth. Dopamine is the anticipation chemical. It spikes before you get the reward, not when you get it. It's the chemical of maybe. Of what if there's something good in there? It's what makes you check your phone even when you just checked it 30 seconds ago. Not because you enjoyed checking it, because the possibility of something new keeps the loop spinning. This is called a variable reward schedule. And it's the same psychological mechanism that makes slot machines addictive. You don't know when the reward is coming, so you keep pulling the lever. Every scroll of your feed is a pull of that lever. Sometimes there's something interesting, sometimes not. But the uncertainty is the hook.

**Для Напарника:** Для награды на финале сессии использовать редкие находки на острове (контингентность по CLAUDE.md §3), но без манипулятивной слот-механики.

## 3. Гедоническая адаптация: глубокая работа кажется болью
`дофамин · внимание · нейро` · [03:55](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=235)

**Мысль:** После частых быстрых наград планка стимуляции растёт, и долгая работа ощущается как вязкая грязь не из-за лени, а из-за калибровки системы награды.

**Слова автора:**

> It's what heavy dopamine loop behavior does to your baseline. When your brain gets used to rapidfire, high stimulation rewards, genuinely meaningful work, the kind that takes 30, 60, 90 minutes of unbroken focus, stops feeling possible. It actually starts to feel painful. Neuroscientists call this hydonic adaptation. The bar for what feels stimulating keeps rising. Which means sitting down to write, to think deeply, to create, it all feels like slogging through mud. Not because you're lazy, because your reward system has been calibrated to a frequency your work can't match.

**Для Напарника:** Фокус: давать быстрые маленькие микро-награды внутри длинной сессии (рост острова, реплика напарника), чтобы работа не была пустой по стимуляции.

## 4. Attention residue и цена переключения (10–20 мин на вход в глубокое состояние)
`внимание · нейро · запуск-задачи` · [04:46](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=286)

**Мысль:** При смене задачи часть внимания остаётся на прошлой; вход в глубокое состояние занимает 10–20 минут, а одно уведомление его рушит.

**Слова автора:**

> It's called attention residue, a term coined by organizational psychologist Sophie Loy. Here's the concept. When you switch from task A to task B, your mind doesn't fully switch. part of your cognitive bandwidth stays stuck on task A. Still processing it, still worrying about it, still holding space for it. That leftover mental weight, that's the residue, and it silently tanks your performance on everything you do next. This is why multitasking is a myth. You're not doing two things at once. You're rapidly switching between them. And each switch costs you. Cognitive science calls this the switch cost. Every time you check email in the middle of writing, you don't just lose the seconds it takes to read the email, you lose the mental state you were in. And rebuilding that state takes time. Deep focused mental states, the ones required for real creative or analytical work, can take 10 to 20 minutes to enter. One notification can wipe that out instantly.

**Для Напарника:** Экран Фокуса: не слать уведомления внутри сессии, молчаливый напарник; объяснить это одной строкой в голосе кота.

## 5. Фокус — не воля, а дизайн среды и подсказок (Фогг)
`внимание · СДВГ · процесс` · [06:53](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=413)

**Мысль:** Поведение = мотивация, способность и подсказки; среда с триггерами отвлечения гарантирует отвлечение независимо от силы воли.

**Слова автора:**

> We treat focus like it's an internal resource. like you either have willpower today or you don't. Like the solution is to want it more, try harder, be more disciplined. And that framing is almost completely wrong. Focus is not primarily a willpower problem. It is an environment design problem. Your brain doesn't operate in isolation. It operates in context. And your context is constantly giving your brain signals. Is this a place to focus? Is this a place to relax? Is this a place where distractions are normal? Stanford psychologist BJ Fog has spent decades researching this. His conclusion, behavior is a function of motivation, ability, and crucially prompts. If your environment is filled with prompts for distraction, you will be distracted. Not because you're weak, because you're human. Your brain is exquisitly sensitive to environmental cues. That's a feature, not a bug. It's just currently calibrated to a world optimized for your distraction.

**Для Напарника:** Фокус-экран должен быть средой без подсказок отвлечения; AppBackdrop как сигнал «здесь место для фокуса».

## 6. Час без цифрового ввода для перекалибровки базовой планки
`дофамин · привычки · внимание` · [09:27](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=567)

**Мысль:** Намеренное снижение стимуляции на 2–3 недели возвращает толерантность к медленной глубокой работе.

**Слова автора:**

> Layer one, recalibrate your dopamine baseline. This takes time, but the principle is simple. Your brain has adapted to high stimulation inputs. You need to deliberately lower stimulation to give it time to readjust. Practically pick one hour per day, ideally in the morning with zero digital input. No phone, no podcast, no music. Let your brain experience what boredom feels like again. This is uncomfortable at first. That discomfort is the recalibration happening. After 2 to 3 weeks, most people report that their baseline tolerance for slow, deep work dramatically improves.

**Для Напарника:** Мягкий необязательный челлендж «тихий час» в Мире; без стыда за пропуски.

## 7. Телефон в другую комнату и убрать подсказки: инструменты среды, не костыли
`внимание · запуск-задачи · процесс` · [10:23](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=623)

**Мысль:** Даже выключенный телефон в поле зрения съедает рабочую память; блокировщики убирают подсказки, чтобы мозгу не приходилось с ними бороться. Рекомендуют начинать с 45 минут.

**Слова автора:**

> Stop trying to do focused work while leaving every distraction open. This sounds obvious, but almost nobody actually does it. When you start a focused work block, and I'd recommend starting with just 45 minutes, put your phone in a different room. Not face down, a different room. Research shows that even a phone that is face down, silenced, and not being used reduces working memory capacity just by being in your visual field. The brain is allocating resources to resist checking it. Close your email. Close Slack. Put your browser in full screen mode. Use a distraction blocker if you need to. Freedom, cold turkey, one focus. These are not crutches. They are environment design tools. They remove the prompts so your brain doesn't have to fight them.

**Для Напарника:** Чек-лист перед стартом сессии в настройке шага: «телефон подальше», с дружелюбным голосом напарника; 45 мин как вариант длительности.

## 8. Аудит отвлечений: небольшое трение ломает автоматический цикл
`внимание · процесс · дизайн-система` · [12:15](https://youtu.be/1294OUlmwtA?t=735)

**Мысль:** Лёгкое трение (убрать иконку с главного экрана) достаточно, чтобы разорвать автоматику; всё, что не относится к текущей задаче, не должно быть видимым.

**Слова автора:**

> Do a distraction audit right now after this video. Actually do this. Open your phone. Go through every app. Ask one question for each one. Does this app serve me or does it extract from me? The apps that extract from you that you open out of habit rather than intention, delete them or move them off your home screen. make access slightly harder. That tiny friction is enough to break the automatic behavior loop. And for your workspace, one simple rule. If it's not related to the work you're doing right now, it should not be visible. Visible things compete for attention. A clear desk is not just aesthetic. It is cognitive infrastructure.

**Для Напарника:** Принцип минимального UI на Фокусе: на экране только текущий шаг и таймер.
