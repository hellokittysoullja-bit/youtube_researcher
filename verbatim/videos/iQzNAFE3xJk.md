## 1. Якорь цены: самый дорогой пункт делает остальные дешевле
`цены · этика · метрики` · [04:50](https://youtu.be/iQzNAFE3xJk?t=290)

**Мысль:** Винная карта начинается с дорогого, чтобы всё ниже выглядело доступнее; в магазине держат дорогую позицию как якорь.

**Слова автора:**

> Anyway they start with a very high priced wine and what that does is it sets a expectation for what wine costs and then everything beneath that is then anchored to that first price making it appear in this case much cheaper so if i started instead with the sparkling rose at 12 bucks a glass then everything would look more expensive but because it's been started with the prosecco at 23 bucks a glass then everything looks cheaper oh i can't afford the prosecco but i can afford the sparkling rose the pinot grigio blah at those other prices you'll see it all the time now in good restaurants you'll see it all the time so how do we use that oftentimes a really good strategy to use is to have something that is on your in your store that is very expensive because it anchors everything else as a price point.

**Для Напарника:** Если появится платный план: честно показывать премиум-вариант, но без искусственных дорогих приманок.

## 2. Цены на 99 кажутся дешевле; круглые числа продают люксовый статус
`цены · конверсия` · [08:01](https://youtu.be/iQzNAFE3xJk?t=481)

**Мысль:** 9,99 воспринимается заметно дешевле 10; люксовые бренды используют только круглые числа, чтобы выглядеть дорогими.

**Слова автора:**

> Numbers that end in 99 or 97 or 95 seem cheaper than the whole number okay so whilst 999 is only one cent away from 10 bucks psychologically they are worlds apart they are worlds apart okay so something that is 9.99 feels like it's much cheaper than something that's 10 dollars why it's just a quirk of human nature so when you're pricing very important okay here's an example of that odd numbers they seem like a better deal so 12.99 we see 1200 not 1300 okay that's how it kind of works on the other side of that if you've ever been in louis vuitton or chanel or prada gucci or any of those kind of high-end shops you'll notice their pricing is always the whole number they never ever use that strategy because what they want to look like is they want to look expensive they're doing everything they can to look expensive that's the cachet of their brands their whole shtick is only expensive only wealthy people can afford this if you've got it you are wealthy therefore you have elevated status within society that's their entire shtick okay and so because of that they always advertise in round numbers never the 12 the 3 795 they'd never use that okay so round numbers are used to sell luxury products always round numbers sell luxury.

**Для Напарника:** Тёплый доступный продукт — цена вроде 299 ₽; не люкс, поэтому круглые числа не нужны.

## 3. «Одно бесплатно» побеждает «скидка 50%» при том же размере
`цены · награды · мотивация` · [11:49](https://youtu.be/iQzNAFE3xJk?t=709)

**Мысль:** Люди воспринимают получение целой вещи бесплатно как больший выигрыш, чем скидку на ту же вещь.

**Слова автора:**

> Saying somebody something is 50 off versus buy one get one free buy one get one free always outperforms fifty percent off they're the same thing they're the same thing but quirk of human nature we don't perceive it as being the same we perceive it as one thing where we're getting a whole new thing for free versus just getting a discount on the existing thing and because of that it constantly outperforms it constantly outperforms and if you use it's also as i've demonstrated here more profitable as well so offering something for free often will beat a discount often beats discount so two for one usually beats discount.

**Для Напарника:** В наградах предпочитать «получаешь новый элемент на острове», а не «скидка 50% на прогресс».

## 4. Разбивай цену на день/неделю/использование
`цены · конверсия` · [12:48](https://youtu.be/iQzNAFE3xJk?t=768)

**Мысль:** Мелкая сумма отвлекает от общей цены и упрощает сравнение.

**Слова автора:**

> Breaking prices down and demonstrating things like what that costs you per week what it costs you per day things like that is a really good strategy like if you drive down any high street where there are cars for sale typically what you'll see are is cars being advertised as a x amount of dollars per month okay and the reason they do that is because it works it draws our attention away from the actual price of the car and instead focuses it on a much smaller quantum of money which is the monthly charge for the car okay and it also sets expectations something else around getting financed for the car which is really where car companies make most of their money anyway so if you can use this as a comparison like it will cost you using this product will cost you x per day x per week x per use for instance it's really a very good thing to do.

**Для Напарника:** Подписка: «меньше чашки кофе в неделю» без давления; всегда показывать полную цену честно.
