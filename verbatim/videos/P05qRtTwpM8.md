# Don't Let Your Character Die! Keep Alive Workflow

**Канал:** Sir Wade Neistadt · 14 мин · субтитры: en (авто YouTube) · https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8

> «Слова автора» — дословный текст из транскрипта (убраны только um/uh и заикания; в авто-субтитрах мало знаков препинания). «Мысль» и «Для Напарника» — вывод разметчика, это не речь автора.

## 1. Keep alive: персонаж не должен «умирать» в паузе
`маскот · анимация · внимание` · [00:00](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=0)

**Мысль:** Когда персонаж ничего не делает, неподвижный экран выглядит как зависание. Решение: дыхание как базовый idle, живое присутствие без действия.

**Слова автора:**

> One of the things I always struggled with a lot as an animation student was keep alive what to do with a character when they're not actively acting or remoting or doing something I remember being told a lot as a student that things shouldn't just move because you need motion and you need movement in your shot you should be able to take a breath pause and just let things be still and then you can move after that but I didn't know what to do in those moments it wasn't until much later that I realized oh like what all of us do when we're not actively doing something while we're breathing and breathing is just one example of how to have your character idle and just kind of hang out and not do a lot but not just totally die on screen with no pixel motion or feels like the screen just froze and nothing's working.

**Для Напарника:** Кот-напарник в сессии Фокус должен всегда иметь тихий idle (дыхание), чтобы экран не казался замершим, но без отвлекающего движения.

## 2. Idle-движение нужно чувствовать, а не видеть
`анимация · маскот · крафт` · [05:46](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=346)

**Мысль:** Дыхание должно быть настолько тонким, что его не замечают; при перебарщивании выглядит как сломанный контрол, а не дыхание.

**Слова автора:**

> Remember that these things shouldn't stand out and you shouldn't notice them they're there for you to feel not to see but for emphasis just to kind of demonstrate to you what I did and why I'm gonna go ahead and bump this up really aggressively so you can see the effect this is how this is obviously way too much but the idea is that the chest is expanding and contracting with every breath and it is slightly offset from the up-and-down forward back of links body which if I where do I go ahead and dial that up you're gonna see that things just get all kinds of wacky so you do not want to push these things too far because it won't look good I wouldn't even look like he's breathing it just looks like you've broken the control and I don't know what this looks like but back to normal again you don't even really notice what I did it's just that there is motion in that full connected area.

**Для Напарника:** Амплитуду дыхания кота держать минимальной; согласуется с принципом медленности и диегетичности фона. MascotSvg не трогать ради полировки одного экрана.

## 3. Копировать кривую движения на связанные части со смещением
`анимация · маскот · крафт` · [06:50](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=410)

**Мысль:** Одно основное движение копируется в другие атрибуты со сдвигом и масштабом: грудь двигает плечи, шею, голову, всё связано.

**Слова автора:**

> Most of what I did from here on out is take the forward and back of that body rotation and I copy the curve and I pasted it into the translate y or the rotate X or the scale Z or whatever it is I copy pasted that curve into another attribute maybe offset it a bit maybe scaled it up and down maybe had to flip it in case it was going the wrong way at the wrong time or something when you move the chest that means the shoulders need to move which means the arms need to move which means the hands need to move and going back to the shoulders in the chest the neck is moving the head is moving you're affecting the stomach because breathing happens all through the diaphragm which might affect the hips somewhat everything is connected all the way down to the most polished little details.

**Для Напарника:** Дыхание кота можно строить как одну кривую, применённую с разной фазой к телу, ушам, хвосту.

## 4. Мелкое окружающее движение до самых мелочей
`анимация · маскот · крафт` · [08:06](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=486)

**Мысль:** Даже пальцы получают лёгкое движение, чтобы не быть мёртвыми; дыхание распространяется по всей фигуре.

**Слова автора:**

> Didn't leave anything to not move even the fingers the fingers are very subtle and probably could use a little bit extra work as well but if you look here you can actually see the hand kind of closing and opening a little bit the thumbs moving I wanted a little bit of ambient motion now I probably could have offset the fingers - not all go in and out at the same time they should all have a little bit more independence but I did do that on each hand just so that they weren't completely dead and if I were actually doing a full shot it might have been a good idea to have him kind of adjust his hand shift something around maybe you know reset his hand and get a better grip on the sword whatever it is don't forget about these little details when you're thinking about Ovid the shoulders and the chest of breathing that motion travels all the way down to the fingers.

**Для Напарника:** Хвост, усы, кончики ушей кота могут слегка двигаться в разной фазе, чтобы он казался живым.

## 5. Не синхронизировать всё: смещённые ключи дают органику
`анимация · крафт` · [09:11](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=551)

**Мысль:** Если все части двигаются одновременно, выглядит механически; ключи должны быть разбросаны и смещены.

**Слова автора:**

> With this type of an animation I don't recommend any kind of a pose to pose your stuff workflow at least the way I did it this is something that I would recommend just kind of getting your first main motion and then copy-pasting off setting your keys you're gonna be all over the place do your best to keep track of all of that but don't be afraid of keys being in different places because you kind of need them to be you don't want the whole character at the same time you want everything to feel offset broken up organic so that's important.

**Для Напарника:** Фазы разных частей кота и элементов фона разводить по времени и длительности циклов.

## 6. Волосы и плащ на разной частоте: ощущение пространства
`анимация · маскот · крафт` · [10:18](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=618)

**Мысль:** Ветер и вторичное движение на разных частотах и фазах создают ощущение, что персонаж находится в реальной среде.

**Слова автора:**

> Here's an example of what that looks like with link you can see his cloak has this kind of ambient blowing in the wind type of motion just to feel like he's in a space with wind same goes for his hair there's a little bit of motion on his hair at a different frequency and if we look at his hair every section of his hair does kind of the same thing but it's a slightly different time his whole hair is not all moving at once it's broken up into this nice organic when the you know things move at different paces different rates.

**Для Напарника:** Лёгкое вторичное движение (шерсть, отблеск костра) у кота и острова с разными периодами, привязанное к окружению Дома.

## 7. Продумай, где находится персонаж: среда определяет движение
`анимация · маскот · крафт` · [11:03](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=663)

**Мысль:** Внутри или снаружи, ветер, время суток и погода влияют на то, как двигаются детали даже в простом idle.

**Слова автора:**

> One of the quickest ways to figure out if you need to add that kind of stuff in your shot is to think about where is your character and if you haven't thought about it if it's just somewhere in Maya gray background just basic play blasting of an exercise that you're doing just think about this are they inside or the outside make a decision it's totally up to you if the outside there's probably some amount of wind so consider the environment and location and time of day and weather and that kind of stuff when you are animating even something as simple as a breathing exercise.

**Для Напарника:** Кот у костра: реакции на время суток и очаг уже заложены в AppBackdrop; движение шерсти и пламени связать с этой сценой.

## 8. Дыхание без живого лица всё равно мёртвое
`анимация · маскот · персонаж` · [11:35](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=695)

**Мысль:** Тело дышит, но если лицо статично, персонаж не выглядит живым; нужны взгляды, моргание, зевание.

**Слова автора:**

> You may get a character to look like he's breathing but he doesn't look like he's alive if you leave the face just dead so whether you have him doing eye darts whether you have him looking around whether you have him yawning or whatever maybe he's talking while he's breathing maybe you already have acting and you're just using the breathing to plus it regardless the difference between this and this should be pretty clear one of them has I mean obviously he's looking around but you should feel a lot more life from the character in this state.

**Для Напарника:** Выражения кота (mascot expression) в idle: редкое моргание, взгляд в сторону, зевок вместо статичной морды.

## 9. Невидимые микро-детали (зрачки при моргании) создают чувство реальности
`анимация · маскот · крафт · вкус` · [12:18](https://youtu.be/P05qRtTwpM8?t=738)

**Мысль:** Мелкая деталь, которую никто сознательно не заметит, добавляет ощущение подлинности. Автор сам называет её необязательной.

**Слова автора:**

> One little detail that I do want to point out since this is the close-up shot look really closely at his eyes when he blinks and looks away do you guys notice anything about the pupils not to go here it's really quick it's really subtle you would probably never need to do this unless you were really close up but I actually decided to have when he blinks the size of his pupil if you look at the size of his pupil hopefully you can tell he closes his eyes his pupils dilate they get bigger because there's less light when he closes his eyes and he opens and the pupils shrink again they actually do a slight overshoot as well they go small and big it's a very small and subtle detail that may not have been necessary but I felt really good about adding it and it was just something that I added in that polished process that I was like oh yeah this is good no one's gonna notice this especially at the distance that the shots suppose be viewed from but I thought it would add to that feeling of the character just feels real for some reason.

**Для Напарника:** Микро-детали моргания кота (лёгкий перерегулирующий отскок) допустимы, но без привязки к принципу не добавлять ради красоты.
