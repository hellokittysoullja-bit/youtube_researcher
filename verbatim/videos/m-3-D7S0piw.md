## 1. Персонаж как «мысленный мяч»: меньше трудности, больше цели

**Мысль:** Аналогия со спортом: с мячом и соревнованием думаешь меньше о физическом усилии. Персонаж и мир делают долгий трудный путь приятным.

**Слова автора:**

> One of the biggest reasons we're adding characters is because we believe characters can play a large role in motivating people. A lot of games we play internally have great characters and those characters help to keep us engaged. At Duolingo we love the benefits that games have on our brain health. I think of this the same way sports benefit our physical health. Sometimes it's hard to just get up and run around your yard, but once you introduce a ball and healthy competition, you think less about how physically hard it is, and more about the ball and the goal. Because let's face it, language journeys can sometimes be long and difficult. So Duolingo strives to introduce that mental ball to help keep you motivated. ## 3. Персонажи как отражение реальных пользователей
`персонаж · доверие · вкус` · [03:15](https://youtu.be/m-3-D7S0piw?t=195)

**Мысль:** Люди вместо фэнтезийных зверей, чтобы изучение языка ощущалось как реальное и достижимое; персонажи многогранны, со странностями и узнаваемыми трудностями.

**Слова автора:**

> We knew from the get go that we wanted our characters to be a diverse cast of humans. It might seem logical to expect that our cast be a pack of friendly, woodland creatures to compliment Duo being an owl. But we don't want learning a new language to feel like something that exists only in a fantasy world, it's something that can happen to real people, living in this world. By designing our characters as humans, in a small way we're reflecting our user base, people who use Duolingo every day. In addition, since we teach languages from all over the world, our characters had to represent cultures from all over the world. We especially wanted to represent those that are typically underrepresented in Western media, and take a lot of care to ensure that every character's personality is well-rounded, three-dimensional, with fun quirks, and relatable struggles.

**Для Напарника:** Тон кота: тёплый, с лёгкими слабостями и юмором, а не идеальный гуру.

## 4. Новые персонажи строятся из визуального языка маскота
`дизайн-система · маскот · персонаж` · [04:17](https://youtu.be/m-3-D7S0piw?t=257)

**Мысль:** Выделили четыре компонента дизайна Дуо (простое тело, большие глаза, уникальная форма, отдельные ноги) и применили к людям, чтобы мир был цельным.

**Слова автора:**

> Another aspect we took into consideration when designing our characters was our mascot, Duo. Duo really is the most important visual aspect of our brand. In order to create characters that worked well within our existing design language, we knew they had to resemble Duo. So we dug deeper into Duo's design. When you break down Duo's design, there are four main components. First off, Duo's constructed very simply, he's really just a body with wings. Second, he has these really big, endearing eyes. Thirdly, he has a unique body shape. And finally, and probably the strangest feature, are his detached feet. Now, when you look at all of these together and you begin to apply them to a new human character, you start to see something interesting. A human character reflecting Duo's design language, and thus making them feel like part of the same world.

**Для Напарника:** Если появятся второстепенные существа на острове, наследовать форму и глаза кота; не трогать MascotSvg ради этого.

## 5. Базовая форма говорит о характере: дизайн пишет персонажа
`персонаж · маскот · вкус` · [05:02](https://youtu.be/m-3-D7S0piw?t=302)

**Мысль:** Треугольники опасны, квадраты упрямы и надёжны, круги безопасны и игривы. Без сюжета форма определяла личность, дизайнеры стали рассказчиками.

**Слова автора:**

> Now, these shapes that we use to design each character are important because they're an outward expression of a character's inward personality. If you look at any characters in video games or movies, and you just focus on their basic shape construction, you'll probably be able to inherently identify if they're good or bad, and what kind of personality they have. Take these characters from Up, for example. Each character uses one prominent shape in their design to help communicate a persona. Triangles, for example, are dangerous and dynamic shapes. In this case, they're used for the bad guy to convey danger. Squares are structurally sound shapes, they're reliable a lot of times, and in this case, they're stubborn shapes. And circles are fun, safe, playful. These rules apply to all characters, even our own. However, since Duolingo doesn't have a story arc like most other design productions have, we didn't feel like we had enough information to solely write great characters. So we let their outward design influence what personality they would have, essentially letting the design write the characters for us. This was interesting, because it allowed the designers to become the storytellers instead of the writers. We went through hundreds of iterations of characters, looking for the right shapes that we thought told the best stories.

**Для Напарника:** Круглые мягкие формы кота и построек острова работают на ощущение безопасности и тепла; держать их в иллюстрациях и иконках.

**Слова автора:**

> The downside of this, as you can hear, is that it doesn't reflect our character's personality at all because we have one generic masculine voice and one generic feminine voice for all characters. So unfortunately, Lily sounds robotic instead of sounding like a deeply unenthused teenager.
