## 1. Казино убирают часы и окна, а СДВГ-день ощущается так же
`время · СДВГ · тёмные-паттерны` · [01:17](https://youtu.be/u9bPfLKuJgE?t=77)

**Мысль:** Казино намеренно лишают чувства времени; у людей с СДВГ погружение в ритм без часов даёт то же ощущение утекающего дня.

**Слова автора:**

> So if you think about Las Vegas and the casinos that they actually do everything they can to promote time blindness in all of the people that go there in order to get them to spend more time doing what they're doing there which is spending more money and giving all their money away to the casinos right and what they do for example is they get rid of clocks and windows they manage people's movements to create a steady Rhythm and they give them free alcohol to impair their time perception and they enhance a comfort of being in that place with nice furniture warm lighting consistent temperature control that are used to lower that alertness what happens is the they do everything they can to make sure that you are not aware of what time it is how long you've been there Etc and that's how it feels to live with ADHD where you get into a rhythm of doing what you're doing and next thing you know time has slipped away and it's night time and you haven't done your work etc.

**Для Напарника:** Показывать течение времени мягко и видимо (небо по часу суток, кольцо); не быть казино-паттерном без часов.

## 2. Время как «сейчас / не сейчас»: всё не-сейчас одинаково далеко
`время · СДВГ · внимание` · [02:35](https://youtu.be/u9bPfLKuJgE?t=155)

**Мысль:** Дело на пять минут и на пять лет равно «не сейчас», поэтому приоритизация сложна.

**Слова автора:**

> Another way to look at time blindness is this all or nothing thinking this now or not now feeling that we have where whatever task we're doing is what's happening right now and that's where our focus is everything else whether it's five minutes away or five years away is not now so it's split right down the middle this is what I'm doing which is the now and that's what I'm not doing which is the not now so it could be a challenge to figure out prioritization.

**Для Напарника:** Превращать далёкое в ближайший шаг («сейчас» = 5 минут) и показывать его на Доме.

## 3. Среда видимого времени и дробление задач проявляют их реальную цену
`время · СДВГ · запуск-задачи` · [03:28](https://youtu.be/u9bPfLKuJgE?t=208)

**Мысль:** Часы, окна и визуальные напоминания делают время видимым; разбиение на мелкие задачи показывает, сколько всего в одной.

**Слова автора:**

> Some examples which is using physical clocks wearing watches spending time near Windows visual reminders basically creating an environment for us where we are constantly able to see the passage of time one of the things that I talk with my clients about is breaking things into smaller tasks because once you start to look at those tasks and estimate how long they take then you'll be surprised at how much actually is involved in that one particular task.

**Для Напарника:** Разбиение на микрошаги с оценкой минут для каждого.

## 4. Оцени и замерь: 15 минут масла на деле 38
`время · СДВГ · исследования` · [03:50](https://youtu.be/u9bPfLKuJgE?t=230)

**Мысль:** Расхождение оценки и факта копится каждый день и разрушает план; тренировать нужно навык оценки через замеры.

**Слова автора:**

> Another thing is carrying around a kitchen sign which is something that I have here basically the idea is that if you try to estimate how long you think a task will take and then time how long it actually takes it's actually usually a very different figure so let's say you think it takes 10 minutes to clean your kitchen and it actually takes 15 or 20 minutes that's not very much in the small scale but if that happens over and over for people with ADHD that can compound and it means that we are unable to plan our day and it adds to that feeling of overwhelm and that knock-on effect means that those five minutes here and 10 minutes there add up and can take away a significant part of our day for example a client of mine one of the tasks was taking their car to the garage to get an oil change and in their mind they thought it would take 15 minutes because it's a quick oil change place but the fact is it actually took 38 minutes and in hindsight it makes a lot of sense but until we start training our brain to think in that way and to really sit down and plan and estimate how long things take for people with ADHD especially it can be very difficult.

**Для Напарника:** После шага спрашивать «сколько ушло?» и накапливать личную калибровку, показывать мягко, без оценок.

## 5. Таймер обратного отсчёта и правило «умножь оценку на два»
`время · СДВГ · запуск-задачи` · [04:48](https://youtu.be/u9bPfLKuJgE?t=288)

**Мысль:** Убывающее время видно и ощущаемо; двойной запас либо даёт бонусные минуты, либо покрывает ошибку.

**Слова автора:**

> Another way of combating it is actually using the timer in the opposite way so it can comes down so for example if you want to spend 20 minutes cleaning your kitchen rather than timing it upwards and counting to see if it does take 20 minutes start it at 20 minutes and count it down so that you're more aware and keep that time with you as you're doing that task so as you're doing it you can visually see that time counting down and get a better idea of how long things actually take one other thing that we can do is spend time in the morning planning out your day and as you're planning out that day on your calendar or whatever it is that you're using for however long you think a task will be double it so if you think something's going to take 10 minutes give yourself 20 minutes to do it the thinking behind this is let's say that you think a task takes 10 minutes and it does take 10 minutes but you've given yourself 20 minutes to do it that's great that means you have a spare 10 minutes to start the next task but if you're wrong and you think it takes 10 minutes and it does take 20 minutes then you've accounted for that time blindness.

**Для Напарника:** Таймер Фокуса — обратный отсчёт; при планировании добавлять запас (буфер) и подавать как подарок времени.
