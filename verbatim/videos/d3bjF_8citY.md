## 1. Тайм-менеджмент для нейротипиков предполагает осознание времени, которого нет
`СДВГ · время · исследования` · [00:57](https://youtu.be/d3bjF_8citY?t=57)

**Мысль:** Советы исходят из того, что время ощущается реальным и оценка в час верна; у людей со слепотой ко времени «два часа» превращаются в восемь, и сначала нужно научиться воспринимать время.

**Слова автора:**

> The first challenge with a lot of neurotypical productivity advice around time management is that it presumes that you have time awareness. It presumes that for the person who's following the advice, time feels real. presumes that the only thing that's stopping them is that they've just never thought about writing down what the things are that they need to do. It presumes that if you estimate that something's going to take an hour, that more often than not, it actually takes an hour. But here's the challenge. You can see where I'm going with this. That isn't always true of ADHD brains. Because folks with ADHD who have sort of unmanaged ADHD often have a different perception of time, a perception of time sometimes called time blindness until we've gone to work on it. And this is a pretty common pattern of folks who come with me that we start working together is that they'll tell me things like, you know, they tell their spouse like, "Yeah, I got about two hours more of work here and then I'm heading home." And then sure enough, like 8 hours later, they're still in the still like in the thick of it. And the reason is that at the time they were trying to assess what was going on. Like they weren't able to make accurate predictions. I hope you can appreciate that if you can't yet make accurate predictions as the first step, the no time management system made for neurotypical people will work. Does that make sense? It's sort of like if I was trying to like fill a cup and I wanted to fill the cup and like just to the top, but I didn't want it to go over, but I couldn't. I was blind to see how close the water was getting to the top. Well, obviously I'm not going to be very good at filling it up cuz I'm not very good at predicting and seeing actually what's getting. I don't have that actual sense of perceiving what is happening. And so often like if you're finding yourself in this moment and maybe just to assess for yourself is this a pattern that you go through because if it is that actually what we have to do if you want to get better at time management the very first step is you have to become better at time awareness.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник не требует точных оценок; сначала помогает калибровать ощущение времени (оценка → факт), потом планировать.

## 2. Потолок сложности: система сложнее рабочей памяти обречена, как 13 чисел вместо 3
`СДВГ · внимание · процесс · нейро` · [03:01](https://youtu.be/d3bjF_8citY?t=181)

**Мысль:** Любая система, превышающая то, что мозг удерживает, ломается через несколько дней; дело не в хорошей или плохой памяти, а в соответствии системы ёмкости.

**Слова автора:**

> The next piece of where trying to follow time management strategies that are really more aligned for neurotypical people often fails business owners with ADHD is that they kind of assume a level of mental RAM that you might not have yet. So here's what I want to offer. Everyone has everyone neurotypical ADHD, whatever your brain is, has sort of a complexity ceiling of how many sort of like open tabs they can keep open in their brain where everything sort of like makes sense and you can maintain it all at one spot. >> >> What I would offer is that often it's the case for people with ADHD that they might have a smaller sort of working memory than others. That's okay. There's nothing there's nothing wrong with that. But it becomes a challenge whenever you try to adhere to a system that is more complex than sort of like your brain can hold in one spot at one point. So like let's give an example. Let's give an example of like a filing system. where this goes sideways on people is they say, "Okay, I'm going to have this brand new system for my files and it's going to have like all of these colored tabs and it's going to be like 20 different dividers and it's going to be so wonderful and it's wonderful on day number one." But if that system that you've set up is at least at the moment more complex than your ability to adhere to it, then it's going to fall short. And this often happens with sort of time management systems as well is that people will get hyper granular and create systems that have a level of granularity that their brain might have challenges sort of staying on top of and perceiving at a particular time. So what I mean by hyperranularity and perceiving at a particular time, it's no different than if I said to you, hey, I want you to remember these three numbers. I'm going to tell you three numbers today and you're going to remember them tomorrow. You could probably do that pretty easily. But if I say, hey, I'm going to give you 13 numbers. I'm going to see them once and you're going to remember them and tell me them tomorrow. You might struggle. Now, does that mean that you're good or bad at memory? No. It just means that you need to ensure that any sort of system that you set up is within your it's sort of within your capacity to execute. And often what leads business owners with ADHD to go through these patterns of like adopting a system and failing and adopting a system and failing over and over again while it might work for others is that you're trying to adopt a system that sort of requires and assumes a level of sort of RAM, a level of working memory that you might not yet have.

**Для Напарника:** Дом не должен превращаться в сложную систему: минимум сущностей (один шаг, один таймер, остров); не добавлять уровни и теги.

## 3. Приоритеты требуют ощущения дефицита времени (как отпуск против бесконечного времени)
`СДВГ · время · внимание` · [05:33](https://youtu.be/d3bjF_8citY?t=333)

**Мысль:** Приоритизация это распределение дефицитного ресурса; если время кажется бесконечным или «всё прямо сейчас», выбирать нечего. Бюджет лучше планирует тот, у кого мало денег.

**Слова автора:**

> The third pitfall is trying to prioritize before you have time awareness. So, a subset of time management is prioritizing what to do with your time. I want you to just think about what priority is and what prioritization is. Prioritization is some sort of management and allocation of scarce resources. For you to prioritize something, it has to actually be scarce. And a challenge of trying where a lot of folks fail in trying to set priorities before they've developed time awareness is that if you don't have time awareness, it might kind of you might be in that sense of time where time is sort of like it is like everything is happening all at once. I have no time. Everything is right now. Or I kind of have infinite time. Well, like it's not happening yet. And I would offer that when you don't have scarcity in your conception of time, it's going to make prioritization hard. Think about who would budget better. Think about if you had to take a guess of who's going to budget better. Is it going to be somebody with infinite money or is it going to be somebody with a small amount of money? What feels more real? If you think about in your own life and other areas of where you might have a little bit of time awareness, do you find that you tend to plan your days more and you tend to be more aware of time and making choices when you're on vacation and you know you're only there for a small amount of time compared to when you're not on vacation and time may feel infinite. And so what I want to offer is that prior to actually being able to really prioritize time, you actually have to feel time as a limited resource. I know that maybe that sounds too simple to be true, but it might be the thing that's been holding you back that has been meaning that all of these systems that people have been telling you to use in the past haven't worked.

**Для Напарника:** Показывать ограниченность дня визуально (небо и час суток уже делают это) и просить выбрать одно из трёх, а не ранжировать двадцать.

## 4. Прыжки между проектами размывают время; осознание слабеет без маркеров

**Слова автора:**

> The next thing that keeps business owners with ADHD stuck when it comes to time management is that often they have structured their business in a way that inadvertently reinforces time blindness. So, maybe you've noticed this, maybe you've noticed this in your life. Have you noticed that maybe when you were in school, maybe you didn't feel that some of these challenges were as big as they are now? Or maybe when you were in a corporate environment, it's very common that somebody's in a corporate environment and then when they're out on their own, it's very different. And often what they are missing is that they've inadvertently designed the business where they're sort of just like jumping from one project to another. You know what I mean? like it's like one meeting to another to another to another, which introduces switching costs, which might feel like all time starts to blur and blend together. Particularly for business owners, if you're at a stage where you haven't really got out of a firefighting stage, that's a particular time where time starts to blur together, where we're just jumping from one thing to the other. What I would offer is that, you know, if you could think of time awareness as something that we can strengthen in our brain through working it, kind of like working out at the gym, we strengthen it working it, or it can atrophy by not using it. Very common pattern that happens with business owners with ADHD is without sort of common structured sort of markers that allow us to see time, time starts to blur. the more time blurs, the weaker our time awareness gets, the weaker our time awareness gets, the more difficult it is to use any sort of time management system because you're not.

**Для Напарника:** Сессии Фокуса как чёткие вехи в дне (начало-конец, ритуал) вместо одного размытого потока.

## 5. Тренируй осознание времени: предскажи, замерь, удивись без самобичевания
`СДВГ · время · привычки · запуск-задачи` · [09:52](https://youtu.be/d3bjF_8citY?t=592)

**Мысль:** Как подрядчик, учащийся оценивать бетон: прогноз → результат → разница. Цель не уложиться, а с любопытством заметить расхождение; навык растёт сам.

**Слова автора:**

> These are all things that we can develop through putting ourselves in situations where we make predictions and then notice what happens. We want to force feed. So this is how we develop time awareness is we want to force feed giving our brain the evidence and the patterns where it can draw the connection. In the same way that if you wanted to get good at anything, if you were a contractor and you wanted to get really good at estimating how much concrete was needed in a particular space, how would you get good at doing that? Well, you would look at a space. You would make your best guess. You would see what actually happened. You would observe the difference. And then over time, your brain would start to see those patterns where you could just sort of eyeball it, right? Maybe you're not good at eyeballing how much concrete is needed in environment right now, but if you needed to, you could figure out that skill. And so, here's what I would like you to do next is over the next week, I want you to work on developing your time awareness. And what this looks like is every time you're about to start a task, I want you to make a prediction of how long this task is likely to take. Set a time or when you start. At the end of the task, I want you to see how long did it actually take. You said it was going to be a 15-minute task to send this email, and you notice actually it was 45 minutes. Here's the point of doing it. The point of doing it, just to be clear, point of doing this isn't to stay within your time. It's not saying, well, I've just given myself 15 minutes to make this email, so I'm going to force myself to do in 15 minutes. That's not what I'm suggesting. What I want you to notice is almost like with a level of curiosity of, isn't that interesting? I keep thinking that this is a 15inute task, but I've learned that this is a 45minute task. It's going through not sort of like beating yourself up like, "Oh my gosh, why am I doing this again? It keeps taking longer and longer." I thought it's just more interesting over time. When you do this, you sort of like make the prediction, call the shot, observe the difference. Your ability to sort of accurately estimate how long things will actually take is going to get better.

**Для Напарника:** После шага: «думал 15 минут, ушло 45 — интересно». Калибровка времени подаётся как любопытство, без стыда.
