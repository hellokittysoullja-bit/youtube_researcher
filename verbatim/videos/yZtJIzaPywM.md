## 1. Слепота к изменениям — не зрение, а ёмкость внимания
`внимание · нейро · исследования` · [00:48](https://youtu.be/yZtJIzaPywM?t=48)

**Мысль:** Горилла и мигающий ящик остаются незамеченными, пока внимание занято другим, но видны сразу после подсказки: ограничена выборка, не восприятие.

**Слова автора:**

> A good example of attention comes from change blindness so here we have two still images that are alternating the image of the boat at the back has a box that is disappearing and reappearing but you don't notice it at first perhaps until I cue you to that but the fact that when I cue you can see it shows that this is not a problem with visual perception your visual system can see it once it's attended to it the problem was focusing and selecting that relevant information in the first place from a clustered scene count how many times the players wearing white pass the basketball another example of attention operating like this is the famous gorilla experiment where people are counting basketball passes from one group of players to another if you're attention is diverted to accounting basketball passes then you don't notice a gorilla who enters the scene bangs his chest and walks off so about 50% of people do notice the gorilla and 50% of people don't but when their attention is oriented towards the grill if you tell people not to count the basketball players 100% of people will notice this so again this is a phenomenon related to attention rather than perception so although we call these things change blindness in intentional blindness they're not really blindness this is not a problem with our visual system it's rather a limited capacity of our attention system that is selecting one information above another it's focusing on the basketball players to such extent that you're almost blind you can't notice or report the presence of the gorilla.

**Для Напарника:** В сессии Фокуса не добавлять второстепенные элементы: сфокусированный человек их не увидит, а редкое важное событие нужно подавать явно.

## 2. Прожектор внимания: вспышка в углу его перетягивает
`внимание · анимация · нейро` · [02:43](https://youtu.be/yZtJIzaPywM?t=163)

**Мысль:** Внимание можно направлять стратегически, но внезапное изменение на периферии притягивает его само.

**Слова автора:**

> We can think of attention in terms of a spotlight metaphor that we're highlighting certain parts of a scene at the expense of other others and we can move this Spotlight around either strategically we can choose to move it or maybe it moves of its own free will if something flashes in the corner then the spotlight gets attracted to it.

**Для Напарника:** Любое движение на Фокусе тянет взгляд: фон должен быть медленным, а резкое — только на событие.

## 3. Теменная кора — карта значимости, настроенная задачей
`внимание · нейро · цвет` · [05:29](https://youtu.be/yZtJIzaPywM?t=329)

**Мысль:** Нейроны реагируют на неожиданное и на релевантное задаче (красное при поиске красного), а давно присутствующее игнорируют.

**Слова автора:**

> It does responds to particular aspects of the scene that might be important so for example if a neuron is not expecting anything on the left side but all of a sudden there's a flash on the left side then this would activate neurons in the parial L whereas an expected stimulus might not do that or a stimulus that is irrelevant and has been present for a long time similar if you're searching for red objects then here your task is telling you to look for red what we find is that neurons in the parietal lobe would respond more to Red stimuli than to Blue stimuli but again if the task changes to be searching for blue then all of a sudden the neurons would respond more to Blue so again it's as if the neurons within certain parts of the parietal lobe are acting as a filter they're not just responding to color they're responding to the relevant colors for a given task and maybe it's the frontal Lo that is telling the PRI Lo which features are relevant for a particular task so if you're searching for tomatoes in a crowded Market you're going to be looking for red we can say that the pariet lobe has something along the lines of a salience map that is it contains maps of the sensory world that highlights the most important or Salient aspects of it.

**Для Напарника:** Привыкание к повторяющемуся убивает заметность; редкое значимое событие (находка) лучше всего заметно на спокойном фоне.
