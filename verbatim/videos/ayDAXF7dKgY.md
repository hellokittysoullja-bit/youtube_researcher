## 1. Открыл не из желания, а потому что потеря хуже продолжения
`стрики · мотивация · удержание` · [00:22](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=22)

**Мысль:** Усталый человек в 23:59 заходит ради сохранения числа: инженерия возвращения, а не желания.

**Слова автора:**

> You did not open the app because your desire to learn suddenly returned. You opened it because losing felt worse than continuing. That tiny decision, repeated across an audience of millions, is part of the mechanism behind one of the world's largest education businesses. Duolingo did not invent the desire to learn a language. It engineered a system that makes people come back tomorrow.

**Для Напарника:** Напарник строит возвращение через тепло и присутствие, а не через страх потери.

## 2. Серия превращается из счётчика во владение
`стрики · геймификация · мотивация` · [01:45](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=105)

**Мысль:** Чем длиннее серия, тем меньше она счётчик и тем больше «построенное»; мотив сдвигается от получения дня к избеганию потери; пламя, вехи и анимации усиливают.

**Слова автора:**

> A one-day streak means almost nothing. Seven days feels like momentum. Thirty days feels like proof. 365 days can feel like part of your identity. The longer the streak grows, the less it resembles a counter. It becomes something you have built. And once people feel ownership over progress, the motivation changes. At first, you complete a lesson to gain another day. Later, you complete it to avoid losing hundreds of days behind you. This is loss aversion: the tendency to experience a loss more strongly than an equivalent gain. Duolingo has explicitly described designing parts of the streak experience around this behavior. The flame becomes more prominent. Milestones are celebrated. Animations make the streak feel alive. A missed day threatens the visible history of your consistency.

**Для Напарника:** Остров как «построенное» без угрозы потери: накопление остаётся, пропуск не разрушает.

## 3. Стрик работает внутри системы; заморозка спасает привычку
`геймификация · привычки · СДВГ` · [03:05](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=185)

**Мысль:** Короткие уроки по низкой мотивации, очки, квесты, лидерборды, напоминания; заморозка нужна, чтобы одна ошибка не стерла двухлетнюю серию и не вызвала уход.

**Слова автора:**

> But the streak would not be powerful on its own. It works because Duolingo surrounds it with a behavioral system. Lessons are short enough to finish when motivation is low. Experience points turn practice into a score. Daily quests create immediate targets. Leaderboards convert private progress into competition. Notifications arrive when the streak is at risk. And Streak Freeze protects users from the moment that could destroy the habit completely. If one mistake erased a two-year streak, the frustration could make someone abandon the app. A freeze preserves the emotional investment and gives the user a reason to return.

**Для Напарника:** Короткие сессии для низкой энергии (5-10 мин) и мягкая «заморозка» для пропуска.

## 4. Эксперименты: Streak Wager +14% к D7, анимации +1,7%
`метрики · анимация · исследования` · [03:41](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=221)

**Мысль:** Небольшие улучшения (усиленные анимации серии, wager) складываются в тысячи людей, которые остаются.

**Слова автора:**

> Duolingo tests these mechanics instead of relying only on intuition. In one company-run experiment, users offered a Streak Wager returned more frequently and completed more lessons than users who were not offered it. Duolingo reported statistically significant increases in day-one, day-seven, and day-fourteen retention, with the largest improvement, a 14% increase, appearing in day-seven retention. In another internal experiment, stronger streak animations increased the likelihood that a new learner was still Duolingo seven days later by 1.7%. Those percentages may appear small. But across a platform operating at Duolingo's scale, small improvements can compound into thousands of additional people continuing to use the product.

**Для Напарника:** Тестировать микроанимации награды (reveal-island) на удержание D7, а не полагаться на интуицию.

## 5. Friend Streaks: ответственность перед другом, +22% (корреляция)
`body-doubling · геймификация · удержание` · [04:27](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=267)

**Мысль:** Когда к серии привязан друг, пропуск ощущается как подведение другого; данные показывают ассоциацию, не доказательство причины.

**Слова автора:**

> Then Duolingo made the streak social. Leaderboards gave users people to compete Friend Streaks added shared responsibility. When only your streak is at risk, skipping affects you. When a friend is attached to it, skipping can feel like letting someone else down. According to Duolingo's internal data, learners with at least one Friend Streak are 22% more likely to complete their daily lesson. That figure shows an association within Duolingo's data. It does not, by itself, prove that the feature caused every additional lesson. But it shows the direction of the system. Personal progress. Fear of loss. Competition. Friendship. Reminders. And relief when the streak survives. All of them create reasons for tomorrow's session.

**Для Напарника:** Идея компаньона/body-doubling: присутствие другого повышает возвращаемость; осторожно: давление чувством вины не применимо.

## 6. Агрессивная монетизация разрушает привычку; Duolingo отказывается от $50M
`этика · цены · удержание` · [07:22](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=442)

**Мысль:** Слишком много рекламы и допродаж создаёт трение, которое вредит привычке, на которой держится монетизация; компания пожертвовала выручкой ради бесплатного опыта.

**Слова автора:**

> But this creates a difficult balance. The free product must be useful enough that people keep returning, while advertising and premium benefits must persuade some users to pay. Push monetization too softly, and revenue suffers. Push it too aggressively, and friction can damage the habit that makes monetization possible. Duolingo acknowledged this tension after increasing ads, subscription upsells, and other friction. For 2026, the company estimated that it would give up more than $50 million in potential bookings from these tactics to invest in the free experience and long-term growth. The most valuable asset is not a single transaction. It is the user who returns every day.

**Для Напарника:** Не ставить монетизацию между человеком и ядром Напарника: долгосрочная привычка ценнее.

## 7. Геймификация смещает цель: очки и серия вместо обучения
`этика · мотивация · тёмные-паттерны` · [08:12](https://youtu.be/ayDAXF7dKgY?t=492)

**Мысль:** Активность не равна результату; люди повторяют лёгкие уроки ради очков и защищают серию минимальным упражнением. Система производит две цели: учиться и сохранять число.

**Слова автора:**

> There is another tension. A system can increase activity without guaranteeing that every activity produces meaningful learning. A peer-reviewed study assessed 225 adults who had completed the beginning content of Duolingo's Spanish or French courses and reported using Duolingo as their only learning tool. Their reading and listening results were comparable to those of university students after four semesters of language study. But the research did not assess speaking or writing, did not cover every course, and did not demonstrate complete fluency. Gamification can also change what users optimize for. Some may repeat easy lessons for points. Some may care more about winning a league than understanding a sentence. Some may protect a streak with the shortest possible exercise and then close the app. A qualitative study analyzed 357 forum posts, more than 30,000 comments, and interviews with 15 users. It documented cases in which people became overly focused on points, streaks, badges, and competition. The study could describe that behavior, but not establish how common it is across Duolingo's entire audience. That does not mean gamification is a trick. And it does not mean Duolingo does not teach. It means the system can produce two goals: learning consistently, and keeping the number alive. Is the streak still serving your learning... or has your learning started serving the streak?

**Для Напарника:** Награды Напарника привязывать к реальному действию по делу, а не к пустым заходам; метрика результата, а не активность.
